## Rank (join first, then FOMO)

### 1. Grocery **circular** (weekly advertised SKU)

**What the masses stare at:** the sticker. Milk, eggs, chicken, cereal. Daily. Forever.

**Public tape, not a priest:** the **weekly ad** is *meant* to be posted. Chain + week + SKU + advertised price. Homogeneous goods. Two chains in one ZIP can be compared without a Nielsen license.

**Join:** π̄ = advertised (or shelf if you later add a receipt, still public to the holder). πN ≈ rival circular on the same UPC/week/market. πM is the hard part (private label / one-chain dominance) — still defined, unlike a house.

**FOMO:** 8. Functional: 7 if you parse ads you are allowed to fetch. Architecture: **9** — this is posted-price.

**Fail-sale:** SKU–week–market feed. Not a meal app. Not Instacart.

**Empty vs staffing:** Circana / Nielsen own **scanner** (what sold). BLS CPI aggregates. USDA F-MAP is lagged categories, not stickers. Nobody owns “anyone recomputes this week’s advertised Δ on eggs in this ZIP.” That is the atomic hole. It is boring. That is the point.

**Do not:** buy IRI. Do not health-SKU the basket. Do not found a grocer.

### 2. **Posted wage range** (where the ruling already forced the sticker)

**What the masses stare at:** the offer. Not BLS. The number on the posting.

**Public tape:** Colorado EPEWA Part 2 (C.R.S. 8-5-201; 7 CCR 1103-18) — postings must carry a **good-faith min and max**, not “$30k and up.” NY/CA/WA have cousins. BLS OEWS (May 2025 tables, public) is the lagged occupation mean — a candidate πN, **not** the offer.

**Join:** π̄ = posted range midpoint (or the floor — pick one rule and freeze it). Same SOC + metro. Anyone with the posting HTML and OEWS xlsx gets the same Δ.

**FOMO:** 8 for people changing jobs. Architecture: **8** where the law already printed the range. Functional: 6 — career pages are public; Indeed is a priest; do not scrape Indeed.

**Fail-sale:** a tape of **required** postings vs OEWS, city by city. Clerk energy. Complements Colorado assessment (employment is a 6-1-1703 consequential class) without being the assessment SKU.

**Empty vs staffing:** Levels.fyi / Blind are priest + people-hunt. OEWS is the index. The **offer sticker** is the missing join, and Colorado already made it illegal to hide.

**Do not:** a “salary negotiation chatbot.”

### 3. Rent **asking** — FOMO 10, tape fail

**What they stare at:** rent. The number that ate the wage.

**What is public:** HUD FY2026 FMR / SAFMR (ZIP in designated metros). That is a **voucher benchmark**, including utilities, **not** today’s asking rent ([huduser.gov FMR](https://www.huduser.gov/Portal/datasets/fmr.html)).

**What is priest:** Zillow / Apartments.com asking. RealPage AIRM / YieldStar was the **correlating device** on the landlord side. DOJ + states (incl. Colorado) v. RealPage: proposed final judgment; court entered the stipulation **26 Mar 2026**; Tunney comments answered **8 May 2026**. Runtime use of competitors’ nonpublic data restricted. Do not clone YieldStar. Do not be the next AIRM.

**Join:** blocked unless a **city already publishes** asking listings (license/registry). Scraping portals is not a rail.

**Verdict:** **kill as a product.** Keep as the **subject** you feel. The architecture you want (public asking tape → same Δ) is exactly what the platforms refuse to be. Building it from HUD alone prints last year’s fair market, not this week’s posting.

### 4. House **price** — FOMO 9, unique goods

**Public:** FHFA HPI — repeat-sales on GSE mortgages, national → tract, transparent method, 2026 release calendar posted. That is an **index of transacted conforming loans**, not the asking sticker on Zillow.

**Priest:** ZHVI / Zestimate (neural, proprietary).

**Join:** a house is not a UPC. Δ across unique goods is a story. Redfin already sells asking-vs-sold.

**Verdict:** **kill as Δ.** Use FHFA if you ever need a public housing *index*. Do not found a Zillow.

### 5. Fare — NYC already printed the realized tape

**What they stare at:** surge. Uber quote in the app.

**Public:** NYC TLC trip records, monthly parquet, ~two-month lag. Yellow itemized fares. High-volume FHV (HV0003 Uber, HV0005 Lyft) with zone IDs and fare fields ([TLC trip record data](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page)). NY VAT § 1692: TNC must show actual or estimated fare before the passenger enters, and post the calculation method on the website — **to that passenger**, not as a citywide quote tape.

**Join:** you can recompute **realized** NYC zone-pair Δ on a lag. You cannot recompute **this minute’s posted quote** without the app priest. “Kayak for Uber” is staffed and ToS-fragile.

**Verdict:** **do not rebuild TLC.** Optional later: one paper on lagged HVFHV Δ (study, not a SKU). National fare: dead.
