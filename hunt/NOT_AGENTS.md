# Future bets that are not agents

The laptop product (the check) is agents. Chat, errand-bots, coding-bots, “it does the tabs” — that’s one aisle, already a gold rush. These four are not that aisle. They are **where the computer lives next**, in a body, on a face, in a power plant, or in a model of touching.

---

**1. A machine in the house that has hands.**  
Not a chatbot that books a plumber. A body that folds the laundry. Today a human is usually driving it through cameras. The bet is when it doesn’t need the driver. Maybe around 2030. You don’t start this from a laptop.

**2. A computer on your face.**  
Glasses you wear. Later, labels and arrows sitting on the real world. Meta is already selling the awkward first ones. Overlay maybe ~2027. Not an agent. A new screen that isn’t a screen.

**3. Electricity.**  
The big models and the robots eat power. The scarce thing is plants, wires, and who gets to plug in. Not a smarter prompt. Not an agent. Land.

**4. Knowing what happens when you touch something.**  
ChatGPT knows words. The next stack is a model of cups, floors, hands. That’s how a robot stops being a puppet. Not a staff that emails people. Physics.

---

Same move, four shapes: the computer leaves the phone. Agents were the *software* chapter of that move (do, don’t talk). These are the *atoms* chapters.

From a laptop you can visit 1 and 2 (film the puppet house, sit the first glasses week). You cannot own them. 3 is out with no money. 4 is a lab. The check is the only empty *tool*, and it is agents. That does not mean the future is only agents.
