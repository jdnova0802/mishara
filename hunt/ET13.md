## The actual machine (three rails, not one)

**Rail A — Postal informal (IMDW email).** Live since 24 Jul 2026. Monthly Excel/CSV to CBPDM@cbp.dhs.gov named `[FilerCode]_[MM]_[YYYY]_Payment`, Pay.gov ACH by the **7th of the following month**. 10-digit HTSUS, S-10 tracking, bond number. Eligible: ≤ $2,500, international mail, **Chapters 1–97 only**, otherwise informal-eligible under 19 CFR 143.21.

**Rail B — Entry Type 13 in ACE.** Voluntary test, **no application**, PROD **22 Sep 2026**. Same 143.26(a) filer class. Required: filer code, IOR, S-10 / foreign postal operator tracking (SE15), shipper name+address (SE30/SE35) or ACE rejects, Manifest Component Issuer = **Secure Filing Postal (SFP)** code. This is the electronic path for the goods Rail A is about to spit out.

**Rail C — Formal entry.** Always required for > $2,500, **quota, AD/CVD**. Alcohol/tobacco also off the postal informal path.

**Cliff — 22 Oct 2026 (32 days from this hunt).** Enforcement of 19 CFR 145.12(a)(2)(v)–(vi): merchandise with **PGA requirements**, **Chapter 98 or 99** duties or Ch.98 duty-free claims, or **FTA duty-free claims** is **ineligible** for Rail A. CBP FAQ: those goods must use **ET13 or formal**. Until 22 Oct they may still ride Rail A if extra data is stuffed into the worksheet.

CBP’s own words on failure: shipment **will not be released from CBP custody** until entry is filed and the bond is on file. That is the will-not. It is already in the FAQ.

## Why the volume is life-changing and the bottleneck is real

FY2024: 1.36B de minimis shipments. Almost 10× 2015 (139M). ~90% of US inbound shipment count was small-package. Postal alone was 74.8M de minimis BOLs in FY2024 — and that was the **shrinking** postal share after Type 86 ate the rest.

The IFR’s paperwork estimate is the tell: CBP models **100 filers** submitting the monthly IMDW. One hundred parties, against tens of millions of postal parcels. Brokers file one IMDW covering all clients that month. The constraint is not “someone should write a blog.” The constraint is **who has 143.26(a) standing + ACE filer code + activity-1 eBond + 10-digit HTS on every line**.

Foreign postal operators and unlicensed “qualified third parties” who ran the EO 14324/14388 interim spreadsheet **cannot file**. Consignee who is not owner/purchaser (USPS, forwarder, carrier) **must** put a licensed broker on as IOR. That is in the ET13 FR notice, not a LinkedIn take.
