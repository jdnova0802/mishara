## 1. The humanoid is a teleop costume

**Suspicion:** they sell the body before the brain is done; the product is a scheduled ghost in your house.

**Hit:** 1X Expert Mode — you book a window, a human drives NEO through cameras; WSJ hands-on: *every* household task was teleoperated. Data-collection operators hired on-site San Carlos, VR, $28–36/hr (Aug 2026). Teleop software jobs to $250k. Thesis: paying homes are the training set.

**Size:** if 10k robots ship, that’s a new labor class looking through other people’s kitchens.

**Taken?** 1X owns the fleet and the experts. A random “Uber for teleop” is a fool unless you’re the OEM. The *idea* is massive. The *company* is already named.

## 2. World models = ImageNet for robots

**Suspicion:** language is played; the unplayed model is “how the room behaves when you touch it.”

**Hit:** World Labs **Atlas** (1 Sep 2026) — omni world model, text/image/video/3D. **SceniX** joined them 21 Jul 2026: real-to-sim-to-real, policies trained in sim with **zero** real-world training data (their claim). Fei-Fei: renderer vs simulator vs planner still not one model.

**Size:** this is the primitive under every home robot that isn’t a puppeteer.

**Taken?** Frontier lab. You don’t “start a world model” from a walk. You watch who licenses the sim.

## 3. Megawatts are the new GPUs

**Suspicion:** glamorous AI is now a *queue for electricity*.

**Hit:** ERCOT ~**474 GW** interconnection requests, ~**90% data centers**; Abbott freeze; energization of new large loads **paused**; eligibility report **10 Dec 2026**. Queue ~5× Texas peak demand. Transformers 18–24 month lead times. WEF: grid hookup 4–10 years vs DC build 2–3. Interruptible “emergency lane” load could unlock 5–15% in congestion (DNV/Netherlands cited).

**Size:** who is allowed to exist as an AI company.

**Taken?** Utilities, hyperscalers, Texas politics. Laptop tool: fool. Behind-the-meter power, batteries that eat curtailment, fake-queue detection: atoms + permits. Glamorous if you like thrones, not feeds.

## 4. Agents need a human principal

**Suspicion:** when everything can click, the missing stamp is “a unique person authorized this.”

**Size:** the internet’s next login.

**Taken?** World + Coinbase + IdP vendors. Orb is the priest. A seventh “proof of human” coin is a fool. The *job* (agent isn’t a ghost bot) is real and contested.

## 5. Coding agents won; review is the fire

**Suspicion:** errand-agents flopped; the primitive that actually shipped is **async workers that open PRs**.

**Hit:** Copilot coding agent, Cursor cloud VMs, Codex, Jules, Devin, Claude Managed Agents (Apr 2026). Background/subagents default. Writeups in 2026: throughput up, **review time and change-failure up** — the bottleneck moved to judgment.

**Size:** how software gets made.

**Taken?** The *agent* is taken. The *foreman* (portfolio of agents, what may merge, evidence not vibes) is the crowded-but-unsolved layer. Another chat-that-codes is a fool.

## 6. The wrist is the mouse

**Suspicion:** glasses fail without a socially invisible click.

**Hit:** **Meta Neural Band** (EMG) ships with Ray-Ban Display **$799** (US Sep 2025 → EU/UK/Canada 2026). Subtle finger → scroll/click; trained on ~200k people. Orion still prototype.

**Size:** input for the face-computer.

**Taken?** Meta (CTRL-Labs). Don’t build a wristband in a garage and call it a primitive.
