# Under the slogans: quantum and batteries

People talk **quantum** and **batteries** the way they talk **AI**. The word is the umbrella. The bets are the children. Factories still own most of the children. One child under quantum is actually software on a normal computer.

---

## Quantum (the word)

**1. The quantum computer.**  
The sci-fi box. Error correction, “quantum advantage,” rent-a-qubit in the cloud. This is what the word means in a bar. It is not a product you ship from a laptop. Still a lab.

**2. Sensing / finding yourself without GPS.**  
A quieter child, closer to fielded. Gravity and magnetism as a map. Q-CTRL and defense money. Real. Not a website.

**3. Magic phones (QKD).**  
New physics on a dedicated fiber to notice eavesdroppers. Hardware plant. People mix this up with (4). It is not (4).

**4. New locks on the computers you already own.**  
**Post-quantum cryptography.** Same laptops. New math (NIST 2024: ML-KEM / ML-DSA). Google, Apple, Signal already putting it on billions of people. Harvest-now-decrypt-later: steal the ciphertext today, open it when the box in (1) exists. NIST wants the old locks gone by **2035**.

**(4) is to quantum what agents were to AI.** The slogan is a machine nobody has. The thing that is actually moving is swapping the locks. No qubit required.

---

## Batteries (the word)

**1. Solid-state / “better lithium.”**  
Bar talk. Still mostly a factory race, not in your driveway as the default.

**2. Sodium packs for the grid.**  
The child that is actually shipping in 2026: CATL TENER sodium storage, first deliveries this window, GWh-scale talk, 1–8 hour dump. Salt is cheap. This is the “agents” of batteries — not a phone battery, a shipping container that holds the afternoon.

**3. Duration.**  
Not a flashier cell. How many hours the pack can push. Eight hours vs one is a different object. Plants and integrators.

**4. The brain of the pack.**  
When it may charge, how full it really is, whether it can sell power back. Software next to a cell. The cell maker still owns it.

There is **no** clean laptop child under batteries the way (4) sits under quantum. Sodium and duration are CATL/Hithium. A phone app that says “charge at 2am” is not the bet.

---

**Normie cut:** under quantum, the future bet you can actually touch is **the new locks**. Under batteries, the future bet is **sodium on the grid**, and you cannot touch it without a factory. Don’t confuse talking about the umbrella with sitting in the child.
