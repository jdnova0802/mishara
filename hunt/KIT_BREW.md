# Kit brew (ancient / any era) — filmable will-not-go

Company two is **off** unless the object can be filmed refusing to move. Nisaba stays the boring house. Not Palantir glass. Not a jet you don’t own. Not a people-map.

Same lock: two stations, time window, yes in the gear, then **Go**.

---

**Harbor chain.** Constantinople, old navies: an iron chain across the mouth. Ships do not leave and do not enter until it drops. City + military. Extremely filmable. Imposing. Not a dashboard.

**Portcullis.** Teeth of iron. The mouth of a castle. Will not raise. Medieval edit factory. Same clutch. City wall / base gate.

**Beacon.** A pyre that will not light until the yes. Then the next hill answers. Culture already knows this picture. Product is the **cold** beacon, not LOTR fan-cam. Watch the clone-risk.

**Galley / trireme.** Oars packed. Drum is the yes (cousin of the horn). Then they pull and the hull leaves the slip. Ancient navy. Loud. Physical.

**Turnpike.** A literal bar / pike across the king’s road. The word is already “you don’t go.” Less pretty than a chain. Very honest.

**Imperial chair / palanquin.** The ruling body does not move until two seals. Face lives next to the chair. Easy to get kitsch. Strong myth, weaker machine.

**Skip for now:** war elephant (cruel/cringe), jet you don’t have, observatory shutter (weak), people-scanner.
