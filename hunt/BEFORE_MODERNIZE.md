# Before you modernize

Don’t modernize *all* of it. That’s a museum gift shop. Mood board stays godly. Product stays **one mouth**: it will not go until a real yes in the gear.

**Keep as pictures, not SKUs:** Colossus, Ishtar, Karnak, Petra, Persepolis. Wow. You don’t sell a bronze giant.

**Modernize these three bodies, same lock:**  
- Water → harbor boom  
- Earth → wedge / blast gate (portcullis)  
- Air → pad / hangar (galley)  

**Myth layer:** Hero’s doors = the hangar/gate only answers a *true* yes, not a mashed button. Horn = the yes made audible. Time window = clepsydra. Seal = cylinder roll (digital).

**Still true:** two stations, not one phone. Stranger can check. Draft does nothing. Unplug you and it still goes → paint. Separate company, not Nisaba. No people-map. No coded-threat scanner. Factory later = 5, not 8. Face is of the object. Film the pause, then the open. Don’t steal Palantir glass.

If you modernize everything on the mood board, you get a catalog and the TikTok dies.
