## Rank (extreme → still real)

### 1. Colorado high-risk AI **impact assessment** — **30 Jun 2026**

**Ruling.** SB24-205 (C.R.S. 6-1-1703). SB25B-004 moved the date from 1 Feb 2026 to **30 Jun 2026**.

**Named object.** Deployer of a high-risk system (consequential decision: education, employment, lending, government service, health, housing, insurance, legal) must complete an **impact assessment**: on deploy, **annually**, and within **90 days** of an intentional substantial modification. **A third party contracted by the deployer may do it.** Keep the file **three years** after final deployment. AG may demand it in 90 days. Rebuttable presumption of reasonable care if they complied.

**Quark.** One dated packet: purpose / intended uses / context / benefits; known or reasonably foreseeable algorithmic-discrimination risks and mitigations; input categories and outputs; customization data; metrics used to evaluate performance; transparency measures; post-deployment monitoring. After a substantial modification: a statement of how actual use matched or varied from the developer’s intended uses. One assessment may cover a comparable set of systems. A reasonably similar assessment done for another law counts.

**Empty vs staffing.** NYC LL144 already has BABL / ORCAA / Holistic. Colorado’s date **just slipped**. Same vendors will expand. Mouth is **calendar-empty for ~3.5 months** — CO-shaped packets, not a 10-year monopoly.

**Money.** B2B. Clerk. Complements remaining: remaining is halt the write; this is **the paper that says they used care**. Do not sell halt. Do not DEPOSIT to banks. Sell to Colorado deployers (insurers, lenders, HR, housing) who need the file by **30 Jun**.

**Code.** `assessment/` — fail-closed generator that emits markdown + JSON with 6-1-1703(3)(b)(I)–(VII), (3)(g), and the (3)(c) rider on modification. Blanks do not print a packet. Not a fuse. Not a bias-science startup. Brand stays Velaru assessment.

### 2. EU Cyber Resilience Act **Article 14** — **live since 11 Sep 2026**

**Ruling.** Regulation (EU) 2024/2847. Reporting of **actively exploited vulnerabilities** and **severe incidents** affecting products with digital elements. ENISA **Single Reporting Platform** launched the same day. Full product-security / SBOM / CE duties: **11 Dec 2027**.

**Named object.** Early warning **24h**, notification **72h**, final report **14 days** after a fix (vuln) or **one month** (incident). One submit through SRP → coordinating CSIRT + ENISA.

**Quark.** The SRP notification, backed by a machine-readable SBOM (CycloneDX/SPDX, at least top-level deps) so they know which SKU is hit.

**Empty vs staffing.** AppSec vendors (Snyk, Chainguard, Anchore) already sell SBOMs. ENISA built the **platform**. The remaining mouth is **small EU-selling manufacturers** who have a product on the market and no 24h clerk. Not empty forever. Extreme as law. Coding-heavy. Compete with every CVE product.

**Do not:** reinvent ENISA. If picked: a fill-the-SRP pack from SBOM+CVE for shops that ship software/hardware into the EU. Dual-use later hope sits here (vuln handling), still not exploit work.
