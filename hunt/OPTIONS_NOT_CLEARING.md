# Options that are not clearing

Clearing / “be the pipes” / payout vendor is **off** until you put it back. Insurance stays the old house. Platforms-as-a-feature stays off. Magazine sci-fi (bodies, glass, plants) is not this menu.

Engineer path: you own the **mechanism**. Someone else can be the face. Kid warmup then strict.

---

**K. Stay the kernel (Gate).**  
Nothing irreversible without a live yes. You already are this. Ruling: whoever must not act without may. Mass: they never see the kernel; they feel “it didn’t just happen.” Not a new planet. Colder on what’s already real.

**F. File-gate (courts) — S3.**  
Draft ≠ filed. Checkers crowded. The **file** mouth is open. Ruling: courts / e-file. Mass: weak (most people aren’t in a case). Best institutional wedge you already found. Different shop. Not a pipe.

**A. Act-gate for agents — S1 leftover.**  
Thought ≠ the act. Pay-fences crowded. Open-ish: non-pay acts + stranger receipt. Ruling: anyone whose agents can break the world. Mass: their agent doesn’t spend/send/delete without a yes. Not being the wire.

**Y. The yes as the product (not the pipes).**  
They call your live yes every time. You are not the money moving. You are the stamp. Ruling and mass can both hit a stamp. This is Gate-shaped. Easy to confuse with K.

**R. The receipt.**  
A stranger can check that it happened (or didn’t). Ruling: audit. Mass: “did my send/file/act land?” Muscle of information theory + crypto. Easy to become a dashboard. Failure if it’s only a PDF.

**P. The packet / folder.**  
The record that has to travel to the next office. Ruling: agencies. Mass: license, title, the letter. Ugly. Sit-able. Not sci-fi looking.

**X. Exit.**  
They leave, the hours/photos/save file come with them. Ruling: portability law. Mass: they actually get their stuff. Format war.

**M. Mandate (afterlife / speak-as) — S4.**  
You don’t get to speak as someone without a live mandate. Ruling: replica laws. Mass: family. Grief UX already died as a product. Mandate object still missing. Ugly.

**C. The check (agent work).**  
No pack, no yes on a patch. Mass: anyone drowning in agent code. Ruling: whoever ships software. Already a tool in `check/`. Not nations. Not sci-fi looking.

**T. The slot.**  
Friday 8am with the human who can stamp. Mass: appointments. Ruling: the gatekeeper’s calendar. Not a kernel.

---

**Not on this menu:** being the wire; insurance; a new coin; liveness (S2, don’t fight that vendor); body-archive SKU (S5, deferred); PQC “new locks” as a company (hygiene).

Cross off what repels. Pick a letter. Study 1–6 only stays if the object still needs settlement atoms. If you pick F/A/Y/K/R, the book list may shrink to kernel + crypto + systems, not PFMI-as-spine.
