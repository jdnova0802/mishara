## 2. NORS/DIRS state→county relay — **real FCC leftover, not our object**

**What is real.** Direct NORS/DIRS access (since 30 Sep 2022) is for certified **Participating Agencies** (state/federal/Tribal/territorial EM). Counties are **Downstream Agencies**: they request from the state; they do not get the database. FCC 2025 Resilient Networks FNPRM: only **22** agencies sought/got access; FCC’s own words, information is **underutilized during emergencies**; they are asking whether to loosen sharing toward local public safety. Maui 2023 (DIRS was activated) and the carrier-duty litigation are real.

**What is not the leftover.** The missing piece is **access rules + confidentiality**, which FCC is already rewriting. An automated push with a delivery receipt requires sitting inside that certified regime (or becoming Everbridge-class emergency comms). A Velaru receipt that “the push happened” does not grant NORS access and does not stop a wildfire. Do not build a people-adjacent outage feed. Do not found a 911 overlay.
