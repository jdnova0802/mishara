# The bets, in English

Not “an empty app.” Not grocery. Not a chatbot. Four ideas that are the same move: **the computer leaves the phone/laptop rectangle.**

---

**1. A computer with hands, in the room.**  
Today’s house robots are mostly puppets — a person remote-driving them. The bet is the one that **does the chore without a driver**. That’s the robotics “ChatGPT moment,” maybe around 2030 if it happens. 1X/Figure are the ugly first Palms. The fortune is the brain after the puppet.

**2. A computer on your face.**  
Glasses with a camera are already for sale (Meta). The bet is when the **world gets labels in the air** — true overlay, maybe ~2027. First money is whoever becomes the default on that glass, not another website.

**3. Power, not prompts.**  
If robots and giant models are the next decade, the scarce thing is **electricity and the wires between chips**, not a cleverer chat box. That’s land and plants, not a landing page.

**4. A model of touching, not talking.**  
ChatGPT knows words. The next stack is a model that knows **what happens when a hand hits a cup**. Whoever owns “contact” owns the next CUDA.

---

Those four can all be true. Occupancy already has names on the thrones (Meta, 1X, NVIDIA, World Labs). The Day-1 bet is still **after** chat, **after** teleop, **after** the first ugly glasses.

**What this is not:** a $0 company tonight. Zero-capital work is living in those primitives (film the puppet house, first week of a glasses API, check that an agent actually did the thing). Weekend auction sites are a different sport.
