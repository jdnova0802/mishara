# Past / present / future — how the actual bets were made

Occupancy-of-this-year is how you **avoid fools**. It is not how Gates, Bezos, or Huang got paid. They bet **where the computer would live next**, while the present still looked taken.

Plain list of the four bets: `hunt/THE_BETS.md`.  
Not the agent aisle: `hunt/NOT_AGENTS.md`.

---

## Past (the pattern, not the myth)

**Gates (’70s):** IBM *was* the computer industry. He and Allen wrote down: a personal computer on every desk and in every home. Then they squeezed BASIC into 4K and evangelized companies into making the boxes. The fortune was **software as the layer** when hardware was the throne. People already “had computers.” Wrong computer.

**Bezos (1997 letter):** “This is Day 1 for the Internet.” Books were not an empty category. Bookstores existed. He said larger players would marshal resources; the window was **new customers + slow incumbents**. He also said it required **serious investment** (people 158→614, warehouses 50k→285k sq ft, $125M cash). Day 1 was not a three-hour site. Outbid.lol is not this letter.

**Huang:** CUDA 2006 (GPU as a computer, not a toy). AlexNet 2012 on two GTX 580s. “We are an AI company” ~2013 while AI still looked like a graveyard. Hand-delivered DGX-1 to OpenAI **2016**. ChatGPT **2022**. The payday is **a decade after the bet**, on a platform other people built on.

None of them googled “is there already an app.” They picked a **primitive that wasn’t the current product aisle**.

## Future (the actual billionaire object)

Not “a missing website.” A **place the computer hasn’t finished moving**.

**1. The computer lives in the room and has hands.**  
Huawei *Intelligent World 2035*: they explicitly call a **“ChatGPT moment” in robotics around 2030** — vision-tactile-language-action / world models, 10+ DoF hands, *selected* consumer scenes after industry. Nature’s robotics+AI roadmap: lifelong learning, safety, data of the physical world — still open. 1X is the **1999 Palm** of that: real, puppeted, privacy-horror. The Huang bet is the **brain that doesn’t need the puppeteer**. That’s 5–10 years if it works at all.

**2. The computer lives on the face.**  
Ray-Ban Display + EMG wrist is 1984 Macintosh: shipping, awkward, owned. Orion/Artemis ~2027 is when the overlay might exist. First fortunes on a new surface are **defaults and developers in year one**, not a 2026 wrapper.

**3. Intelligence is limited by watts, not prompts.**  
If the 2030s run on models and robots, the scarce input is **energy and interconnect**, not another chatbot. That’s Bezos warehouses, not Bezos the bookstore homepage.

**4. A model of *contact*, not of *talk*.**  
World models for robot learning are the CUDA of bodies. Papers in 2026 still call the literature fragmented. Fei-Fei/World Labs are the lab. The future bet is: **whoever owns “what happens when you touch it” owns the next stack.**

Those four can all be true at once. They are **one move**: the computer leaves the rectangle.

## What this means for “me and you, $0”

Huang did not CUDA from a weekend. Bezos said the risk **is** capital. Gates was early *and* wrote the runtime for a machine that barely existed.

The honest $0 analog of that pattern:

- **Pick the future primitive** (room-with-hands / face / watts / contact-model).  
- **Live there before it’s a product aisle** — worlds from phone video, agent-that-acts (and the check), first week of a glasses/Quest API, artifacts (the teleop-house film) that only make sense if you believe the body is coming.  
- **Do not** confuse that with “find an empty consumer app in 2026.” That’s the opposite bet.  
- **Do not** confuse Outbid (a weekend spectacle on the old internet) with Huang (a ten-year platform). Both can print money. Only one is the billionaire *pattern* you named.

**Winner of the *future* bet (not a company tonight):** the computer leaves the glass rectangle — **hands in the room, or eyes on the face, backed by a model of physics, starved by watts.** Occupancy says those thrones have names. The fortune is still **after** teleop, **after** chat, **after** the first ugly glasses. That’s the Day 1. This year’s kill list is just proof you’re not in 1999’s bookstore homepage. You’re in 2013 NVIDIA: the product aisle looks taken; the stack hasn’t moved yet.
