# Categories that are not AI and not agents (no capital)

Everyone’s mouth is **AI** and **agents**. The magazine “what’s next” list is quantum, wetware, batteries — that is money. This list is **kinds of life** two people with a laptop can sit in. Not robots. Not power plants. Not the check. Not glasses. Not videos.

We did not use these as frames before.

---

**1. The folder that has to travel.**  
You already have a right (stay, drive, collect, own the house). The next office does not believe you. The category is the packet that moves between silos — visa, address, title, license — not a bot that chats with the DMV. The complaint is 751 days to change an address, paper beating the portal, five offices wanting the same ID.

**2. Leaving with your record.**  
Switch country, bank, landlord, school, platform — the hours / photos / credit / save file stay behind. The category is **exit**: the thing is still yours on the way out. Not an agent. A format you can take.

**3. Keeping the object alive.**  
Fix the phone, the washer, the bike. Europe is writing right-to-repair; shops and manuals are the work. The category is **maintenance**, not a new gadget and not a chatbot. Laptop piece: the record of what’s broken and who can open it.

**4. The appointment.**  
The scarce thing is a Friday 8am slot with a human who can stamp. The category is **time with a gatekeeper**. Not “an agent booked it.” The fight is seeing the window and holding it.

**5. When a life moves.**  
Death, divorce, inheritance, a house that still sits in a dead person’s name. The category is **succession** — transferring a life across paper. Rare. Brutal. Not a daily app. Not AI.

**6. The format everyone else has to speak.**  
Calendar, receipt, contact, proof. The category is **the shared file type**. Ugly. Free to write. How RSS and vCard actually paid: they became the language.

---

These are not empty aisles. Lawyers, TurboTax, iFixit, DMV bots, Facebook groups already nibble them. They are also **not** the two things people talk about. They are paperwork, exit, repair, the slot, succession, and the format.

If the ask is “what else is there besides agents that we can touch with no money” — it is these kinds of life, not another model.
