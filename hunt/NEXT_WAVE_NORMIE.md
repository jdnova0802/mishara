# Next wave, for people who can actually sit it

After AI and agents, the *named* next waves are things a laptop cannot join. So they are not this page.

There is **no second public wave** right now that is both (1) what people mean by a wave and (2) open to a normal person with no plant.

What you can actually participate in:

**1. The leftover of *this* wave.**  
Agents already write. Humans still say yes. The door on that yes (a pack, not a vibe). That is still agents. It is also the only empty *tool* on the current floor. Not a new decade. The last meter.

**2. A new hole the week it opens.**  
Not a decade brand. Days. A new OS / API / format ships and the first people who sit it get paid (Erly-shaped). You cannot invent the hole. You can be at the laptop the week it exists. Next calendar on the table: Connect **23–24 Sep**, only if a real API drops. If it doesn’t, skip.

**3. Life that was never a wave.**  
The folder that has to travel. Exit with your record. The appointment slot. Repair. Succession. The shared file type. People already pay lawyers and TurboTax. You can sit one packet. This is not “the next AI.” It is the work that was always there while everyone talked about models.

If the question is “what do I ride like AI was ridden”: **nothing with a magazine name, from this chair, today.**  
If the question is “what can I actually do”: leftover of agents, or a packet of someone else’s life, or the week a hole opens.
