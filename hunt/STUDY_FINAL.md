## DESK A

### Information theory
Cover & Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*.

### Game theory
Osborne, *An Introduction to Game Theory*, **or** Fudenberg & Tirole, *Game Theory*.

### Oligopoly / tacit collusion (economics)
Tirole, *The Theory of Industrial Organization* (oligopoly and collusion chapters).  
Drill until you can compute Δ = (π̄ − πN) / (πM − πN).

### Focal points
Schelling, *The Strategy of Conflict* (1960).

### Correlated equilibrium
Aumann, “Subjectivity and Correlation in Randomized Strategies,” *J. Math. Econ.* 1974.

### Algorithmic monoculture
Bommasani et al. (2022) — same model class → correlated decisions.

### The prisoner / warden game
Simmons, “The Prisoners’ Problem and the Subliminal Channel” (1984).  
Cachin, “An Information-Theoretic Model for Steganography” (1998).  
Schroeder de Witt et al., arXiv **2210.14889** (theorems only — do not ship an encoder).

### Collusion-free cryptography
Alwen / shelat / Visconti (CRYPTO 2008–09).  
Lepinski, Micali, shelat, STOC — collusion-free protocols.

### Algorithmic pricing collusion (before LLMs)
Calvano, Calzolari, Denicolò, Pastorello, *AER* 2020.  
Harrington 2018 (interpretability vs behavioral tests).

### LLM pricing / auction collusion
Fish, Gonczarowski, Shorrer, arXiv **2404.00806**.  
Riemer et al., arXiv **2608.18078**.  
Cao & Hu, arXiv **2601.01279**.  
Jo, Wilson, Creel, Raghavan, NeurIPS 2025.

### Secret collusion among AI agents
Motwani, Schroeder de Witt, et al., arXiv **2402.07510**.

### Multi-agent security (the field map)
*Open Challenges in Multi-Agent Security*, arXiv **2505.02077**.  
Peigné et al., AAAI 2025, Multi-Agent Security Tax.

### Latent / non-text channels (observation only)
*Beyond the Transcript* / VLA, arXiv **2608.19161**.  
Tool-channel paper, arXiv **2606.28425**.  
A2ABreak, arXiv **2609.10871**.  
A2A spec and MCP spec — read once.
