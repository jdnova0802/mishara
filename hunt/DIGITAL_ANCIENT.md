# Digital, from the old world

Not a website. Not Palantir. Not a coin. The ancients already did **information as physics**.

**Tiger tally (hufu).** Two bronze halves. Palace keeps one. The field keeps one. The army does not march until they join. That is two stations as metal. Digital Go.

**Tally stick.** Split wood, matching notches. Fake yes doesn’t fit.

**Bulla.** Clay envelope around tokens. Draft is sealed. Breaking/reading is the act. Wet clay ≠ the world.

**Symbolon.** A token broken in two. Reunion proves the act, not a face on a map.

**Cylinder seal.** The roll is the signature. Impression a stranger can check.

**Modern:** two rugged halves that must **join**. Then the boom may open. Login screens are CAD. The join is the clutch.

Same lock. Water boom, earth wedge, air pad, **digital join**. Separate company. No filing.
