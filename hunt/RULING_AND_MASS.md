# Ruling class + mass (not personal “my keys”)

Sovereignty here = **the class that rules**. Two jobs: so in *their* wall they cannot pull you out; and a thing **average people actually buy**. Not grandma’s folder unless that is the mass SKU of the same muscle.

---

**Who counts as the ruling class (pick):**

**R1. The state.** Agencies, courts, the stamp that is law.  
**R2. Platforms.** The few companies that already are the default computer.  
**R3. Capital.** Banks, insurers, whoever clears money.  
**R4. Standards priests.** The room that writes the number everyone else has to use.  
**R5. Mix.** Say which two.

**How you are in their wall (pick):**

**E1. The number.** They have to use your figure (a tape, a Δ, a format).  
**E2. The stamp.** Nothing they do lands without that yes.  
**E3. The language.** Their systems speak your file type.  
**E4. The vendor they cannot fire.** Procurement, installed base, switching is a scandal.  
**E5. The citation.** The paper/law they have to point at. You wrote the muscle.

**Mass (pick):**

**M1. Same object, dumb surface.** Rulers see the engine; people see a button.  
**M2. Two products, one muscle.** Ruling SKU and consumer SKU. Different doors.  
**M3. Mass only pays for a feeling.** The embed is invisible. They never know you.

Desk A (Δ, Calvano, Harrington) was **R1 + E1**. Personal lock/file was the wrong menu. It can come back *if* rulers + number is the pick.
