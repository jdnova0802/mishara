# How a normal person makes it now (the rules)

Not a wave list. Rules. Depth is one of them. The rest are what the hunt actually proved.

**1. Be technically deep in one real thing.**  
Not “into AI.” A stack you can sit at 2am without a tutorial. That’s the ticket. Shallow wrappers are dead.

**2. Stop hunting a named next wave.**  
If it has a magazine name, you are a spectator. There is no second AI for a laptop right now. Waiting for one is how you stall.

**3. Money is the last meter, not the generator.**  
The model already writes. The person who owns **yes** — the door, the packet, the stamp — gets paid. A chatbot that says LGTM is not a living.

**4. Show up the week a hole opens.**  
Days, not a decade. New API, new OS slot, new file type. You don’t invent it. You’re at the laptop when it exists. Miss the week, the format is cloned.

**5. Sell a packet someone already buys.**  
Folder, appointment, exit, repair, succession. Ugly. Staffed at the high end. The middle still pays a human. That is not glamorous. It is sit-able.

**6. Hygiene is not a company.**  
Upgrading locks, upgrading SSH, using the new encrypt — do it for yourself. Don’t found on it.

**7. A weekend spectacle is not a ten-year stack.**  
A one-page stunt can print. It clones by morning. Don’t confuse that with “I made it.”

**8. No buyer, no product.**  
Hunger is not a SKU. A tool you and a model use on your own patches is hygiene until someone will refuse to move without it.

**9. Don’t dress a clone as a primitive.**  
If the aisle has a name on the box, you’re late. Occupancy is a kill, not a dare.

**10. Cash first is still allowed.**  
A boring paid job (one niche, one workflow, they already pay) is how people with no plant actually eat. It is not a wave. It counts.

---

Depth gets you in the room. Rules 2–9 stop you wasting the depth on a wave you can’t sit. Rule 10 is the floor if the room is empty.
