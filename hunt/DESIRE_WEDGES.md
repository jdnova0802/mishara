# Desire wedges — atomically small (16 Sep 2026)

**Status:** brainstorm hunt. Not a company. Not 9am mail. Not a chatbot.  
**Parent:** grocery **S** still wins the industry cut (`hunt/INDUSTRY_DEEP.md`). This file asks a different question: **what do people want**, then where is a gap small enough to be one object.

**Kill the same way:** priest tape, unique goods, already staffed, health SKU, OEM, people-hunt, selling may, inventing a law.

---

## The want under the industries

People do not want “a grocery company.” They want:

1. **The number they showed me is the number that counts.**
2. **I’m not the only fool who was shown that number.**
3. **I can prove it later.**
4. **I can feed us / keep the roof / get paid this month.**
5. **I don’t lose the slot I already spent time or money on.**
6. **The total is visible before I say yes.**

Rent, tickets, surge, job ads, circulars are costumes on (1)–(6).

Atomic gap = one named file that serves one of those wants, that two strangers can recompute, that is not already Flipp / Zillow / Ticketmaster / Lightcast.

## Wants → tiny objects (keep / kill)

### Want: the sale is a real sale

**Object:** for one UPC at one chain, **was-price vs now-price vs whether “was” ever sat on a public ad for a real stretch of time.**  
Law already in English: FTC *Guides Against Deceptive Pricing*, 16 CFR § 233.1 — a “was $10, now $7.50” is a lie if $10 was a fake peak. Guides, not Part 464 penalties. California is often stricter on how long the old price had to last.

**Gap:** Flipp shows this week’s deal. It does not keep a **hashed history** that proves the “was” was bona fide. Grocery **S** across chains is bigger than this. This is **S cut in half**: one chain, time, former vs advertised.

**Keep as the smallest grocery quark.** Still not a Flipp.

### Want: the beep matches the ad

**Object:** advertised unit price vs what the register / public e-comm price was the same week, same store, same UPC.  
People’s actual humiliation: “it rang up wrong.”  
California already makes stores put a readable price on most packaged goods. Consumer Reports / local news already do one-off audits.

**Gap:** a fail-closed **ad vs beep** row, not a shopping list. Empty-ish. Thin money (store managers, a DA). Honest cousin of S. Do not build a scanner app for fun.

### Want: I’m not charged a different grocery total than the person behind me

**Object:** my quote vs the public sticker.  
Instacart/Eversight already in the news (shoppers in the same store, different totals; FTC CID reporting Dec 2025).  
Colorado **HB26-1210** would have banned surveillance price/wage setting — **Polis vetoed 2 Jun 2026**. California **AB 2564** still a bill, not a file you can invoice.

**Kill as a product.** The tape is personalized, so join dies unless you become a people-hunt of screenshots. Dual-use later hope, not a SKU. Do not found the vetoed ban.

### Want: the total before I say yes (no drip)

**Object:** advertised price vs **mandatory-fee total**.  
Live rule: FTC **16 CFR Part 464**, effective **12 May 2025**, **tickets + short-term lodging only**. Food delivery: FTC **ANPRM Apr 2026** (after Instacart $60M “free delivery” settlement Dec 2025, Grubhub $25M Dec 2024). Not a final grocery-delivery rule yet.

**Tickets:** OEM / Live Nation. We already killed a Ticketmaster.  
**Hotels / Airbnbs:** desire is real (“$340 became $412”). Google Hotels / Booking already race to all-in. Remaining mouth is tiny hosts who still drip resort fees — a clerk, not a 9.  
**Food delivery fees:** calendar is open (ANPRM, not law). Do not mint a rule. Watch only.

**Kill as the industry winner.** Keep lodging drip as a **small clerk** if someone asks for paper, not as the hunt.

### Want: the wage they posted is the wage they offer

**Object:** posting min vs offer letter.  
That’s private. Proof lives in two PDFs, not a public tape. Mishara-shaped. People-hunt adjacent.

**Kill as join.** HR range-vs-OEWS packet remains the **public** cousin (already ranked second).

### Want: the rent they listed is the rent on the lease

**Object:** listing vs lease. Unique unit. Portals are priests. RealPage is the other side’s machine.

**Kill.** Same as the industry cut.

### Want: I don’t lose the slot I paid to chase

Apartment application fees, job posts with no close date, ticket queues.  
Colorado already requires a **good-faith application deadline** on job posts (or “open until filled” said plainly). That’s a **checkbox clerk**, not a platform.

**Tiny, real, not a 9.** Do not build an ATS.

### Want: I can prove what they showed me at 7:12pm

**Object:** `source_url + fetched_at + sha256` of the public post.  
This is the architecture under grocery S, former-price, ad-vs-beep, and Gate receipts. It is not a new desire. It is how (1) and (3) become a file.

**Keep as the method.** Do not sell “trust” as a chatbot.

### Want: feed us this week on this money

Flipp already. WIC/SNAP/health — killed SKU.  
Grocery S is the **economic** object behind this want, not a meal plan.

### Want: don’t wait, don’t get shut off, don’t lose the kid’s place

DMV, utilities, school — no public posted-price tape of a homogeneous good. Unique appointments. Staffed by the state.

**Kill** as this hunt.

## Atomically smallest keepers (stack)

Smaller than “grocery industry,” still the same family:

| Size | Want | Object |
|------|------|--------|
| Smallest | The sale is real | **Was vs now**, one UPC, one chain, hashed ads over weeks (16 CFR 233.1) |
| Small | The beep matches | **Ad vs register**, same UPC-store-week |
| Industry winner | Don’t get a worse public sticker than the next ZIP | **S** across 3 chains, 20 UPCs, 12 weeks |
| Clerk-second | Don’t get a cartoon wage band | CO posting min/max vs OEWS |
| Method | Prove it | Hash of the public bytes at time T |

Everything else on the desire list is either a priest, a unique good, a vetoed/pending bill, Ticketmaster, health, or Gate/Mishara already.

## English

People want not to be played on a number.  
The atomically small version of that, that is still public and still the same item twice, is: **did this store’s “sale” use a real old price, and did the register match the ad.**  
That sits inside grocery S. It is not a new planet. It is the wedge cut thinner.

Do not turn “I want fairness” into a surveillance-pricing startup. Colorado already said no for now. Do not turn “I want the total” into a hotel app. The FTC already named tickets and rooms.

Next object if we keep cutting: **schema + a handful of fixture rows for was/now/ad/beep on one chain**, not a brainstorm of more feelings.
