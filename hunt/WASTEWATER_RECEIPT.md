# Wastewater CoC receipt (Claude) — not a lane

**CoC is real.** APHL/CDC wastewater guides want a chain-of-custody with collection time, location, flow, method before the lab. NWSS already takes a **data dictionary** through DCIPHER: plant + sample + analysis fields together. LIMS shops digitize the paper CoC next to the result. That binding exists as **metadata in the reporting system**, not as a Velaru receipt.

**The 2024 AJPH / NIST paper is not that gap.** Keenum et al., “Optimizing Wastewater Surveillance: The Necessity of Standardized Reporting **and Proficiency**,” is about **units, methods, recovery, comparability, proficiency tests** so states can add numbers. Visualization/dissemination differ; they asked for reporting guidelines, not a cryptographic seal from sampler to dashboard.

**Academic cron → GitHub** is a real mess in some university dashboards. That is not “CDC has no custody.” Occupancy: NWSS, APHL, LIMS, BioBot-class vendors, health-dept contracts. Leftover is standards and lab QA, not a fourth Nisaba receipt.

**Do not ship.** Health SKU is already out (`S_TIER_BREW`, `DREAM_LOOPS`). This is Velaru applied to sewage: same receipt, new poster, civilizational caption. Public-health procurement will not pay $5–8k in 72 hours to prove a dashboard number. Pitching “anyone can verify the alert isn’t fake” is a trust-war, not Go and not Gate diligence.

**Lanes stay:** Gate waves (3 / 3 business days) and Go/clutch. Museum stays museum.
