## Bucket C (knowledge, not outbound)

- **CISA SSDF common form**, **BABA self-cert letters**, **SAM/FAR reps**, and **FMVSS OEM self-cert** are the honest federal cousins of water self-attestation: the United States *chose* manufacturer/vendor signature as the control. Enforcement is FCA / questioned costs / recall after the fact, not a hop GO. Occupancy of “make a list / 3PAO” is the government’s next move if they want one (CMMC already did; 45V already did). Do not sell them a mouth they designed out.
- **SBA 7(a)** is the closest “real OIG dollar gap + private hand on the write” this pass found. Still fail: SBA won’t rent the exclusive door; lenders already live under SOP + guaranty-file review + KPMG/OIG; they will not buy a stranger GO that slows origination. Same miss-shape as pharmacy PA.
- **GAO High-Risk** is a congressional monitoring list. Adding “Improving Delivery of Federal Disaster Assistance” (2025) is fragmentation + workforce, not a missing exclusive door.
