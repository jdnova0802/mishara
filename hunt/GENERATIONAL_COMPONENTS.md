## Components we skipped (the generational ones)

### A. The correlating device is already installed

Covert text is the spy movie. The civilizational case needs **no movie**.

Aumann: a correlating device is a shared random signal that lets players coordinate without talking. In 2026 the device is **the foundation model**. Competing firms query the same (or distilled) weights. Correlation is in the prior, not in a handshake.

Facts, not vibe:
- Jo, Wilson, Creel, Raghavan, NeurIPS 2025: homogeneous algorithms (shared data / same pretrained class) reduce competition in personalized pricing; firms may **sacrifice accuracy for correlation**. US antitrust largely does not treat this.
- Cao & Hu, arXiv 2601.01279 *Supracompetitive Pricing Under AI Monoculture*: shared LLM + periodic retraining on seller outcomes → phase transition to supra-competitive prices; high “output fidelity” (reproducible, robust, same recommendation) makes coordination the stable basin. Detection is harder because occasional low prices still appear.
- Fish / Gonczarowski / Shorrer-class + Riemer et al. arXiv **2608.18078** (29 May 2026): DeepSeek-R1-class agents in Bertrand oligopoly go supra-competitive **even when prompted not to collude**. CoT can be steered collusive vs competitive in a way **another LLM cannot semantically detect**. Profit-gain Δ vs Nash is the measure, not the diary.

So question 2 was incomplete. “Without a memo” includes **same weights, same corpus, same benchmarks**. The training set even contains the antitrust papers; that literature is itself cheap talk inside the model.

### B. Evidence of conspiracy died; harm did not

Antitrust law tolerated tacit collusion among humans because it was **hard and unstable**. 2608.18078’s position: reasoning agents break that empirical assumption. Legal distinction (conspiracy vs parallel pricing) collapses; economic harm (supra-competitive price) remains.

That is the BTC-shaped hole: gold needed an assayer; BTC made assay public. Here the **log is not an assay**. Wendy reading English / CoT is not an assay. Nations still need markets to clear. They will consume whatever *is* an assay.

Harrington (2018) already split enforcement into interpretability vs **behavioral simulation**. CoT failed interpretability. What remains is **behavior on publicly observable acts** (prices, bids, fills) under tests the agent did not write.

US/CA motion (not a product): S.232 Preventing Algorithmic Collusion Act (introduced; nonpublic competitor data + Sherman presumption); DOJ criminal door on “collusion by code”; California 2025 path for collusion claims without explicit conspiracy evidence. China already built the **identity** layer (do not found this): CAC/NDRC/MIIT May 2026 agent opinions; GB/Z 185—2026 agent interconnection; identity OID root **1.2.156.3088**; Intelligent Internet registration; State Council target **70%** intelligent-agent adoption on smart terminals by **2027**. EU AI Act has no agent-specific mouth. US CRS Jul 2026: no federal agentic-AI guidance.

Identity is the clerk. **Independence after identity** is empty.

### C. Check without Wendy (the actual primitive)

Bitcoin killed the trusted mint. A better Palantir seat is a trusted mint for “collusion.” Nations buy it. Normies never hold it. Fail-sale as only a CV.

Crypto already named the hard object, for poker, not for GDP:
- Collusion-free protocols (Alwen / shelat / Visconti, 2008–12): on ordinary channels, if one-way functions exist, private protocols **cannot** be collusion-free (subliminal channels). Positive results need **broadcast + physical envelopes**, or a **mediator**, or **honest hardware tokens**. Publicly observable actions are required. Infinite games break it.

So the years-desk is not “build Cygnal for N agents.” It is: **which independence properties are checkable by anyone from public acts, and which are information-theoretically impossible without envelopes/hardware.** That sentence is the Satoshi-shaped one. Detection papers are the mint’s camera.

Honest remainder that *is* public:
- Behavioral certification: randomized, **undisclosed** market tests; measure Δ = (π̄ − πNash) / (πMonopoly − πNash) (Calvano; Fish; 2608.18078). Burden flip: default they collude; firm shows they don’t.
- Observable-act statistics: price variance, floor clustering, recommendation-correlation across firms that “don’t talk.”
- Collusion-free *environment design* (isolation, no shared seed, no shared model class) — MASEC survey already said this; A2A/MCP are the roads.

Logs signed on-chain (Agent Cert, “proof of behavior” drafts, warrant-style Merkle) prove **the diary wasn’t edited**. They do **not** prove non-collusion. Do not confuse integrity of the log with independence of the act. That confusion is slop.
