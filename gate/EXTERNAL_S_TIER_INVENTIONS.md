## The ten

### S1 — Crisis Handoff Clearinghouse (CHC)
**Brand:** Gate (+ Nisaba scheme)  
**External shatter:** No cross-network crisis handoff (satellite ↔ satellite ↔ terrestrial) **counts** until it clears a single bind. The world gains a clearinghouse for emergency routing commits — not another radio.  
**Primitive:** multi-party clear · epoch · single-use redeem at handoff finality · member floors  
**If latched forever:** crisis communications become a **cleared market**, not a pile of bilateral favours.

---

### S2 — Evacuation Bind Ticket (EBT)
**Brand:** Gate · Velaru (verify)  
**External shatter:** Evacuation and mass-movement routing requires a **single-use ticket** redeemed at the moment of handoff; strangers verify ALLOW/BLOCK without an account.  
**Primitive:** spend fingerprint · fail-closed · ρ-deny receipt  
**If latched forever:** “we attempted the route” becomes a **public fact**, not a press conference.

---

### S3 — Closing Dependency (settlement bind)
**Brand:** Gate  
**External shatter:** Material capital, reinsurance, and high-stakes policy binds **cannot close** without a Gate verify URL on the irreversible commit. Closing checklists inherit the mouth.  
**Primitive:** may-before-settlement · stranger prove · no narrative substitute  
**If latched forever:** money and risk stop moving on chat logs; they move on **proved may**.

---

### S4 — Planetary Fuse Standard
**Brand:** Velaru  
**External shatter:** LIVE / ARMED / DEAD / UNSIGNED becomes the default **world vocabulary** for whether an agent, gateway, or desk may still act — verifiable by any stranger.  
**Primitive:** signed fuse · public verify · fail-closed on silence  
**If latched forever:** “is it still allowed to act?” stops being an internal dashboard question.

---

### S5 — Restraint Receipt as Public Good
**Brand:** Gate · Velaru  
**External shatter:** The valuable artifact is proof of **what did not execute** — blocked binds, denied handoffs, frozen agents — court- and board-grade.  
**Primitive:** ρ-receipt · counterfactual seal · independent verify  
**If latched forever:** civilization pays for **prevention evidence**, not only for explosions and logs.

---

### S6 — Edition Pin of Record
**Brand:** Gate (registry)  
**External shatter:** At every regulated irreversible act, the world pins **which law, form, model, or routing table was live** — one registry of record.  
**Primitive:** edition pin · immutable cite · meter per pin  
**If latched forever:** disputes shift from “what did we mean?” to “**which edition bound?**”

---

### S7 — Agent Mouth (irreversible tool gate)
**Brand:** Gate · Velaru  
**External shatter:** Every irreversible tool act (wire, delete, ship, bind, publish) must redeem a ticket at the tool boundary — OS- and runtime-level, fail-closed.  
**Primitive:** pre-action redeem · single-use · fuse check  
**If latched forever:** autonomous systems lose the right to **silent fail-open** on the acts that remake the world.

---

### S8 — Harm Ledger (civil pattern rail)
**Brand:** Mishara · Velaru  
**External shatter:** Individuals obtain cryptographically anchored records of AI / automated harm; patterns aggregate into a **civil ledger** insurers, courts, and platforms cannot ignore.  
**Primitive:** receipt · demand path · anonymous pattern  
**If latched forever:** rights enforcement stops being a PDF complaint; it becomes **stranger-verifiable infrastructure**.

---

### S9 — Contingent Handoff Syndicate
**Brand:** Gate · Nisaba federation  
**External shatter:** Members pre-clear failover paths; when a primary pipe dies, contingents invoke **through the same clear** — a syndicate of crisis routes.  
**Primitive:** contingent arrangement · CHC clear · member pact (legal + economic)  
**If latched forever:** disaster routing becomes a **pre-funded scheme**, not improvisation under fire.

---

### S10 — Continuity Mouth (civilization reboot bind)
**Brand:** Gate · Nisaba  
**External shatter:** After collapse, partition, or blackout, may is reconstituted only under surviving coordinators with **genealogy of authority** — no ghost kings, no charismatic LIVE.  
**Primitive:** authority genealogy · epoch HALT · non-admin resurrection  
**If latched forever:** power after catastrophe cannot be seized by whoever shouts loudest on a dead network.
