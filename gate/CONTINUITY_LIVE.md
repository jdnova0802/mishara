## The nuts inventions (Living Continuity pack)

**Real gap:** On 9/11, continuity protocol existed; principals didn’t follow it.  
**Invention:** Continuity seats carry an **Obey Bit** — pre-committed, stranger-auditable obligation to disperse / assume / hand off when Continuity Condition trips. Refusal is a **Funeral event** on the seat (Loss Deed), not a private choice.  
**Market shape:** Continuity compliance bond for high-μ desks — exercise logs public to coordinators; no-show = seat death.  
**SKU costume:** “continuity exercise attestation” / “succession compliance bond.”

**Formal:** `Named successor who can refuse without funeral ⇒ paper continuity.`
