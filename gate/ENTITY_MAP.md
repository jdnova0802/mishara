**Status:** formal operating stub — **not tax or legal advice.** Counsel + CPA before first paid license or distribution.  
**Purpose:** Separate **IP ownership**, **operations**, and **personal liquidity** so recurring royalties flow correctly and the throat stays in HoldCo.  
**Companion:** `IP_OWNERSHIP_CHECKLIST.md` · `DISTRIBUTION_POLICY.md` · `PERSONAL_LIQUIDITY_STUB.md` · `BUSINESS_CATEGORIES.md` · `/.well-known/owner-guardrails.json`

## Principle

> **Patents and marks live in HoldCo. Delivery lives in OpCo. Personal wires live outside both.**

The 50–800 recurring-income cohort runs **layered entities**, not one checking account doing everything.

## When to split HoldCo / OpCo

Counsel + CPA trigger (any one):

- First **field-limited paid patent license** signed  
- First **$500k** collected in a rolling 12 months  
- First **employee** on W-2 outside founder  
- First **customer** asking for your IP in their M&A dataroom  
- Non-US licensee requiring **withholding** structure  

Until then: unified Nisaba is acceptable **if** cap table, patent assignment, and bank separation are clean.

---

## Banned structures

- Customer or pilot **owns or co-owns** patent / commit-auth spec  
- Personal account receives **licensee payments** directly  
- **May / CHARGE / override** sold with partner or secondary  
- OpCo **assigns** IP to customer for "partnership"  
- Mixing **catalog securitization** proceeds with weekly opex without counsel
