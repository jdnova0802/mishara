Stranger check

Check a receipt yourself

Someone claims a write was stopped. This page is how you check that claim. You do not log in. You do not take our word for it.

What you are checking

  1. The bytes of the receipt hash to the published hash.
  2. The signature matches Gate’s public key.
  3. If those two hold, the receipt was not rewritten after it was signed.

That is all. It does not mean the underlying decision was wise. It does not mean a court has accepted it. It means the record was not silently edited.
