Trust & proof

What Gate proves, how it proves it, and what we explicitly do not claim.

The question is cheap

Can this agent still act right now?

Four answers only: LIVE · ARMED · DEAD · UNSIGNED. Not a safety score. More valuable than the question is a no that holds.

The bound answer

  • Narrow — yes or no, not a score
  • Enforced — DEAD cannot act; timeout is halt, never LIVE
  • Provable — a stranger opens the receipt with no login

A DEAD hop that still lets bind-and-issue fire is a no that leaked.

The flow

question → four states → hop that fails closed → verify
   ↓
Lookup → LIVE|ARMED|DEAD|UNSIGNED
Hop    → DEAD → verdict:false + restraint receipt
Prove  → stranger verify at {{ velaru_base }}/verify (free, no login)

What we prove

  • Signed public fuse existence at hop time
  • Fail-closed behavior when fuse is DEAD
  • Hash-chained receipts strangers can verify
  • Metered access — no infinite free labor

What we do not claim

  • We do not prove model truth or complete logs
  • UNSIGNED is not a legality or safety score
  • We are not resurrecting fuses from UI — CHARGE webhook only on Velaru
  • Gate is the meter + identity layer; Velaru is the proof engine
