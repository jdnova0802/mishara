Trust & limits

What Gate proves on irreversible writes, how a stranger checks it, and what we explicitly do not claim.

The question

Can this withdraw, payout, or bind still execute right now?

Clearance is binary under uncertainty: if the hop cannot prove allow, the write does not run. Timeout and unreachable are halt — never allow.

What holds

  • Fail closed — uncertain / timeout / unreachable → no write
  • Parent license revoked → child spend blocked
  • Independent verify — stranger opens the receipt with no login
  • their_production: false until a recorded third-party production weld

What we prove

  • Signed clearance state at hop time
  • Fail-closed behavior when clearance is denied or unreachable
  • Hash-chained receipts a stranger can verify
  • No bypass on the welded write path

What we do not claim

  • We do not prove model truth or complete application logs
  • We are not a seat license or Free/Pro API product
  • Checkout does not equal third-party production status
  • Gate is the clearance meter + weld; Velaru is the proof engine
