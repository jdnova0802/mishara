## The ten market inventions

These are not more L2 throats.  
They are the **missing load-bearing products** if Gate is to live through civilizations.

---

### 1. Teeth Latch

**Gap it kills:** DENY with no consequence is forged open.  
**One line:** After DENY, something real must get more expensive — status, capital, access, or amputation of the write path.  
**Market shape:** Consequence bond tied to high-μ DENY — sanction schedule under coordinators; stranger-visible; funeral-capable.  
**Not:** private police. Not CIC-as-warlord.  
**SKU costume (≤2 later):** “commit-control enforcement policy” / “fail-closed consequence profile.”

**Formal rule:** `LIVE/DENY without Teeth Latch ⇒ theater.`

---

### 2. Reality Root

**Gap it kills:** Epistemic war — rival feeds, deepfakes, split publics, competing “proof.”  
**One line:** Prove requires a shared real; without a root quorum, strangers cannot audit the no.  
**Market shape:** Multi-coordinator **prove-root attestation** — which ledger/world Gate receipts bind to; fork detection; rival-root DENY.  
**Not:** one global truth ministry.  
**SKU costume:** “receipt root attestation” / “audit-world binding.”

**Formal rule:** `No Reality Root ⇒ stranger prove collapses into another feed.`

---

### 3. Loss Deed

**Gap it kills:** Legitimate losers — every DENY creates enemies who call it theft.  
**One line:** Publish *who lost, what they lost, why it still counts* as a first-class receipt — not a press release.  
**Market shape:** Examiner/court/public **loss pack** auto-issued on DENY for sacred classes; appeal path that cannot soft-LIVE.  
**Not:** therapy. Not PR.  
**SKU costume:** “adverse-action receipt” / “denial audit pack.”

**Formal rule:** `DENY without Loss Deed ⇒ riot narrative wins; Gate burns.`

---

### 4. Telos Charter

**Gap it kills:** Neutral may gets rented by the worst principal who can pay.  
**One line:** What Gate is *for* — and what it will not clear — must be explicit, revisable under jubilee, never vibes.  
**Market shape:** Versioned charter: anti-runaway, anti-forgery, anti-throne, named exclusion classes; stranger-readable; coordinator-ratified.  
**Not:** culture-war cosplay. Not one man’s morality.  
**SKU costume:** “clearance policy charter” / “commit-class exclusions.”

**Formal rule:** `May without Telos Charter ⇒ captured weather.`

---

### 5. Catechism Ordeal

**Gap it kills:** Operator illiteracy — clicking LIVE without understanding Bone Law.  
**One line:** Seats that clear high-μ acts must periodically prove they still know what they clear.  
**Market shape:** Timed ordeal / exam / scenario drill bound to Title Seat renewal; fail ⇒ seat funeral, not warning email.  
**Not:** LMS theater. Not badge collecting.  
**SKU costume:** “clearance operator certification” / “commit-desk fitness.”

**Formal rule:** `Illiterate LIVE ⇒ cargo-cult mouth ⇒ catastrophe.`

---

### 6. Attested Exit

**Gap it kills:** Totalizing Gate (Filter) vs underground forgery.  
**One line:** Legitimate fork/exit that **preserves prove lineage** — leave without erasing the ledger.  
**Market shape:** Exit protocol: export receipts, tombstone old mouth, birth attested child root, no soft path around DENY history.  
**Not:** “crypto exit and forget.” Not dark bind.  
**SKU costume:** “audit-preserving migration” / “controlled clearance fork.”

**Formal rule:** `No Attested Exit ⇒ underground may or Gate-as-regime.`

---

### 7. Joule Hostage Map

**Gap it kills:** Mouth captive to whoever owns energy, food, repair, pads.  
**One line:** Every welded mouth publishes what **can** can kill it — power, plant, vendor, flag force.  
**Market shape:** Dependence disclosure + hostage score; high hostage ⇒ mandatory Continuity / dual-plant / coordinator escrow.  
**Not:** Gate becomes an energy company (L0).  
**SKU costume:** “operational dependency register” / “commit-path resilience map.”

**Formal rule:** `May that hides its joule hostage ⇒ surprise capture.`

---

### 8. Unborn Seat

**Gap it kills:** Living desks discount the future on seed-, replication-, planetary-class writes.  
**One line:** High-μ classes require a **future-principal** constraint — debt to unborn made executable, not poetic.  
**Market shape:** Charter-bound future seat: delay, escrow, multi-decade inhibit, or civilizational quorum before LIVE; stranger-visible.  
**Not:** mystic council. Not one NGO veto forever.  
**SKU costume:** “long-horizon commit review” / “intergenerational clearance constraint.”

**Formal rule:** `Sacred future writes without Unborn Seat ⇒ present power with vocabulary.`

---

### 9. Domination Facing

**Gap it kills:** Fake neutrality — Gate used as domination with receipts.  
**One line:** Every meta-sheath / CMCP / registry deployment must **face** its domination stance: anti-domination mouth, meta-clearing for rulers, or named hybrid — disclosed.  
**Market shape:** Mandatory facing statement in Mouth Registry / Meta-Sheath license; lying facing ⇒ funeral.  
**Not:** purity spiral. Not “we’re just infrastructure” cope.  
**SKU costume:** “governance posture disclosure” / “control-plane ethics facing.”

**Formal rule:** `Undisclosed domination ⇒ Filter with better HMAC.`

---

### 10. Name Death

**Gap it kills:** Brand immortality fantasy — Gate/Nisaba as throne.  
**One line:** Success = grammar that survives the company’s funeral; brand escheat is a feature.  
**Market shape:** Pre-committed Name Death bit — open prove roots, de-licensable meta-sheath, coordinator inheritance of rails without founder key.  
**Not:** martyr branding. Not “open source and pray.”  
**SKU costume:** “vendor-exit continuity” / “rails after insolvency.”

**Formal rule:** `If Gate cannot die as a name, it will die as a capture.`
