## S-Tier — Most Vital Role (Formal Ruling)

### #1 — Chief of Irreversibility Clearance (May Warden)

**Ruling:** Among S-tier roles, CIC is **most vital**.

**Reasons:**

1. CIC **is the verb** — LIVE / DENY / CHOKE on sacred writes.  
2. Without CIC, every other S-tier role is metadata about a mouth that does not exist.  
3. High undo-cost commits — replication, manifest, handoff, maneuver class — all collapse to: *may this irreversible act proceed?*  
4. Every other S-tier role exists to make CIC **legitimate, unforgeable, priced, and durable**.

**Load-bearing walls around the same throne:**

| S-tier role | Serves CIC by… |
|-------------|----------------|
| Stranger Prove Custodian | Making clearance **count** outside the actor (without it: club fiction) |
| May Metrologist (μ) | Telling clearance **what tier** of sacred it judges |
| Principal Continuity Officer | Keeping clearance **alive** across death / substrate |
| Bone Law Engineer | Keeping clearance **unpeelable** from the machine |
| Consent Lattice Seat | Keeping clearance **legitimate** across flags |
| Light-Delay Commander | Keeping clearance **honest** across light-cone latency |
| Replication Census | Keeping clearance **safe** against copy / runaway |
| CMCP + ɱ | Keeping clearance **settle and price** at civilization scale |

**If one person:** Chief of Irreversibility Clearance.  
**If one system property (inseparable twin):** Clearance + stranger prove — no prove = no chief, only a person saying yes.

**Most vital S-tier (one line):** Whoever holds LIVE on irreversible acts so strangers can still audit the no.
