## Method

For each branch ask three questions:

1. **Before** — what must stay flexible / optional / uncounted?  
2. **At** — what is the felicitous consume-once act?  
3. **Make it count** — what status + settleability frame makes strangers treat it as real?

Then ask the fourth: **what secondary rule is missing?** (Hart: recognition · change · adjudication)

### The one-line transform

> **Gate clears primary acts (may · sheath · prove).**  
> **Nisaba’s transformation is to become the rule of recognition — the secondary institution that says what *counts as* a valid Gate-may across domains, who may change that grammar, and how disputes/pardons adjudicate — without claiming sovereignty.**
