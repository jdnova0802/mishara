## What “liquid” means here

| Type | Counts as | Doesn’t count alone |
|------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Liquid** | Bank/broker cash, T-bills, distributions already paid to you, closed secondary proceeds | Cap-table fantasy |
| **Semi-liquid** | Contracted ARR, prepaid multi-year floors in company bank, exerciseable secondary | “We’re worth $X on Paper” |
| **Paper** | Mark × your % with no buyer / no cash | Vibes |

**Score weekly:** `Liquid · Semi · Paper · Total`.  
**Pride metric:** **Liquid + Semi**, not Total.
