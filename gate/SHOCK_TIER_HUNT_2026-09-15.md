Not another bank-send vertical. Three moves that feel almost improper:

### 1. Productize a **binding publish** that markets already treat as law
**Z1 ISDA DC.** An agent that “detects” a Credit Event is worthless next to a mouth that refuses to treat anything as settled until a DC Resolution exists and is stranger-fetchable. That is logos-before-actus in pure private international market law — and almost no “fintech AI” will touch the DC priesthood.

### 2. Productize **settlement finality** itself
**Z2 CLS.** The cut is not “approve the trade”; it is “admit the instruction into irrevocable PvP.” Your stranger receipt becomes the thing a bank’s ops/risk team fetches when Herstatt ghosts return. Adjacent to FedNow, but a different religion.

### 3. Productize the **internet’s apex write**
**Z3 Root zone.** Changing a DS record is not a DNS product feature — it is a civilizational write. A fail-closed mouth with tech-check DENY + stranger change-id is the kind of thing that makes security researchers and ICANN people sit up. Adjacent to credentialed-publish, but upstream of every name.

Honorable shock (smaller GMV, larger jaw-drop): **Z9 Peerage Roll** — proving a software company ships a DENY mouth for Crown recognition of dignity. Almost nobody will expect that from an “agent actus” line.
