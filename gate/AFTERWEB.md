## What the Internet actually is

The Internet is a **network of reversible speech**.

- Packets can be resent.
- Pages can be edited.
- Copies can be infinite.
- “Delete” is often social fiction.
- It moves **representations of the world**, not the world’s permission to change.
- Its scarce resource was attention, then compute — never **one-wayness**.

That is an astonishing invention. It is still only **half of civilization**.

Speech lets minds *say*.  
Civilization also needs minds (and machines) to **become together** — to spend irreversibility into one shared after, that a stranger can open, that an inhabitant can survive.

The Internet never conserved the spend.  
It never seated the remaining.  
It never made prove the default of the write.  
It networked **logos**. It did not network **praxis**.
