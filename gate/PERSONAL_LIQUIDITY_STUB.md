## Trigger ladder (when to act)

| Personal liquid (approx.) | Action | Owner |
|---------------------------|--------|-------|
| **$0 – $250k** | Separate personal vs ops bank; no lifestyle leverage on throat | You |
| **$250k – $1M** | CPA: tax election review · quarterly estimated tax · basic treasury (T-bills) | CPA |
| **$1M – $5M** | Estate counsel: revocable trust or equivalent · investing LLC · document beneficiary | Counsel |
| **$5M – $20M** | Family-office-lite: bookkeeper + CPA + 1 RIA or self-directed rules · GRAT/SLAT **if** counsel recommends | Counsel + CPA |
| **$20M+** | Full family office / multi-entity — out of scope until earned | Counsel |

**Do not build Layer 4 at Gate 0.** Premature trust complexity is drag, not alpha.
