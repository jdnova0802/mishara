## Challenges that landed

### 1. Illocution ≠ perlocution (Austin / Searle)
Saying / modeling / intending ≠ making it count.  
**Felicity conditions** (preparatory, sincerity, essential) can fail even when words are perfect.  
**Upgrade:** At-the-act is not “physics moved” — it is a **felicitous illocution under constitutive rules**: *this redeem counts as may in C*.

### 2. Constitutive vs regulative rules
Regulative: “if binding, do X.”  
Constitutive: “X counts as Y in C” — creates the game.  
**Upgrade:** Gate is not a better policy engine (regulative). Gate is a **constitutive mouth**: without redeem, there is no authorized act of that type.

### 3. Trying vs succeeding (Anscombe / action theory)
Agents can try without succeeding; practical knowledge ≠ outcome guarantee.  
Basic “trying” can be agentially perfect while worldly success fails.  
**Upgrade:** Split the act layer:  
- **Attempt / redeem attempt** (agent-side)  
- **World uptake** (rail settled, bind issued, load tripped)  
May must bind **both** or liability fog returns.

### 4. Branching time / future contingents (Prior / Belnap)
Before the act: open branches. At the act: a chronicle is selected (thin red line / settled history).  
**Upgrade:** Before = **preserve branch optionality**. At = **chronicle selection** (which future becomes actual-for-record). Continuation ethics = refuse some chronicle selections near absorbing ruin.

### 5. Common knowledge often fails (Halpern–Moses lineage)
Infinite nested “I know that you know…” is often unattainable under timing friction.  
**Upgrade:** Civilizationally real ≠ perfect common knowledge. It is **public evidence + settleable status** (common p-belief enough to adjudicate).

### 6. Contextuality / Bohr topos
Incompatible observables: no global classical assignment across all contexts.  
**Upgrade:** Measurement incompatibility is formal: prove must name a **context** (which commutative frame / which edition / which epoch). Stranger verify is verify-in-C, not verify-from-nowhere.

### 7. Performativity (MacKenzie / Callon)
Models don’t just describe markets — they help make them.  
**Upgrade:** Gate’s grammar is **performative infrastructure**: deploying may·sheath·prove changes what counts as an authorized irreversible write. Risk: bad mouth design performs a worse civilization (1987-style). Design is political.

### 8. Public reason / discourse (Habermas / Rawls line)
Moral/political “counts as right” needs intersubjective redeemability of reasons, not private certainty.  
**Upgrade:** Prove is cousin to **publicly shareable warrants** — not mystic truth, not tribal lore.

### 9. Synthetic epistemic collapse (2026)
When content/identity/interaction are forgeable, societies discount digital evidence.  
**Upgrade:** Civilizationally real now requires **provenance that still settles liability under synthetic attack** — not another detector dashboard.
