## One sentence

> **Infrastructure is not the top of business.**  
> Above it: rules, scarce base, capital (and sometimes forced protocols / demand monopolies).  
> Beside it: platforms, institutions, protocols, utilities, critical manufacturing, extractors, brands.  
> Below it: products, apps, agencies.  
> **Nisaba’s home is infrastructure** — with protocol ambition and product/service skin.

## 2. Categories **beside** infrastructure (peers)

Same altitude energy — “other businesses sit on us” — different mechanism:

| Peer | Mechanism | Monetize |
|------|-----------|----------|
| **Infrastructure** | Pipe, rail, pin, meter, fuse | License, bps, per-check, MAR |
| **Platform** | Multi-sided demand aggregation | Take rate, ads, SaaS |
| **Institution** | Balance sheet + legal charter | Spread, fees, underwriting |
| **Protocol owner** | Grammar / scheme rules | FRAND, membership, SEP |
| **Utility** | Regulated monopoly pipe | Rate base, tariff |
| **Critical manufacturing** | Make the irreplaceable physical | Unit + scarcity premium |
| **Extractor** | Pull the scarce base | Commodity + offtake |
| **Brand / attention** | Own demand without owning pipe | Margin on desire |

**Infra ≠ always richest peer.** Platforms and institutions often extract more cash than the pipes under them.

## 3. Categories **above** infrastructure

These constrain or tax infra; infra rarely constrains them:

| Above | Why above |
|-------|-----------|
| **Sovereignty / regulation** | Can mandate, ban, or seize the pipe |
| **Scarce base** | No power / land / spectrum → no data center |
| **Capital** | Owns the infra company; sets cost of build |
| **Forced protocol** | When the standard is law, implementers follow |
| **Demand monopoly** (sometimes) | One buyer-platform can turn infra into a vendor |

## 10. Quick tests

**“Is this above infrastructure?”**  
→ Can it ban you, starve your power/land, or own you? → **above**.  

**“Is this beside infrastructure?”**  
→ Do other businesses sit on it without it being a pipe? → **platform / institution / brand / manufacturer**.  

**“Is this inside infrastructure?”**  
→ Is it a pin, meter, registry, stamp, mark, fuse? → **sub-class of #5**.  

**“Would this strengthen Nisaba?”**  
→ Does it add **mandate, demand, registry, or meter** — without selling the throat? → **yes**.
