VIOLENCE AUTHORIZATION PYRAMID
(under-discussed. who can hurt whom — and still sleep.)
Pair with: NEW SOCIAL CLASS PYRAMID + Part 0 (How the world works)

Edited notes vs older draft:
- Title is AUTHORIZATION, not "monopoly" (only layer 1 claims the monopoly)
- Layer 2 split: kinetic contract vs remote systemic
- Not a pure ladder: gap kings (5) can be local peak
- Legitimacy-cover tags on every layer
- Sexual/gendered coercion called out under intimate
- Communal/ritual violence as side-rail between 5 and 7
- Hard line kept

CORE LINE
The violence pyramid isn't who throws the punch.
It's who can authorize pain and still be invited to dinner.

SHAPE (read this first)
- UPSTREAM AUTHORIZATION: 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 (scale + laundry)
- GAP SOVEREIGNS: 5 when layer 1 is weak, absent, or for sale
- DOWNSTREAM BLEED: 6-8 (bodies, blocks, basements)
Higher usually = more legitimacy cover + more scale.
Lower usually = more visible + less strategic power.
Exception: locally, layer 5 can outrank everyone else on that block.

═══════════════════════════════════════
1. SOVEREIGN VIOLENCE (peak)
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: FLAG + PAPERWORK
State claim on "legitimate" force
- War, police, prisons, borders, seizure, conscription
- Can kill/cage in daylight and call it order
Feature: paperwork + flag = moral laundry
Note: States CLAIM the license. Factions inside the state sometimes rent the gaps too.

═══════════════════════════════════════
2. DELEGATED VIOLENCE (split on purpose)
═══════════════════════════════════════

2a. KINETIC CONTRACT
Cover: INVOICE / subcontract
- PMCs, private security, private prisons, police vendors
- Operators get paid; principals keep dinner invites

2b. REMOTE SYSTEMIC
Cover: POLICY / "precision"
- Sanctions, embargoes, payment kill-switches, siege logistics
- Hurt at scale without a local fist
Feature: elites keep hands clean; harm still lands

═══════════════════════════════════════
3. ECONOMIC / ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: PROCEDURE
Hurt without a punch
- Eviction, care denial, debt ruin, job death, benefit cutoff
- Licensing + compliance as weapons
Feature: looks like policy; lands like a beating over months
Often the largest quiet destroyer in rich states.

═══════════════════════════════════════
4. PLATFORM / NARRATIVE VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: VIRALITY / "accountability"
Destruction by attention
- Mobbing, deplatforming, reputational execution
- Leak, smear, selective amplification
Feature: no blood; career / family / psyche take the hit
Note: public executions moved to feeds
Limit: platforms SCALE harm; they are not sovereign.
        Layer 1 can still seize, ban, fine, jail, or capture the rails.

═══════════════════════════════════════
5. ORGANIZED ILLICIT VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: FEAR / "protection"
Parallel sovereigns
- Cartels, gangs, extortion networks
Feature: real monopoly locally where the state is weak, absent, or partnered
Not simply "below" eviction — on some blocks this IS the peak.

═══════════════════════════════════════
SIDE-RAIL — COMMUNAL / RITUAL VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: MOB HOLINESS / tradition
Pogroms, lynching, communal riots, purge festivals
Not random street crime; not clean sovereign paperwork.
Sits between gap kings (5) and chaos (7).

═══════════════════════════════════════
6. DOMESTIC / INTIMATE VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: PRIVACY / "family matters"
Closest-range force
- Household coercion, isolation, battery
- Sexual/gendered coercion here and in trafficking pipelines (also touches 5)
Feature: statistically huge; culturally minimized; hardest to exit

═══════════════════════════════════════
7. STREET / CHAOTIC VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: CHAOS (TV's favorite)
What feeds call "violence"
- Robbery, random assault, riots-as-noise
Feature: most visible, often least powerful strategically
Normie illusion: they think this layer is the whole pyramid

═══════════════════════════════════════
8. SELF-VIOLENCE (basement society ignores)
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: PATHOLOGY / "personal responsibility" only
- Overdose, suicide, slow suicide by addiction
Feature: surplus pain with no enemy face
Often an OUTPUT of higher layers (3-4 stress, 5 recruitment, 6 trap), not a separate planet.

INFLUENCE ARROWS (who moves whom)
═══════════════════════════════════════
1 authorizes the map of legal pain
1 rents 2a/2b; 2+1 feed 3
1-3 feed 4 (story laundry + amplification)
1 weak/corrupt -> 5 rises as local peak

8 becomes stats + sermons; rarely dethrones 1

═══════════════════════════════════════
Sovereigns                 -> live in 1, dine above 2-4

while only condemning layer 7.
