VIOLENCE AUTHORIZATION PYRAMID
(under-discussed. who can hurt whom — and still sleep.)
Pair with: social class pyramid + Part 0 (How the world works)
File role: analytical map. Not fiction. Not a hit list.

CORE LINE
The violence pyramid is not who throws the punch.
It is who can authorize pain and still be invited to dinner.

METHOD RULE
- Titles below are ROLE LABELS from political sociology / PE — not a secret roster.
- Prefer primary functions over vibe words.
- "Oligarch" = extreme private wealth that systematically shapes state outcomes
  (comparative politics usage; not a comic-book caste).

SHAPE (read first)
- UPSTREAM AUTHORIZATION: 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4
- GAP SOVEREIGNS: 5 when layer 1 is weak, absent, captured, or for sale
- DOWNSTREAM BLEED: 6-8
Local exception: layer 5 can be peak on a given territory.

CITEABLE SPINE (starter set — open these, don't cosplay them)
- Max Weber — politics as vocation; state as monopoly claim on legitimate force
  https://archive.org/details/politicsasvocati0000webe
- Charles Tilly — state making / war making / organized crime analogy
  https://www.jstor.org / search: Tilly "War Making and State Making as Organized Crime"
- Michael Mann — sources of social power (ideological, economic, military, political)
- Johan Galtung — structural violence (harm via systems without a direct shooter)
- Pierre Bourdieu — symbolic violence (domination misrecognized as natural)
- Diego Gambetta — mafia as industry of private protection
- Mancur Olson — stationary vs roving bandits (why some predators prefer order)
- Susan Strange — structural power in international political economy
- Weberian bureaucracy / "mandarins" as administrative domination literature

═══════════════════════════════════════
1. SOVEREIGN VIOLENCE (peak)
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: FLAG + PAPERWORK
Weberian claim: monopoly on legitimate physical force (a CLAIM, not always a fact).

Acts
- War, police, prisons, borders, seizure, conscription, capital punishment where law allows

Formal role names (citeable)
- Sovereign / head of state / head of government
- Legislature + judiciary (authorization + legality cover)
- Security executives (defense, interior/homeland, attorney general)
- Military high command / general staff
- Police leadership / prison authorities / border agencies
- Praetorian formations (units whose first job is regime protection — comparative politics term)
- Nomenklatura / regime elite (Soviet-origin term; now used comparatively for ruling career caste)

Feature: daylight harm called order.
Note: factions inside the state can rent gaps without the whole state "deciding."

═══════════════════════════════════════
2. DELEGATED VIOLENCE (split)
═══════════════════════════════════════

2a. KINETIC CONTRACT
Cover: INVOICE / LAW OF CONTRACT
Acts: private military/security, private prisons, bounty/collection muscle, police technology vendors.

Formal role names
- Private military companies (PMCs) / private security contractors
- Private prison operators
- Mercenary historically; "contractor" in modern procurement language
- Intelligence cutouts / proxies (deniable intermediaries — documented in covert-action literature)
- Paramilitaries operating with state tolerance or sponsorship (comparative: death squads literature)

2b. REMOTE SYSTEMIC
Cover: POLICY / SANCTIONS PAPER
Acts: embargoes, asset freezes, payment-rail exclusion, siege logistics, export controls as coercion.

Formal role names
- Sanctions authorities / financial-intelligence units
- Central banks + payment-system governors (structural power over settlement)
- Export-control / customs enforcement leadership
- Hegemon / great-power economic statecraft operators (IPE term: structural power)

Feature: clean hands, dirty outcomes.

═══════════════════════════════════════
3. ECONOMIC / ADMINISTRATIVE VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: PROCEDURE
Galtung's structural violence lives here most days: harm without a smoking gun.

Acts
- Eviction, foreclosure, care denial, debt ruin, job destruction, benefit cutoff
- Licensing, compliance, selective enforcement as weapons

Formal role names
- Technocrats / mandarins (administrative elite who run the forms)
- Regulators, prosecutors, administrative-law judges
- Creditors / landlords / employers as private coercers backed by state process
- Rentiers (income from ownership claims — land, resources, IP — classical PE term)
- Oligarchs / plutocrats (when private capital routinely writes policy)
- Crony capitalists (wealth contingent on political connection)
- Kleptocrats (office held to extract)
- Comprador elites (local brokers for foreign capital — development PE term)
- Human-resources / compliance bureaucracies (private Layer-3 administrators)

Feature: looks like paperwork; lands like months of beating.

═══════════════════════════════════════
4. PLATFORM / NARRATIVE VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: VIRALITY / "ACCOUNTABILITY"
Bourdieu's symbolic violence adjacent: destruction via status, reputation, misrecognition.

Acts
- Mobbing, deplatforming, reputational execution, selective leak/amplification

Formal role names
- Platform owners / trust-and-safety executives (private speech landlords)
- Publishers, broadcasters, wire services (agenda-setting research)
- Propaganda / public-diplomacy apparatuses (state narrative arms)
- Commissars / ideological enforcers (historical-comparative; party discipline roles)
- Kingmakers in media-politics (access nodes who mint legitimacy)
- Influencers as retail amplifiers (not sovereign; still can destroy individuals)

Limit: scales harm; does not replace Layer 1 seizure/jail power unless captured by it.

═══════════════════════════════════════
5. ORGANIZED ILLICIT VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: FEAR / PRIVATE PROTECTION
Gambetta: mafia as a market for protection. Tilly: bandits ↔ state formation continuum.

Acts
- Extortion, cartel territorial rule, trafficking enforcement, predatory "tax"

Formal role names
- Mafia / Cosa Nostra / 'Ndrangheta / cartel leadership (organization-specific)
- Warlords (territorial military entrepreneurs where state collapsed)
- Gang bosses / prison-gang authorities
- Stationary bandits (Olson) who prefer orderly extraction to chaos
- Shadow governors / parallel authorities (lit. on rebel governance)

Local note: here this layer can BE the peak.

═══════════════════════════════════════
SIDE-RAIL — COMMUNAL / RITUAL VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: MOB HOLINESS / TRADITION
Acts: pogroms, lynching, communal riots, purge festivals.
Literatures: ethnic riots (e.g. Wilkinson/Brass debates), lynching studies, communal violence.

Formal role names
- Militias / communal entrepreneurs
- Vigilante committees
- Crowd elites who convert rumor into massacre

Not random Layer 7; not clean Layer 1 paperwork.

═══════════════════════════════════════
6. DOMESTIC / INTIMATE VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: PRIVACY / "FAMILY MATTERS"
Acts: household coercion, isolation, battery; sexual/gendered coercion;
      overlaps trafficking when markets form (then also Layer 5).

Formal role names
- Intimate partner as coercer (IPV literature)
- Patriarchal household authority where legally/customarily protected (historical-comparative)
- Traffickers / pimps when commercialization begins (criminal market — Layer 5 bridge)

Statistically large; culturally minimized; exit costly.

═══════════════════════════════════════
7. STREET / CHAOTIC VIOLENCE
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: CHAOS (retail TV product)
Acts: robbery, random assault, riot-as-noise.

Formal role names
- Street offenders / riot participants
- "Crime problem" in public discourse (often stands in for the whole pyramid)

Most visible; often least strategically powerful.
Normie illusion: mistaking retail tip for the system.

═══════════════════════════════════════
8. SELF-VIOLENCE (basement)
═══════════════════════════════════════
Cover: PATHOLOGY / individualized blame only
Acts: overdose, suicide, slow suicide by addiction.
Public-health frame: "deaths of despair" (Case & Deaton) as population pattern —
not a cartoon of personal sin alone.

Often OUTPUT of Layers 3-6 pressure + Layer 5 labor markets in drugs.

INFLUENCE ARROWS
═══════════════════════════════════════

PAIR TO CLASS PYRAMID
═══════════════════════════════════════

Capability lords        -> oligarchs/conglomerates; operate 2 + chunks of 3-4

Surplus                 -> disproportionate 5-8 weather

Respectability often = living upstream while condemning only Layer 7.

MYTH vs REALITY
═══════════════════════════════════════
Myth: violence = street crime
Real: street is retail

Myth: power is nonviolent
Real: power is violence with branding + procedure

Myth: soft != violence
Real: eviction, debt, smear, isolation destroy without fists
      (structural / symbolic violence literatures)

Myth: criminals own violence
Real: states claim the license; criminals own gaps;
      state factions sometimes rent both

Myth: "oligarch" means cartoon villain
Real: comparative label for political wealth concentration —
      measure with ownership, procurement, media, party finance
