# The Most Controversial Subject Document

**What this is:** A desktop research bible of the hardest topics people fight about forever — with plain-English names, real source links, and no unexplained alphabet soup.

**How to use it:** Pick **one topic**. Open the **Main site** first, then the **Must-read sources**. Argue using documents, not vibes.

**Four questions for every topic**
1. Who gets paid or protected if this story wins?
2. What does the actual document say vs. the viral clip?
3. Was this a screwup, a bad incentive, or a design?
4. What evidence would make you drop your side?

**Hard rules**
- Spend most of your time on the linked sources, not influencers.
- One topic for the first 90 minutes.
- **Victim safety:** On slavery survivors, trafficking, child welfare, sexual abuse cases — research systems and documents. Do **not** dox, harass, share illegal images, or dig for personal victim identities.
- This document lists controversies for research. Listing a topic is **not** an endorsement of any claim inside it.
- **Stats rule (Parts 18–19):** Method before number. Courts ≠ body counts. Official dashboards ≠ independent counters.

**Related:** lighter culture/archive lineup = `DESKTOP_TAB_LINEUP_RABBIT_HOLE_GOAT.md` · plain-text copy = `CONTROVERSY_PLAIN_COPY.txt`

## Part 0 — How the world works (brief map. not a religion.)

Fused operating stance for every rabbit hole below. Steal the mechanisms; don’t worship the predators.

| Layer | One-liner |
|---|---|
| **Core engine** | Incentives beat speeches. Conspiracy is real sometimes; aligned mediocrity producing evil is more common than genius cabals. |
| **Power** | Making your preferences other people’s constraints. Legitimacy = violence + story + paperwork. Soft harm (debt, eviction, smear) can destroy as cleanly as fists. |
| **Pyramids** | Class: sovereigns → capability lords → access nodes → legible pros → gig cognition → body/care → surplus. Violence: sovereign force → contracted → economic/admin → narrative → illicit → intimate → street tip. |
| **Money** | Claim on energy, time, obedience. Capital compounds; wages linear. Rooms beat resumes. Proof > mythology. |
| **Status** | Relative rank > absolute comfort. Attention is currency. Reputation is a public hostage. |
| **Info / trust** | Lies got cheap; verification got expensive. Platforms sell your attention. |
| **Time** | Cohorts face different opportunity structures. Tools easier, terrain harder. Birth timing matters. |
| **Tech** | Gold rushes → landlords. Cognition + energy + data concentrate upstairs. Soft captivity with sharp edges. |
| **Stance** | See pyramids clearly. Build scarce leverage: proof, skills that move money, trusted rooms, options. Capacity beats lucid despair. |

**One line:** The world runs on incentives, force, status, and stories — win by knowing which layer you’re playing on.

# PART 18 — War crime stats (who counts the dead)

**In plain English:** People argue forever about war because the numbers never match. One side cites a court, another a media body-count, another a peer-reviewed survey, another a government release. This part is the **stats rabbit hole**: definitions, databases, methodology fights, and theater-by-theater competing counts.

**Hard rule:** Research documents and datasets. Do **not** share illegal imagery, graphic snuff, or victim identities for harassment. Numbers are about systems and methods — not revenge porn for atrocity.

**Four fights baked into every number**
1. **Definition:** Is this a war crime (legal), a civilian casualty (descriptive), or an atrocity (moral/political)?
2. **Method:** Documented named deaths vs statistical survey vs “excess mortality” model.
3. **Category:** Civilian vs combatant — who decides, and with what evidence?
4. **Incentive:** Who benefits if the number is high, low, or “unknown”?

### 18.8 How to run a 90-minute war-crime-stats session

1. Pick **one theater** (Iraq, Yemen, Gaza, Ukraine, Rwanda…).
2. Open **two counting engines** (e.g. UCDP + Airwars, or Iraq Body Count + Costs of War).
3. Write down for each: **definition**, **method**, **date range**, **civilian rule**.
4. Only then compare numbers.
5. Open **one legal door** (Rome Statute article or Geneva rule) and ask: does this number prove the legal elements, or only the body count?
6. End by listing what would **falsify** your preferred narrative (a better census, a leaked roster, a court judgment).

# PART 19 — Domestic fight-stats engines (numbers that start wars at dinner)

**In plain English:** Same lesson as Part 18, closer to home. America (and the internet) fights with **dashboards**. These sites are where the numbers come from — and where the definition fights hide.

**Hard rule (again):** **Method before number.** Two honest people can get different totals if “gun death,” “police killing,” “crime,” “migrant encounter,” or “homeless” means different things.

**Working stance (not a religion):** Incentives + force + status + stories. Stats are a story-layer weapon. Steal clean methods; don’t worship the chart that flatters you.

### 19.16 Freshness cheat sheet (are these updated often?)

**Short answer:** Some are near-live. Many are **weekly / monthly / yearly** with lags. “Updated often” ≠ “final.” Always note **as-of date** + **provisional vs final**.

| Cadence | Sites (typical) | Reality check |
|---|---|---|
| **Near-daily / continuous scrape** | Gun Violence Archive · Mapping Police Violence · Fatal Encounters · Airwars (active theaters) · many CBP dashboards (live extract, later corrected) | Fast ≠ complete. Expect revisions. |
| **Weekly** | ACLED (regional Mon/Tue publishes; some daily for select places) · CDC WONDER **provisional** mortality (Wednesdays) · HDX ACLED aggregates | Best “what’s happening now” conflict/death pulse. |
| **Monthly** | UCDP **Candidate** events · some CBP/ICE monthly tables · TRAC immigration often frequent | Still a lag — not same-day truth. |
| **Annual / campaign releases** | UCDP main GED · FBI UCR/NIBRS yearbooks · NCVS · HUD PIT · final CDC mortality · Guttmacher survey waves · FAS Nuclear Notebook · Costs of War papers · SIPRI yearbook | Gold for methods debates; cold for breaking news. |
| **Irregular / case-driven** | ICC dockets · DOJ CRT pattern cases · ICRC treaty texts · OWID charts (depends on underlying source) | Not a “stats refresh” — event or paper cadence. |
| **Often lagged / incomplete** | Global Terrorism Database (multi-year collection breaks; check latest year) · FBI National Use-of-Force (participation thresholds) · Iraq Body Count (slower after peak war years) | Don’t treat as live ticker. |

**Rule of thumb**
- Argue **today’s politics** → prefer weekly/near-live (ACLED, GVA, provisional CDC, CBP) **and** say “provisional.”
- Argue **what is true in the record** → prefer annual finals (UCDP GED, final WONDER, NCVS, PIT) **and** state the year.
- Never mix a 2024 final with a 2026 scrape and call it one number.

### 19.17 90-minute fight-stats session

1. Pick **one fight** (guns, police, crime, border, inequality…).
2. Open **official counter + independent counter** (e.g. CDC WONDER + GVA; Fatal Force + Mapping Police Violence; FBI CDE + NCVS).
3. Write: **definition · inclusion rules · date range · what is excluded · as-of / provisional?**.
4. Ask who is **paid, forced, or status-rewarded** if this number wins (your Part-0 world map).
5. Only then argue policy.
