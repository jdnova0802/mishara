# The Most Controversial Subject Document

**What this is:** A desktop research bible of the hardest topics people fight about forever — with plain-English names, real source links, and no unexplained alphabet soup.

**How to use it:** Pick **one topic**. Open the **Main site** first, then the **Must-read sources**. Argue using documents, not vibes.

**Four questions for every topic**
1. Who gets paid or protected if this story wins?
2. What does the actual document say vs. the viral clip?
3. Was this a screwup, a bad incentive, or a design?
4. What evidence would make you drop your side?

**Hard rules**
- Spend most of your time on the linked sources, not influencers.
- One topic for the first 90 minutes.
- **Victim safety:** On slavery survivors, trafficking, child welfare, sexual abuse cases — research systems and documents. Do **not** dox, harass, share illegal images, or dig for personal victim identities.
- This document lists controversies for research. Listing a topic is **not** an endorsement of any claim inside it.

**Related:** lighter culture/archive lineup = `DESKTOP_TAB_LINEUP_RABBIT_HOLE_GOAT.md`
