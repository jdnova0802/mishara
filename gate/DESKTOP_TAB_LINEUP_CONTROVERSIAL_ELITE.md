## How endless conversations work here

Every topic below has **four eternal forks**. Pick one seat → argue any fork → you never finish.

1. **Cui bono?** — who gets paid / protected if this framing wins  
2. **Primary vs narrative** — what the document says vs what the clip says  
3. **Competence vs conspiracy** — screwup, incentive, or design  
4. **What would falsify it?** — the question that separates research from religion

**Hard rule:** Spend ≥50% of time on primaries (dockets, FOIA, 10-Ks, leaked archives, Federal Register). Hot takes are garnish.

1. **Pick one seat only** for the first 90 minutes (D1, E1, or F1 are strongest cold opens).
2. Open the **primary** first; open Twitter/YouTube *last* or never.
3. Write three lines in a notes tab: *documented / plausible / vibes*.
4. Force one falsifier: “What evidence would make me drop this fork?”
5. When emotionally hot → switch windows, not deeper into the same influencer pile.
6. **Victim-safety rule (F6 and adjacent):** no doxxing, no trafficking material, no harassment — documents about crimes ≠ license to target people.
