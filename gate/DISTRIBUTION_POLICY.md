# Distribution Policy — Nisaba LLC / Gate

**Status:** formal operating policy — **not tax or legal advice.** Counsel and CPA review before first distribution.  
**Purpose:** Convert company recurring (license royalties, floors, bps, welds) to **owner liquid** without selling the throat.  
**Companion:** `WEALTH_SCOREBOARD.md` · `ENTITY_MAP.md` · `PERSONAL_LIQUIDITY_STUB.md` · `IP_OWNERSHIP_CHECKLIST.md` · `/.well-known/owner-guardrails.json`

---

## Principle

> **Others eat. You eat. Mouth stays. Not a dragon pile.**

Recurring IP and operator cash lands in **Nisaba LLC** first. Owner liquidity comes from **policy**, not hope.

---

## Trigger

Distributions begin only when **both**:

1. Company holds **≥ 6 months operating expenses** in reserve (runway bucket), and  
2. No unpaid payroll, tax, or contract minimums in arrears.

Until trigger: retain cash for Gate 1 proof, non-provisional funding, and weld delivery.

---

## Excess FCF split (after reserves satisfied)

| Bucket | % of excess free cash flow | Use |
|--------|----------------------------|-----|
| **Runway / Lattice reserve** | **30%** | Extend opex buffer; patent counsel; infra |
| **Team / partner pool** | **20%** | Employees, contractors, rev-share **sales** partners (economics only — no may) |
| **Owner distribution (you)** | **30%** | **Personal liquid** — member distribution per LLC structure |
| **Growth / welds** | **20%** | R&D, outbound, conformance, hosted redeem |

Adjust percentages only by **written board/member resolution** — not ad hoc.

---

## What counts as FCF here

**Includes:** license MAR + QIC + bps invoices collected, management floors, weld fees, prepaid annual floors (amortize for policy or treat as FCF when collected — **CPA picks**).

**Excludes from “excess” until counsel confirms:** SPV upfront, forward royalty catalog sale proceeds (reserve separately or treat as balance-sheet event).

---

## Owner equity assumption

Target **~70–80%** economic ownership through age 30 unless a **non-may** deal clearly raises liquid + mark (see `WEALTH_SCOREBOARD.md`).

Secondary sales: **economics only**, **no override**, **max 8%** of your shares unless member resolution.

---

## Banned “liquidity”

- Throat sale or CHARGE / may override transfer  
- Soft LIVE for logo  
- Personal guarantee on customer deals  
- **IP assignment** to pilots or customers  
- Dumping >20% early equity for lifestyle  

---

## Royalty path (intellectual asset class)

```
Licensee → Nisaba LLC (Licensor) → company bank → [this policy] → owner distribution (30% of excess)
```

Licensees and customers **do not** receive a cut of Licensor’s royalty stack unless **separate written** rev-share on **sales** (SI/distributor), not patent ownership.
