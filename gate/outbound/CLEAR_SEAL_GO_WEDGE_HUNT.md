## New wedge-shaped finds (pass filter)

### 5) Nacha 2026 credit-push / “False Pretenses” ACH

**Mouth:** May this ACH **credit** proceed when it was “authorized” under deception (BEC / vendor / payroll impersonation)? Seal pre-push validation + stranger-verifiable halt.

**Primary:** Nacha Risk Management Rules — Phase 1 **in effect Mar 20, 2026**; Phase 2 **June 2026** for all remaining volumes.  
https://www.nacha.org/news/new-nacha-risk-management-rules-now-effect  
False Pretenses definition (Nacha Phase 1 topics): inducement by misrepresenting identity, authority/association, or ownership of account to be credited — covers BEC / vendor / payroll impersonation; complements unauthorized credits.  
https://www.nacha.org/rules/risk-management-topics-fraud-monitoring-phase-1  
Credit-push fraud monitoring resource center: https://www.nacha.org/content/credit-push-fraud-monitoring-resource-center

**Buyer:** Non-consumer Originators · ODFIs · TPS/TPSP · RDFIs (now obligated too) · payroll / AP platforms.

**Verifier seated?** Account-validation APIs / Phixius / bank “validation services” exist for *account status*. **Not** seated: stranger-verifiable **Go/Never ticket bound to one ACH credit fingerprint** before send. Gate wedge = halt receipt, not another validator vendor.

**Cash:** High — same Diligence REVIEW; Phase 2 clock is live/near. Sibling of FedNow/vendor-bank (one who-may-spend atom, three rails).

### 6) NYDFS Circular Letter No. 7 (2024) — AIS/ECDIS underwriting notice (15-day clock)

**Mouth:** Did the AIS/ECDIS path determine the applicant cannot be approved that way — and was the **written notice** (reasons) sealed within **15 days** so a stranger (DFS / applicant) can verify? Fail-closed = no silent drop to manual without notice.

**Primary:** NYDFS Insurance Circular Letter No. 7 (2024), Jul 11, 2024 — https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry-guidance/circular-letters/cl2024-07  
Verbatim expectation: within 15 days of determination that applicant will not be approved under ECDIS/AIS process and can only obtain insurance via non-ECDIS/AIS process, insurer should provide written notice identifying reasons; failure may be unfair trade practice Art. 24.

**Buyer:** NY-authorized life/P&C writers using AIS/ECDIS · CUO / compliance · underwriting-workbench vendors.

**Verifier seated?** Market conduct exams exist; **no** product that stranger-verifies the 15-day AIS handoff receipt per decision. Adjacent to SB 1120 / insurance Diligence — extend insurance list, don’t invent health stack.

**Cash:** Medium-high — same Diligence SKU; NY writers already in your insurance orbit.
