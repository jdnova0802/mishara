# Bucket A — three brand shapes on the same political-economy pass (20 Oct 2026)

**Verdict:** no unoccupied seat. Each shape has a live program that looks like it. In each one, a named body already attests, the payment or license already depends on that attestation, and someone is already paid to check it. A file that is only the firm’s report, where that is actually how the program works, is already inside an auditor’s follow-up. Do not open a mouth off this pass.

Method: `BUCKET_A_METHOD.md` criterion 4, then object-per-hit, plus the acquisition-check and the “statute already assigns this seat” check. A hit counts only when the confirming seat is empty or the only attestation is the firm’s, and an inspector general or state auditor does not already cover that confirmation. Time box: sources from 2024 forward. The brands are the ones already in `gate/family_voices.py`. No new brand. No market-size figure is stated below.

| Brand | Question already shipped | What this pass asked that question of |
|---|---|---|
| Verra | Did both rails clear before bind? | A subsidy, credit, or license conditioned on a labor commitment, released only when the state and the labor body have each confirmed. |
| Erra | Should we act? | A milestone-gated industrial tranche, asked before the money moves, by someone other than the agency that picked the firm. |
| Velaru | Did we commit correctly? | A public commitment (jobs, plant, output) with a stranger-checkable confirmation that is not the firm’s own progress report. |

## Verra — both rails before the license or the credit

### Killed: Minnesota cannabis labor-peace licensure

**Minn. Stat. § 342.14, subd. 1(a)(10) (2025).** https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/342.14 An application to obtain or renew a cannabis license must include “an attestation signed by a bona fide labor organization stating that the applicant has entered into a labor peace agreement.”

**Office of Cannabis Management, Qualified Applicant Guide.** https://mn.gov/ocm/assets/Qualified_Applicant_Guide_4.0_tcm1202-669481.pdf Preliminary license approval requires that attestation. Qualified-applicant status expires if it is not submitted within six months. Preliminary approval is not the operating license. It is the step that lets the applicant take the site and raise capital.

**OCM form, filename dated 24 July 2025.** https://mn.gov/ocm/assets/OCM_Attestation_of_Labor_Peace_Agreement_072425_fillable_tcm1202-669515.pdf This sheet is the applicant’s checkbox: “I certify that I will provide this documentation to OCM as a condition of licensure.” Agreements entered on or after 15 August 2025 must address the duration of the election. The checkbox is the promise to upload the labor organization’s attestation. It is not the attestation § 342.14 names.

**One line:** the license file is supposed to contain the labor organization’s signature, and OCM is the office that withholds preliminary approval without it. A product that kept the agreement from the office would fail criterion 4. A second confirmer is not an open seat.

**Object-per-hit:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who attests | A bona fide labor organization, by the signed attestation the statute requires. The applicant’s July 2025 checkbox only promises to provide that document. |
| What act depends on it | Preliminary license approval, and renewal. |
| Who already gets paid to check | The Office of Cannabis Management, as the licensor. |

**Seat:** assigned. Do not build a labor-peace dual-clear in front of OCM.

### Killed: IRA prevailing wage and apprenticeship increase

**Form 7220 instructions, December 2025.** https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-prior/i7220--2025.pdf The form is how a taxpayer reports the prevailing-wage and apprenticeship increase, and any correction or penalty, for the clean-energy credits and the energy-efficient commercial buildings deduction. The increased amount is claimed on the taxpayer’s return.

A project labor agreement can change the penalty treatment. It is not a second confirmation the IRS must have from a union before the increased credit is allowed. The return is the taxpayer’s rail. The exam is the Service’s.

**One line:** the credit’s labor increase is the taxpayer’s form. Strangers are not the party the form is filed with. Seat is the taxpayer plus the IRS.

**Seat:** assigned. Do not build a wage-and-apprenticeship room in front of Form 7220.

### Killed: DOL Scaling Apprenticeship grants

**DOL OIG 02-26-001-03-375, issued 4 August 2026.** https://www.oig.dol.gov/public/reports/oa/2026/02-26-001-03-375.pdf ETA awarded the grants in July 2019, over $183 million, 23 recipients. OIG tested files and found 30 of 82 had no evidence of a credential the recipient had reported, and 27 of 29 incumbent-worker files had no documentation of the reported advancement. TEGL 23-19 did not allow self-attestation for date of birth; Changes 1 and 2 did allow self-attestation as acceptable documentation; Change 3, issued 1 December 2025, limits it again. OIG’s finding is that ETA did not make recipients keep the procedures, validate the data, or use monitoring to track findings.

**One line:** the missing credential file is the grantee’s, and the Inspector General has already audited ETA for not requiring it. That is ETA’s oversight seat, not an empty second rail.

**Seat:** assigned to ETA, with OIG already in the audit.

## Erra — ask before the tranche moves, and not by the agency that picked the firm

### Killed: USA Rare Earth CHIPS direct-funding agreement

**Direct Funding Agreement, Award ID AP-2026-0044, dated 3 June 2026,** filed as an exhibit to USA Rare Earth, Inc. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1970622/000121390026064453/ea029340201ex10-1.htm Authority recited in the agreement: 15 U.S.C. §§ 4652 and 4659(a)(1), CHIPS Incentives Program NOFO 2023-NIST-CHIPS-SMME-01. The award covers the Round Top mine project ($132,000,000), the Stillwater magnet project ($50,000,000), the Stillwater metal project ($20,000,000), Magnet Project 2 ($60,000,000), and Metal Project 2 ($15,000,000).

Section 2.2.2: the recipient may request a disbursement on or after the date it “reasonably determines” the milestone has been achieved, “without regard to, solely with respect to a Disbursement Request, the Department’s confirmation thereof,” and before the milestone longstop date. Section 2.2.3: Commerce then has up to 90 days to issue a Disbursement Approval Notice if it determines the milestone was achieved and the conditions precedent are satisfied, or a denial if not. The disbursement follows within 30 days of the approval notice. Section 5.1.5: satisfactory evidence that the milestone has been achieved. Section 5.1.6: an officer’s certificate one business day before the disbursement date. Section 2.3: making the disbursement is not an approval of the work.

That is the Evans shape. The same Department that awarded the money decides the milestone, on a request the firm is allowed to file off its own determination. It is not an empty seat. Section 8 of the agreement requires the recipient to permit Commerce, the Commerce Inspector General, and the Comptroller General to visit and inspect in order to determine whether disbursement milestones have been achieved, and it cites the biannual GAO audit in 15 U.S.C. § 4652(c). The same statute is what Commerce OIG-25-021-I (3 June 2025) already restated for CHIPS use-of-funds and worker-commitment oversight. The 20 Oct pass killed the program-level version of this seat. This agreement does not reopen it.

**One line:** the act that releases the tranche is Commerce’s approval notice. A stranger-checkable milestone file would be a second copy of a determination Commerce is already paid to make, and that OIG and GAO are already authorized to inspect. First answer if the product sold the nonpublic request. Second answer if the product is “Commerce can refuse before it pays” — and § 2.2.3 already says that.

**Object-per-hit:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who attests | The recipient, in the disbursement request and the officer’s certificate. Commerce then determines whether the milestone was achieved. |
| What act depends on it | The disbursement. No approval notice, no payment under § 2.2.3. |
| Who already gets paid to check | The CHIPS Program Office, before it pays. Commerce OIG and GAO, under 15 U.S.C. § 4652 and the inspection clause of this agreement. |

**Seat:** assigned. Do not build an Erra gate in front of a CHIPS disbursement approval.

The same kill covers the July 2025 and April 2026 CHIPS facts already written in `BUCKET_A_PE_HUNT_2026-10-20.md`. Commerce verified disbursement requests before paying. Adding a rare-earth project name does not move the seat.

## Velaru — was the commitment real, other than on the firm’s report

### Killed: New Jersey EDA tax-credit certifications

**Office of the State Comptroller, follow-up issued 20 February 2025.** https://www.nj.gov/comptroller/reports/2024/20250220.shtml Authority for the follow-up: N.J.S.A. 52:15C-11. Programs: BRRAG, BEIP, HUB, GROW, ERG. The review covered certifications EDA made in 2022 and 2023.

What is still self-attested inside that review, in the Comptroller’s words: EDA had not tracked or reassessed economic benefits through the award term for BRRAG or Commercial ERG, and “exercised its discretion to not rerun” the net-benefit tests for those programs. Annual reporting of actual performance was implemented for GROW and HUB and not implemented for BEIP, ERG, and BRRAG. Job-substantiation procedures were not expanded for BRRAG and HUB, and the independent auditor EDA hired did not review those projects. As of June 2024 that external compliance auditor had not audited the five programs from the 2019 audit. EDA “did not consistently report on actual performance by award recipients.”

What is not an empty seat: Recommendation 3, the comparison of recipient job data to the Department of Labor and Workforce Development and to Treasury’s Division of Taxation, is fully implemented. The MOU with Taxation is dated 13 September 2022 and requires Taxation to keep the verification records. GROW and HUB have an annual net-benefit policy. The Comptroller is the body paid to follow whether EDA does this, and this is the second follow-up.

**One line:** a stranger-checkable job file would be the wage and tax records Taxation and Labor already use on the programs where the comparison is implemented. Selling those records is the first answer under criterion 4. The programs where EDA still does not reassess actual performance are already the subject of the Comptroller’s open recommendations.

**Object-per-hit:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who attests | The recipient, on the annual report EDA uses for certification. |
| What act depends on it | EDA’s certification that the recipient may claim the credit. |
| Who already gets paid to check | EDA; Taxation, under the 13 September 2022 MOU, on the comparison recommendation the Comptroller marked implemented; the Comptroller, on the statutory follow-up. |

**Seat:** assigned. Do not build a Velaru receipt for a New Jersey credit certification. The residual is an EDA procedure the Comptroller has already written down.

### Killed: Wisconsin Economic Development Corporation performance reports

**Legislative Audit Bureau report 25-30, November 2025.** https://legis.wisconsin.gov/lab/media/okolqbxg/25-30full_74669064.pdf Statutes require each tax-credit, grant, and loan recipient to submit a performance report, and require WEDC to annually and independently verify a sample of those reports. WEDC did that verification for calendar years 2022 and 2023. It paid a consultant $247,500. The sample was 156 reports, 11.0 percent of the reports submitted in those two years. The consultant could not verify every item on 32 of the 156. WEDC had acted on 31 of the 32 by August 2025. One 2023 award was still open as of October 2025; the Bureau told WEDC to finish it.

**One line:** the firm’s report is the input. The statute already makes WEDC buy an independent check of a sample before the Bureau is finished with the question. One unfinished award is WEDC’s open item, not an unoccupied seat.

**Seat:** assigned to WEDC under the verification statute, with the Legislative Audit Bureau already on the biennial audit.

## What this pass refuses to call a gap

- A Verra room for Minnesota labor peace. The statute already names the labor organization’s signature, and OCM already withholds preliminary approval without it.
- A Verra room for Form 7220. The increased credit is the taxpayer’s return. The IRS examines it. A project labor agreement is penalty treatment, not a second required clear.
- An Erra gate on CHIPS tranche release, including USA Rare Earth AP-2026-0044. The recipient may file off its own determination. Commerce’s approval notice is what releases the money, and OIG and GAO already have the milestone inspection.
- A Velaru proof of New Jersey jobs or Wisconsin performance measures. Both states already pay an auditor to say whether the agency checked the firm’s report. The nonpublic wage file is not a stranger’s mouth.

No candidate cleared criterion 4 and also had a confirming seat that was empty or only the firm’s. The three brands were pointed at the structures. The structures already name the checker.
