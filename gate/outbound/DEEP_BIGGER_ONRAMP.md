# DEEP ON-RAMP HUNT — bigger than a 5–8k review

**25 Sep 2026.** Rule: no Issuing / OCT / VCC / Vesttoo-diligence rehash. Primaries only.  
**Ask answered:** Is there a bigger on-ramp than a 5–8k review that’s deep (not casually discoverable)?

**Verdict:** Yes. Several. None are “easier than Monday email.” Bigger ≠ nearer. Ranked by **depth × ceiling × Gate adjacency**.

---

## SURVIVOR 1 — Wildfire / utility **subrogation rights trading** (deepest + biggest)

**What it is:** After a catastrophe, insurers who paid claims own the right to sue the utility (subrogation). Instead of waiting years, they **sell those rights** to hedge funds for cash now. Funds bet recovery > purchase price.

**Primaries (confirmed):**
- Oppenheimer executed **10 transactions** on Eaton + Palisades totaling **>$1B** in recovery rights; **>$125M in one day**  
  https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/west/2025/06/09/826772.htm
- Eaton claims traded ~**47–50¢ on the dollar**; Palisades as low as **~5¢**; buyers seeking **~75–85¢** recovery  
  https://www.kbklawyers.com/kabateck-llp-attorneys-featured-in-law-com-on-wildfire-recovery/
- CA Wildfire Fund / SB 254 adding utility **30-day right of first refusal** on assignments — market still live but politicized  
  https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/the-fires-next-time-state-wildfire-liability-limitations-for-utilities/

| Claim | Status |
|-------|--------|
| $1B+ notional traded on LA fires alone | **CONFIRMED** |
| Mid-market price discovery is opaque | **CONFIRMED** (bid/ask gap is the edge) |
| Gate owns a desk / exchange | **NO — not built** |
| You need Oppenheimer-scale capital to *buy* | **Usually yes** |
| Fee-on-trade / verification / ROFR compliance for mid-size P&C sellers | **Buildable wedge** |

**Bigger than 5–8k how:** One intro fee / pricing opinion / assignment diligence on a mid-book can be **mid-five to six figures**. Ceiling is institutional.

**Why single-digit awareness:** This is special-situations / distressed debt + insurance law. Not LinkedIn SaaS.

**On-ramp shape (not buy-the-book):**  
Sell to **regional P&C / surplus lines** who got smoked by CA fires and don’t have Oppenheimer on speed dial:  
“Assignment pack — ROFR check, claim schedule integrity, buyer intro / bid sheet.”  
Same muscle as Gate diligence (halt gaps, document truth) — **different asset**.

## SURVIVOR 2 — **Subrogation file economics** (contingency displacement)

**What it is:** Carriers outsource subro pursuit at **20–35% of recovery**. Vendors skip low-dollar files → carriers leave recoveries on the table. Flat-rate / AI viability review of **100% of closed files** undercuts that cut.

**Primaries:**
- Netmark: **10% contingency** model published  
  https://netmarksubro.com/
- Last Rev competitive claim: incumbents **20–35%**; mid-size carrier can leave **low-six-figures/year** unpursued  
  https://lastrev.com/workflows/subrogation-file-review
- Cozen / specialty firms: contingency is the standard for large subro counsel  
  https://www.cozen.com/subrogation

| Claim | Status |
|-------|--------|
| Contingency 10–35% of recovery is real market | **CONFIRMED** |
| Missed small-file subro is a known leak | **Directionally confirmed** (vendor marketing; not NAIC) |
| You need claims system access + adjuster trust | **YES — hard** |

**Bigger than 5–8k:** One mid-tier book at even **2–5% of incremental recovery** compounds past review money.  
**Nearer than Monday?** No — enterprise sales into claims ops.

## SURVIVOR 3 — **Guaranty-fund assessment → premium-tax credit** harvesting

**What it is:** When an insurer fails, survivors pay **guaranty association assessments**. Most states let you take that back as a **premium tax credit** (~**20%/year for 5 years**). Multi-state groups mis-track credits, miss recapture on refunds, and leave tax on the table. Retaliatory tax interacts. Almost nobody outside tax/statutory accountants talks about this.

**Primaries:**
- NAIC chart — premium tax credits for guaranty assessments (state-by-state 20%/5yr patterns)  
  https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-chart-tx-60-premium-tax-credits-for-guaranty-association-assessment.pdf
- AZ example — Life/Disability fund offsets at 20% over years; multi-million assessment bases  
  https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/ucaa-retaliatory-arizona.pdf
- NJ Form GFA-IPT — **credit recapture** when association refunds assessment  
  https://nj.gov/treasury/taxation/pdf/other_forms/formgfaipt25.pdf

| Claim | Status |
|-------|--------|
| 20%/5yr tax credit structure widespread | **CONFIRMED** |
| Recapture on refunds is a real trap | **CONFIRMED** (NJ form) |
| Software/consult that maps assessment→credit→retaliatory across 50 states | **Not casually built; high ACV** |

**Bigger than 5–8k:** One mid carrier’s missed multi-year credit stack is often **mid–high five figures+** in tax. Project fee can match.  
**Deep:** Yes. Tax × statutory × guaranty — not a landing page keyword.

## SURVIVOR 4 — **LPT / ADC collateral release** (adjacent to Gate, higher ticket)

**What it is:** Loss Portfolio Transfer / Adverse Development Cover moves legacy liabilities off a balance sheet. Deal sizes often **$5–50M+**; one Artex case study cites **>$7M collateral release** from an LPT. Fronting/captive shops care because collateral was trapping surplus (same *economic* pain as Vesttoo, different instrument).

**Primaries:**
- Artex LPT case study — collateral release **>$7M**; target deals **$5–50M**  
  https://www.artexrisk.com/-/media/files/artex/artex/article-pdfs/loss-portfolio-transfer-case-study-2022.pdf
- AIRROC — $335M LPT/ADC (JRG Re / Fortitude) as market proof  
  https://www.airrocupdate.org/basics-of-legacy-transactions-lpts-and-adcs

| Claim | Status |
|-------|--------|
| LPT can free trapped collateral / surplus | **CONFIRMED** |
| Ticket is institutional | **CONFIRMED** |
| Gate wedge: pre-LPT “is collateral still good / still required / Reg 114 vs LOC” truth pack | **Buildable extension of diligence** |

**Bigger on-ramp:** Pitch Monday list (Incline / Clear Blue / fronting) not “$5–8k review” but **“LPT readiness / collateral release diligence”** at weld-class money when they have a legacy book.

## SURVIVOR 5 — **Reg 114 trust continuous verification** (post-LOC world)

**What it is:** After forged LOCs, smart fronts push **cash / Regulation 114 trusts**. Trusts still fail via custodial games, ineligible assets, and release fraud (see IRMI / Great Western v Graham collateral mismanagement). Continuous **trustee-statement verify + eligibility + beneficiary-call readiness** is the non-sexy product that should have existed before Vesttoo.

**Primaries:**
- NY DFS OGC — Reg 114 assets must sit with trustee directly; “clean and unconditional”  
  https://www.dfs.ny.gov/insurance/ogco2003/rg030803.htm
- FORC / IRMI — trust as LOC alternative; beneficiary unilateral draw  
  https://www.forc.org/public/Journals/2010/Articles/Spring/Vol21Ed1Article3.aspx  
  https://www.irmi.com/term/insurance-definitions/regulation-114-trust
- IRMI — collateral mismanagement litigation is real  
  https://www.irmi.com/articles/expert-commentary/some-thoughts-on-reinsurance-security

| Claim | Status |
|-------|--------|
| Reg 114 is the statutory cash/security alternative to LOC | **CONFIRMED** |
| Continuous verify product is thin in market | **Observational — no dominant named SaaS found** |
| Fits Gate “halt / verify / never release” DNA | **YES** |

**Bigger than one review:** Subscription / floor fee per trust program (think **$5k/mo floor** language already in Gate weld packaging) across a front’s book.

**Yes.** The bigger on-ramps are **catastrophe subro assignment**, **trust continuous verify**, and **LPT collateral release** — not another $5–8k “review” SKU.
