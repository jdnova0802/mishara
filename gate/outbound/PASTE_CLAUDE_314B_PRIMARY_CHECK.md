**Exact Q&A (retained in the June 12, 2026 sheet; tagged `[Issued December 2020]` — still printed in the live 2026 document):**

> **Can an entity that is not a financial institution under the BSA and its implementing regulations, such as a compliance services provider, form and operate an association of financial institutions whose members can engage in information sharing covered by the section 314(b) safe harbor?**  
>  
> **Yes.** FinCEN does not require the organization that forms and operates an association of financial institutions whose members engage in information sharing protected by the section 314(b) safe harbor to itself be a regulated financial institution under the BSA and its implementing regulations. Furthermore, there is no section 314(b) requirement that an entity forming and operating an association of financial institutions be a subsidiary or corporate affiliate of a financial institution.  
>  
> Any entity forming an association of financial institutions must, of course, conform its activities with the other requirements of section 314(b), including the use and security requirements of 31 C.F.R. § 1010.540(b)(4).

**What this does *not* say (honesty bounds):**
- It does **not** say Gate-the-SaaS can share under the safe harbor as itself. Sharing still runs FI↔FI / association↔association after **registration** and **verification** that the counterparty is a registrant (same sheet: verify via FI Portal directory before share).
- It **does** say a **compliance services provider** may **form and operate** the association whose **members** (BSA FIs) then share under the safe harbor.
- Secondary sources that only describe “registered FIs share with registered FIs” are incomplete — they omit this Q&A. Primary wins.
