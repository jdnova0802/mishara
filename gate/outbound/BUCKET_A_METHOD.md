## 4. Whose absence of information is this product monetizing?

**Test:** whose absence of information is this product monetizing?

- If the pitch’s value only works because someone else *doesn’t* know something (what the broker/vendor has on them, what the agent actually did, what a buried dispute history shows) — **kill it**. That is the data-broker shape: extraction from someone else’s blind spot.
- If the pitch’s value works *because* a stranger, regulator, or opposing counsel *can* check it — proof of restraint, not proof of access — it clears this filter. Gate’s thesis (stranger-verifiable write-stop evidence) only gets more valuable under scrutiny, which is the opposite of the broker shape.

**One line, before the write-up:**

> Does this candidate’s buyer benefit from someone else NOT being able to see something, or does it benefit from everyone being able to verify something?

- **First answer** — kill it, regardless of how dense the regulatory language is.
- **Second answer** — proceed to the existing object-per-hit test (who attests / what act depends on it / who already gets paid to check), and to the acquisition-check and the “statute already assigns this seat” check.
