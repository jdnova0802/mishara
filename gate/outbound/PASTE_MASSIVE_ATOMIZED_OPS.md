## A1 — 314(b) national association hub *(the life-changing one that is already legal)*

### What it is
Not a tip award. Not an email. **Become the US Salv Bridge** under a statute that already exists:

- USA PATRIOT Act § 314(b); **31 C.F.R. § 1010.540**
- FinCEN Fact Sheet **June 12, 2026**: https://fincen.gov/sites/default/files/shared/314bfactsheet.pdf  
- FinCEN page: https://www.fincen.gov/resources/section-314b  

### The sentence that changes the company
From the **June 12, 2026** fact sheet (Q&A retained from Dec 2020):

> **Can an entity that is not a financial institution under the BSA… such as a compliance services provider, form and operate an association of financial institutions whose members can engage in information sharing covered by the section 314(b) safe harbor?**  
> **Yes.** FinCEN does not require the organization that forms and operates [the] association… to itself be a regulated financial institution…

Director Blanco’s Dec 2020 remarks said the same out loud (non-FI / compliance providers may form and operate; unincorporated contractual associations allowed). FIN-2012-R006 was rescinded by FIN-2020-R002, but the **operative answer in the live 2026 fact sheet is still Yes**.

### Why this is massive (not a mouth)
| Layer | What you own |
|---|---|
| Legal | Safe harbor sits on **member FIs + the association** when registered on FI Portal |
| Product | Encrypted real-time share of Gate deny hashes / claim_scope / sealed-payee Never / Scenario 3 chips — **no SAR** |
| Market | Every US FI that wants Salv-grade interrupt **without waiting for TRACE** |
| Proof of model | Nebraska Bankers Association already runs a state 314(b) Fraud & Security Community Platform (NBA compliance update citing the June 2026 sheet) — **state-scale exists; national is empty** |
| Moat | Membership contracts + FI Portal association registration + audit trail + typology that only Gate mints pre-push |

### What it is **not**
- Not Gate claiming 314(b) as a bare SaaS vendor (that lane is empty — already hunted).
- Not a whistleblower check.
- Not “ship `/314b-share` and declare victory.” The pack mouth is a leaflet. **The association is the company.**

### Build atoms (counsel-gated)
1. Counsel memo: association charter, membership = BSA FIs only, safeguards under § 1010.540(b)(4).
2. FI Portal access → register **association** on 314(b) tile.
3. Member MSA + security/confidentiality procedures.
4. Hub: peer verify against FI Portal directory → encrypted share of Gate artifacts → audit log.
5. Weld path: only welded FIs are members; Gate operates the association (per FinCEN’s explicit permission).

**This is the one.** Statute live. FinCEN yes. Competitor shape proven in EU (Salv) and at US state scale (NBA). National operator seat is still open.

## A2 — TRACE Act fraud-information-sharing system *(contingent megaseat)*

### What it is
House Financial Services discussion draft (hearing materials 5 Mar 2026): **Transaction Risk Analytics and Collaborative Exchange Act of 2026**.

Primary PDF: https://docs.house.gov/meetings/BA/BA20/20260305/119023/BILLS-119pih-securelysharefraud-relatedi.pdf  

Creates, if enacted:
- GLBA §502 amendment: FIs may disclose NPPI to another FI **or to a fraud-information-sharing system** for covered fraud-prevention purposes (incl. **AI train/test/validate**, typologies, risk signals).
- New FCRA §623A: such a system **shall not be treated as a consumer reporting agency** if not used for marketing/underwriting/pricing.
- RFPA carve for transmitting covered info to regulators/LE through the system.

### Why massive
This is the **statutory US Salv** with an explicit **system operator** role and an **AI training** permission that 314(b) does not spell out. Whoever becomes the default “fraud-information-sharing system” under TRACE owns the network.

### Status honesty
**Not law.** Discussion draft at a hearing. Separate from H.R. 3823 (firearms TRACE — different bill). Do not build product on hope. **Do** track and, if/when introduced + marked up, be the operator lobby / reference architecture.

**Relation to A1:** A1 (314(b) association) is the thing you build **now** so that when TRACE lands you are already the system Congress pictured.

## A3 — STOP Payments Fraud Act (H.R. 9331) *(contingent hold-infrastructure)*

### What it is
**H.R. 9331** — Strengthening Transaction Oversight and Preventing Payments Fraud Act of 2026. Amends Expedited Funds Availability Act so Reg CC availability **shall not apply** to checks / wires when the receiving depository has **reasonable suspicion** of fraud (indicators a reasonable person would suspect). Joint Fed Board + CFPB rulemaking within one year of enactment. Includes FI-to-FI communication about invoking the exception.

### Status
- Introduced 18 Jun 2026  
- Ordered reported **51–0** (30 Jun 2026)  
- Reported amended; **placed on Union Calendar No. 692** (1 Sep 2026)  
- Congress.gov: https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/9331  
- H. Rept. 119-792  

### Why massive if enacted
Uncapped (as introduced) suspicion holds create a **compliance product every FI must have**: documented indicators, sealed refuse, audit trail before holding funds. That is Gate’s face — not a share hub, a **hold-justification factory** at national deposit-rail scale.

### Honesty
Still need House floor + Senate + signature + rulemaking. **Do not ship a `/stop-hold` mouth on a bill.** When (if) signed, build the indicator pack against the **final rule**, not the hearing story.
