# Cross-domain gap hunt — EMPTY

**Verdict: no clearing candidate.** Same bar as DSP § 202.1104 / ERCOT / Qusar. Domains searched were outside tonight’s six (insurance, payments, AI/agent policy, grid energy, attestation/verification, DSP already shipped). Real citation or nothing; do not force.

Bar used for each lead:
1. Irreversible action (release / shipment / transfer / certification)
2. Live, enforced, dated report-or-verify duty
3. Stranger currently cannot check independently
4. Unoccupied by an existing vendor product that already owns the classify+clock mouth
5. Reducible to a Gate mouth (REPORT DUE | NOT THIS | HOLD)

## Cleared domains / leads that failed

### Hazardous waste e-Manifest (near-miss — occupied)

- **Primary:** 40 CFR 264.72(c)(2) Discrepancy Report; 40 CFR 262.42 Exception Report; Third Rule **89 FR 60692** (July 26, 2024). Electronic-only for Discrepancy / Exception / Unmanifested Waste reports beginning **December 1, 2025**. Clocks: facility 20-day reconcile then report; LQG 45-day search / 60-day Exception Report.
- **Shape:** custody receipt / transporter acceptance → clock → REPORT DUE. Cross-domain (RCRA shipping, not DSP).
- **Kill:** filer-side deadline/alert products already own the mouth — WasteBits (discrepancy alerts), IMEC (45/60-day exception alerts), RCRAReady (manifest return-copy / RCRA window follow-up). Public can pull manifest tables 90 days post-receipt; reports themselves are regulator/RCRAInfo-side, but the Gate-shaped classify+clock for the obligated party is not open land.

### MoCRA cosmetics SAE (occupied)

- **Primary:** 21 U.S.C. § 364a — serious adverse event to FDA within **15 business days** of receipt by the responsible person (enforceable Dec 29, 2023).
- **Kill:** Registrar Corp Cosmetri Adverse Event Management — intake → investigation → MedWatch 3500A within the 15-day clock.

### FSMA Food Traceability Rule (not live-enforced)

- **Primary:** 21 CFR Part 1 Subpart S; original compliance **January 20, 2026**.
- **Kill:** FDA intends not to enforce before **July 20, 2028** (congressional directive + FDA statements). CTEs/KDEs are real, but the bar requires a live enforced duty.

### ALTA / title wire verification (not enforced law)

- ALTA Best Practices v4.x wire MFA / independent verify — industry voluntary. CertifID and peers occupy the commercial niche. No statute-shaped REPORT DUE mouth.

### PHMSA hazmat incident Form 5800.1 (stranger can check)

- **Primary:** 49 CFR 171.15 / 171.16 (12-hour phone / 30-day written). Old.
- **Kill:** PHMSA public Hazmat Incident Report Search Tool — opposite of stranger-blind.

### FAA Remote ID (stranger can check; occupied)

- Enforcement after discretionary policy ended **March 16, 2024**. FAA-accepted Declaration of Compliance list is public (`uasdoc.faa.gov`). Not a Gate hole.

### Customs ISF 10+2 / AES / vessel NOA (old + broker-crowded)

- ISF: 19 CFR 149.2 (2009-era). Vessel NOA: 33 CFR 160 Subpart C (eNOAD). Filing stack owned by customs brokers / ACE / NVMC. No fresh dated unoccupied classify mouth.

### DSCSA / DEA SORS (pharma — crowded)

- DSCSA serialization/verification stack is vendor-dense. SORS Online (SUPPORT Act, Oct 2019) + 21 CFR 1301.74(b) — distributor compliance systems own suspicious-order detection/reporting.

### AIM Act HFC reclamation reports (calendar; not yet due)

- **Primary:** 40 CFR 84.112(f); ER&R final rule **89 FR 82682** (Oct 11, 2024). Reports due **Feb 14, 2027** and **Feb 14, 2028** via HAWK — annual calendar, not an event-triggered release clock. Allocation/e-GGRT-adjacent tooling already dense.

### Ballast water electronic record books (occupied)

- **Primary:** MEPC.383(81) — electronic BWRB approval / ship-specific declaration in force **1 October 2025**; new BWRB format **1 February 2025** (MEPC.369(80)). DNV and other ROs already issue approvals/declarations.

### FIFRA § 7 annual establishment report / § 6(a)(2) incidents

- § 7: March 1 annual Form 3540-16 via SSTS — calendar, not event-triggered. § 6(a)(2) / 40 CFR 159.184: fatality 15-day, other severity buckets 30/90-day — real clocks, but 1997–98 rule vintage and registrant compliance shops own the workflow. Pesticide NOA (EPA Form 3540-1 / 19 CFR 12.112–12.113) is ACE/PGA broker land.

### CPSC § 15(b) (near-miss on stranger-blind — fails dated/unoccupied)

- **Primary:** 15 U.S.C. § 2064(b); 16 CFR Part 1115 — report “immediately” (24 hours; investigation presumed ≤ 10 working days). Filings are confidential (not published on SaferProducts.gov). Statute itself says a later party need not report only with *actual knowledge* CPSC was informed — and CPSC generally will not confirm to strangers. That is a real stranger-blind structure.
- **Kill:** ancient CPSA duty; SaferProducts.gov business portal is the filing channel; law firms and tools (e.g. RecallDocket Section 15 preparation) already work the classify/report lane. No DSP-class fresh dated hook.

### Telecom CPNI annual certification

- **Primary:** 47 CFR 64.2009(e) — officer certificate due March 1, EB Docket 06-36. Calendar certification, not irreversible-event clock. Longstanding; STIR-SHAKEN / Robocall Mitigation Database are separately public/occupied.

### FAA Part 5 SMS expansion

- Effective **May 28, 2024**; Part 121 SMS update by **May 28, 2025**; Part 135 / 91.147 by **May 28, 2027** with declaration of compliance to the local FAA office. System-implementation mandate, not a per-shipment report clock. Aviation SMS vendors occupy tooling.
