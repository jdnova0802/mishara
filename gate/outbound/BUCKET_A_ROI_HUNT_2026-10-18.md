# Bucket A — live regulatory gaps (18 Oct 2026)

**Verdict:** no unoccupied seat that is in force now, clears the blind-spot test, and is a Gate-shaped pre-write a stranger can check. Do not open a new mouth off this pass.

Method: `BUCKET_A_METHOD.md` criterion 4, then object-per-hit (who attests / what act depends on it / who already gets paid to check), plus the acquisition-check and the “statute already assigns this seat” check. Real cite or cut. No market-size figure is stated below, because none was collected.

## The only live pre-write that matches the shape

**Visa Core Rules and Visa Product and Service Rules, Edition April 2026, §4.1.24.3 (ID# 0031169) and §4.1.24.10 (ID# 0031176).** Change log: agentic payment requirements effective 18 April 2026. Source: https://www.visa.com/vcom-assets/content/dam/VCOM/download/about-visa/visa-rules-public.pdf (text read 18 Oct 2026).

Before an agentic transaction, the Agentic Payment Provider must:

- obtain cardholder consent to provision a token from the payment credential and to use the cardholder-defined payment instruction for search and purchase
- state the expiration date of that instruction
- obtain acknowledgement that the cardholder is responsible for the provider’s actions
- verify identity under the Visa Intelligent Commerce specifications before storing a credential and before acting on the instruction
- if a credential is stored, get express informed consent (last four digits, how changes are notified, how the credential will be used, agreement expiration, any trial length)
- retain that record for the life of the agreement and give it to the cardholder or the issuer on written request

§4.1.24.10: the cardholder is responsible for the provider’s actions as if the cardholder initiated the transaction. §4.1.24.11: Visa may disqualify the provider. The public rule text read here does not state a dollar fine.

**One line:** the buyer benefits because the cardholder and the issuer can be given the instruction. That is the second answer. It clears criterion 4. A product that kept the instruction from the cardholder would fail the filter and would contradict the rule’s own “provide it on written request” sentence.

**Object-per-hit:**

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Who attests | The Agentic Payment Provider, by retaining the consent and producing it to the cardholder or issuer. |
| What act depends on it | The agentic card charge. It does not proceed as an Agentic Transaction under §4.1.24.3 until those steps are done. |
| Who already gets paid to check | Visa. §4.1.24.1 and §4.1.24.2 require enrollment in Visa Intelligent Commerce and registration with Visa. Identity verification is “in accordance with the Visa Intelligent Commerce specifications,” not an open third-party seat. |

**Seat:** assigned. Do not build an agentic-consent vault or a “Visa agentic chargeback” mouth. The parked mouth stays parked: the April 2026 edition still has no agent-specific dispute condition in the sections read. The change log does add Compelling Evidence 3.0 updates for dispute condition 10.4, effective 24 October 2026, including multi-merchant prior transactions as evidence (sections 11.7.5.3 and 11.7.5.6, ID# 0030254 and 0030257). That is a representment tweak inside Visa’s existing 10.4 remedy, six days out, on a dispute stack acquirers and processors already run. It is not a new reason code and not a new seat.

What Nisaba already has is the other side of the same write: a stranger-verifiable issuer decision on `issuing_authorization.request`. That is the live mouth. This hunt does not add a second one.

## In force, and killed

### California risk assessment before the processing starts

**11 CCR § 7155(a)(1), operative 1 January 2026** (Register 2025, No. 39). A business must conduct and document the risk assessment before initiating a processing activity listed in § 7150(b). Processing already underway on 1 January 2026 must be assessed no later than 31 December 2027 (§ 7155(b)). CPPA’s 23 September 2025 announcement: risk-assessment compliance begins 1 January 2026; the attestation and summary go to the agency by 1 April 2028. https://www.cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250923.html · https://govt.westlaw.com/calregs/Document/I0298AEB09FAB11F091968C68B7850296

**One line:** a product that sells or explains the assessment (logic, categories, service providers, the decision to proceed) benefits because outsiders do not have that file. First answer. Kill. The agency sees a summary years later. The business is the party who must write it. That is a paperwork seat, not a public write-stop.

ADMT use for a significant decision remains a separate clock: CPPA says those duties begin 1 January 2027. The existing `/admt` mouth already cites 11 CCR § 7200(b). Do not re-cut it. Do not pitch it as explaining a denial.

### Texas TRAIGA

**Tex. Bus. & Com. Code ch. 552, HB 149, effective 1 January 2026.** Attorney General has exclusive enforcement. No private right of action (§ 552.101). Cure: written notice, then 60 days (§ 552.104). Civil penalties in the statute: $10,000–$12,000 curable, $80,000–$200,000 uncurable, $2,000–$40,000 per day continued (§ 552.105). https://capitol.texas.gov/tlodocs/89R/billtext/html/HB00149F.htm · https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint/consumer-ai-rights

Those ranges are the statute’s, not money collected. A 11 October 2026 secondary check of the Attorney General’s news releases found no public TRAIGA action (https://texasaireport.com/news/traiga-enforcement-watch-september-2026). A civil investigative demand need not be announced, so “no public case” is not “no investigation.”

**One line:** the cure package is for the Attorney General, not a stranger-verifiable stop-receipt on a payment write. Seat is the deployer plus the Attorney General. Not a Gate mouth.

### Nacha fraud monitoring, phase 2

**Practical date 22 June 2026** (technical date 19 June 2026, a federal holiday). https://www.nacha.org/rules/risk-management-topics-fraud-monitoring-phase-2

Nacha’s own page: monitoring is not required pre-processing, and RDFI credit monitoring is not required pre-posting. The False Pretenses mouth already covers the classify step and says it is a monitoring duty, not a pre-push mandate. Phase 2 drops the volume thresholds. It does not create a new pre-write seat. A product whose value is “we see the mule pattern the receiver does not” is the broker shape. Kill as a new build.

### DOJ Data Security Program

**28 CFR Part 202.** Prohibitions from 8 April 2025. Subpart J, § 202.1103, and § 202.1104 from 6 October 2025. NSD’s 90-day non-priority civil window was 8 April through 8 July 2025 only. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-28/chapter-I/part-202 · NSD FAQ PDF dated 24 September 2025.

`/dsp-reject` already classifies the § 202.1104 14-day reject report. Subpart J’s audit is an audit seat on the U.S. person who engages in a restricted transaction. Do not become that auditor. The program regulates access to bulk sensitive data. A product that sells the underlying data fails criterion 4 on its face.
