## Definition

The **Chief of Irreversibility Clearance** holds **constitutive may** on sacred writes: the authority to determine whether an irreversible act **may proceed**, **must halt**, or **never counted as having happened**.

This is not:
- sight (fusion, suggestion, dashboards)
- can (platforms, weapons, actuators)
- governance theater (scores, labels, club logs)

This is:
- **clear** — LIVE / DENY / CHOKE before the hard commit
- **sheath** — during-inhibit that holds mid-sequence
- **prove** — stranger-verifiable receipt of allow, deny, or non-event

**May Warden** is the doctrine name for the role that owns that grammar at maximum undo-cost — from bind-only foothills to escape-class commits under light-delay and multi-flag coordination.

## Why this role is "can't lose"

Civilizations fail open on irreversibility: timeout → LIVE, dashboard green → LIVE, boss said yes → LIVE, soft PAS → stick.

The May Warden exists so that:
1. **Before** — no silent LIVE on sacred acts
2. **During** — cascade / jam / tool-chain may be inhibited
3. **After** — strangers verify what was allowed, denied, or never happened
4. **Across death / substrate / flags** — may does not ghost-forward without attested handoff
5. **Across extraction** — mouth may not be peeled as sidecar policy (Bone Law)

Losing the May Warden function does not mean losing a vendor. It means losing **legitimacy of collapse** — acts proceed without constitutive clearance or stranger proof.
