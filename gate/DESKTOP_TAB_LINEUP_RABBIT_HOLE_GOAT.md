## Session choreography (how to actually get lost)

1. **Warm (10 min):** A1 Unusual Articles → click *one* strange entry → follow three blue links max, then stop.
2. **Engine (45–90 min):** A2 Tropes *or* A3 SCP *or* A4 E2 — pick one; do not tab-hop.
3. **Attic (open-ended):** A8 Archive texts search *or* A9 GE-97 catalog *or* A11 Discmaster query.
4. **Priesthood switch:** When culture fatigue hits, flip to Window B — start B8 ISDA DC resolutions or B6 Charlotin newest cases. Read *primary*, not Twitter takes.
5. **Stall break:** C2 Useless Web once; then return to A or B. Do not open a third window of listicles.

**Hard rule:** Aggregators (C1) are *maps*. Primaries (A/B) are the hole. If you spend the session on maps, you failed the lineup.
