### 1. Medium (where the act lives)

| Medium | Example irreversible edge |
|--------|---------------------------|
| Digital | Bind, settle, sign, mint, open-weights publish |
| Cyber | Destructive or cascading network acts |
| Energy | Shed, trip, reconnect, pulsed dump |
| Matter / kinetic | Launch, release, impact, robot force |
| Spectrum / EM | Jam, dazzle, directed energy |
| Orbital / Δv | Burn, debris-making maneuver |
| Quantum | Measure/collapse, one-shot key consume |
| Biological | Release / replicate (state-fenced, extreme care) |
| Legal / identity | License, sanction, status that won’t un-write cleanly |

**Undo-cost** (thermo + legal + blast radius) ranks what must pass the mouth.

### 2. Time

| Phase | Job |
|-------|-----|
| **Before** | Clear or DENY — no silent LIVE |
| **During** | Inhibit mid-sequence (jam, cascade, tool-chain, burn, dose) |
| **After** | Stranger-verifiable receipt — what was allowed, denied, or never happened |

Most vendors only ship a weak **before**. **During** and **prove-after** are the scarce layers.

### 3. Role

| Role | Job |
|------|-----|
| Map | Act graph, driver edges, undo-cost |
| Coordinate | Levers across desks / agents / allies |
| Clear | LIVE only if justified + quorum policy |
| Inhibit | Fail-closed halt that holds |
| Prove | Receipt outside the actor’s trust boundary |

**Director** = runtime mouth on the act.  
**Manufacturing / engineering** = may designed into the artifact (PLM, firmware, HIL, factory test gates) so ripping the mouth out means redesigning the product.

Long-term Gate aims at **both**: permission on the act **and** in the bones.
