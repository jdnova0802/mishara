### Why this is Columbus (not spices)

Everyone is sailing for **more dispatchable MW** (the spice route).

The landmass is: **cheap remote actuation of private physical plant at population scale** created a new scarcity — **justified irreversibility at the wall socket** — and the industry is still selling last era’s binding (efficiency / revenue).

Dated cracks already visible:
- Texas PUC formal complaint vs Tesla Electric VPP (**Mar 2026**): aggregator priority held charge → customer forced onto expensive grid power; disclosure fight ([Energy Choice Matters](https://www.energychoicematters.com/stories/20260320a.html)).
- Australia AGL VPP battery-drain trust crisis (**May 2025**, ABC).
- FERC 2222 implementation wave makes aggregator→wholesale→device actuate a **regulated mass path**, not a pilot.
