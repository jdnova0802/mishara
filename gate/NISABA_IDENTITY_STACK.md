> **Nisaba is an expeditious clearing entity: it applies compressed tempo to every loop it owns; it seeks DTCC-class gravity on the bind; it maintains a Swiss face and an ARM-class protocol latch; it embeds under crisis when invoked; it rents S-tier load-bearing assets and does not sell them; it is never a spectacle company and never a slow museum.**

## 1. Permanent tempo (not a phase)

Compression is **identity**, not a pre–Gate 1 costume.

| Rule | Determination |
|------|----------------|
| **Owned latency** | Latency under Nisaba’s control shall be minimized (checkout, outbound, quotes, uptime, decisions). |
| **Foreign latency** | Counterparties’ calendars, regulators, and physics are not treated as Nisaba’s pulse; they are scheduled around, not awaited as existential pause. |
| **Parallel paths** | Intellectual property, go-to-market, and proof advance in parallel when resources allow. |
| **Single choke** | Expeditious tempo does not authorize a second planetary choke before latch. |
| **Fail-closed** | Speed shall not produce soft-yes; Gate remain fail-closed under uncertainty. |
| **Exterior / interior** | Exterior presentation remains quiet; interior operations remain compressed. |
