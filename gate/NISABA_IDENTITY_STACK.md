## 1. Tempo

Nisaba treats operational compression as a permanent attribute of the institution, not a temporary founding mood.

| Principle | Statement |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Owned latency** | Processes under Nisaba’s control — issuance, redemption, verification, commercial fulfillment, counsel coordination, system availability — shall be executed with minimal delay. |
| **External latency** | Counterparties’ schedules, regulatory timelines, and physical constraints are planned around; they do not define the institution’s internal pulse. |
| **Concurrency** | Intellectual property, commercial development, and proof of operation advance concurrently insofar as resources permit. |
| **Focus** | Compression does not authorize diffusion into a second planetary choke before the first has latched. |
| **Discipline** | Speed does not authorize soft approval. Gate remains fail-closed when authority is incomplete. |
| **Presentation** | External demeanor remains restrained; internal execution remains compressed. |
