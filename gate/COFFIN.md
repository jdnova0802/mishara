## 1. Bigger than **writing** — **Act-Script (Σ)**

**Writing** externalized speech and law into durable marks. It made memory portable.

**Hole:** Writing records *what was said or claimed*. It does not conserve *what was spent*. Ledgers of money are a thin special case. Machine irreversible writes have no native script of may→remaining.

### Invention: **Act-Script (Σ)**
A script whose only well-formed sentences are **may · spend · remaining · prove** — marks that fail closed like books, open to strangers like writing, but bind the *world-after*, not the utterance.

Writing made history.  
**Act-Script makes the after legible.**

| Writing | Act-Script |
|---------|------------|
| Marks about the world | Marks that *are* the spend of one-wayness |
| Can lie freely | Books fail closed; prove is native |
| Reader interprets | Inhabitant lives in the remaining |

**Monetize:** license Σ as the interchange format of irreversible clears; apostille of well-formed acts; Conformant = “speaks Σ.”  
**Special case of:** H₀ tablet · R₀ packet.
