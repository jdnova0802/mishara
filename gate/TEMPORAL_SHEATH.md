# Temporal Sheath

Time is part of the mouth.

Gate is not only *whether* a write may LIVE. It is *when* force may exist,
expire, be required, or be refused. Without a temporal sheath, CHOKE/LIVE is
spatial metallurgy only — on/off. With it, the mouth has **spacetime**.

**Under coordinators. Never sovereign.**

Lead metaphors stay: commit · mass · sheath · gravity · legitimacy of collapse.  
Irreversibility is physics footnote — not brand.

## Why timing

Most soft power is timing abuse:

- **Speed aristocracy** — executives skip cool-off
- **Delay aristocracy** — HOLD forever as strategy
- **Deadline weapons** — force another principal's clock
- **Stale LIVE** — old clearance worn as fresh
- **Backdate theater** — rewrite when the mouth spoke
- **Eternal pending** — CHOKE as cowardice
- **Forever pardon** — mercy without sunset

Temporal Sheath makes those first-class haunts and operators.
