About Mishara

Mishara is the first place to go when an AI decision harmed you — rejected from a job, denied housing, deactivated on a gig platform, shadowbanned, denied benefits, or wrongfully screened out.

Today, people in those situations have nowhere to turn that gives them proof. Mishara changes that.

Three layers

  • Receipt — Document what happened. Get a cryptographic record powered by Velaru.
  • Action — Plain-English rights guidance and demand letter templates.
  • Pattern — Anonymous aggregation to surface systemic harm and class-action potential.

Mishara stores no personal descriptions in its pattern database — only platform, harm type, classification, and receipt hash.

Mishara means justice in Akkadian — protection of the weak from the powerful.
