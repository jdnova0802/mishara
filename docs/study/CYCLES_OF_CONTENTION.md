**Formal name of the subject:** *contentious politics*; the wave shape is a **cycle of contention** (Charles Tilly, Sidney Tarrow, Doug McAdam). “Social-movement waves” is the street phrase. **Waves of feminism** are one family *inside* this file, not the spine.

**Companion files (different machines — do not mash):** techno-economic paradigms (Perez); cohort/age-structure; hegemonic cycles; medium/attention. ## 0. What a cycle is (operational)

A cycle of contention is a **temporally clustered rise and fall of collective claim-making** — protests, strikes, campaigns, revolutions, countermobilization — that **diffuses** across groups and sites, then **exhausts** or **institutionalizes** (Tarrow, *Power in Movement*).

**Mechanism stack (do not skip):**

1. **Political opportunity / threat** — elite split, state capacity, repression, access (McAdam, *Political Process*; Meyer). Opportunity is not “the people woke up.”
2. **Mobilizing structures** — churches, unions, campuses, parties, mosques, diasporas, Signal groups. Bodies are carried here (McCarthy & Zald, resource mobilization).
3. **Framing** — diagnosis, prognosis, motivation (Snow & Benford); Entman’s problem / cause / moral / remedy. This is **narrative literacy** as used in movements, not a posting menu.
4. **Repertoires** — the historically learned toolkit (barricade, boycott, occupation, hashtag, hunger strike) (Tilly).
5. **Diffusion and scale shift** — local → national → transnational (Tarrow; della Porta).
6. **Countermovement** — restoration plot with its own orgs (Meyer & Staggenborg).
7. **Outcome** — law, collapse, co-optation, schism, or nothing but a feed.

**Street equation (heuristic, not a law of nature):**

`Need(old institution) ≈ 1 / (new wage or law × exit option × frame × medium)`

When **exit gets cheap**, the old “you must” dies. The slogan **names** it. The medium **purifies** it (loyalty tests clip). The counterwave **sells yesterday**. Do not treat the slogan as the cause (Irina Dunn did not move the BLS).

**How to read a crest in this file**

- **Operational fact:** what actually became optional or compulsory (statute, wage, death, vote).
- **Mobilizing plot:** loyalty test (nuance = betrayal).
- **Carriers:** named org types, not a Grey Pope.
- **Counterwave:** yesterday as salvation, with its own orgs.
- **Mass splitter vs feed noise:** electorate / survey ≠ comment section.

**1st and 2nd wave feminism** did the **personhood denominator** (vote, property, wage, body, divorce, credit). **3rd** is expression / who counts after the win. **4th** is half file (MeToo settlements, courts) and half plot (men as a villain *class* = **misandry** when that is the frame; **misogyny** is the pair). Heavy lifting ≠ “3–4 never happened.”

## 2. Black freedom (US-weighted; rhymes in other empires)

Carriers: Black churches, NAACP, Urban League, SCLC, SNCC, CORE, later Black Panther Party, then Movement for Black Lives orgs + phones as evidence.

1. **Abolition → emancipation (~1830s–1865)**  
   Fact: chattel as property ends *on paper* (13th). War is the enforcement.  
   Plot: all men created equal.  
   Read: Du Bois, *Black Reconstruction*; Foner, *Reconstruction*.

2. **Reconstruction → nadir / Jim Crow (~1865–1950s)**  
   Counterwave: Redemption, terror, legal caste (Plessy).  
   Fact: vote and land stripped. Equal Protection as unused tool.

3. **Long civil rights (~1954–1968)**  
   Fact: *Brown*, CRA 1964, VRA 1965, Fair Housing 1968 — public accommodations, vote, formal equality.  
   Plot: King law-as-sermon vs post-1965 street.  
   McAdam: churches + Black colleges + NAACP chapters as **structures**; WWII + Great Migration + Northern vote as **opportunity**.  
   https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/P/bo5939918.html

4. **Black Power / nationalist (~late 1960s–70s)**  
   Fact: pride, police, “colonies at home,” Panthers’ survival programs + COINTELPRO.  
   Plot: dignity is not a permission slip.

5. **Colorblind / neoliberal counter-frame (~1980s–2010s)**  
   Plot: the race story is the hustle; merit is enough.  
   Fact: mass incarceration + urban policy as the **file** under the plot (Alexander, *The New Jim Crow* — pair with BJS tables, not as sole gospel).  
   BJS: https://bjs.ojp.gov/

6. **BLM / after (~2013–)**  
   Fact: phones as evidence; police as the file.  
   Plot: say the name vs “order / crime / fatherlessness.”  
   Live split: **crime and schools vs police and repair**. Not “does racism exist.”

Rhymes: Caribbean/African independence, South African ANC, Brazilian *movimento negro* — same scarce goods (safety, vote, land), different carriers.

## 3. Labor and the state

Carriers: craft unions, industrial unions, socialist/labor/communist parties, later NGOs and then **right-populist parties** speaking working-class vote.

1. **Craft / early unions (19th c.)** — hours, shop, strike.  
2. **Industrial / mass labor / socialist (~1890s–1940s)** — factory, eight-hour day, welfare, the party. Internationals as **orgs**, not a ghost king.  
3. **Social democracy / New Deal compact (~1930s–1970s)** — union density, pensions, mixed economy. State **absorbs** the crest.  
4. **Neoliberal smash (~1979–2008)** — capital mobility, union decline, “there is no alternative.” Thatcher/Reagan as **opportunity for capital**, not a slogan inventing globalization.  
5. **After the crash / populist labor (~2008–)** — Occupy; then right-populist working-class vote; gig.  

Live split: **size of the state, who captured it, prices** — not the Internationale.

Data: BLS https://www.bls.gov/ · ILOSTAT https://ilostat.ilo.org/ · union density OECD https://www.oecd.org/en/data.html

Read: Silver, *Forces of Labor*; Piketty, *Capital in the Twenty-First Century* (distribution file, not a movement history).

## 4. Environment / climate

Carriers: Sierra-type conservation orgs, scientists, EPA-like agencies, Green parties, then climate strikes + oil-state/consumer counter-orgs.

1. **Conservation / wilderness (~1890s–1950s)** — parks as cathedral (Muir/Pinchot split: preserve vs use).  
2. **Regulation / Silent Spring (~1960s–80s)** — Carson 1962; EPA 1970; air/water as public.  
   Carson: https://www.loc.gov/item/2021666185/ (LOC bibliographic; book itself still in copyright in many editions)  
3. **Climate science as politics (~1988–2010s)** — Hansen 1988; IPCC as the **file**.  
   IPCC: https://www.ipcc.ch/  
4. **Emergency vs hustle (~2010s–)** — Plot A: pause or the world ends. Plot B: green elites / prices / jets.  

Live splitter (e.g. Germany MIDEM): **measures vs cost**, high affect. Conservation is not this fight.  
MIDEM: https://forum-midem.de/ · IEA: https://www.iea.org/ · OWID energy: https://ourworldindata.org/energy

## 5. LGBTQ

Carriers: homophile orgs, Stonewall-era street, Gay Liberation Front, ACT UP, Human Rights Campaign–type law orgs, then intra-coalition splits.

1. **Decriminalize / medical (~1950s–70s; Stonewall 1969 as crest)** — state stops treating it as crime (uneven: Wolfenden UK 1957; *Lawrence* US 2003).  
2. **AIDS / ACT UP (~1980s–90s)** — death, FDA, quilt. Operational: drug pipeline + stigma as the file.  
3. **Marriage / inclusion (~1990s–2015)** — spouse as legal person (*Obergefell* 2015). **This was the operational win.**  
4. **Sex / gender inside the coalition (~2010s–)** — Live split: **same-sex rights vs gender identity as the file**. Not “did Stonewall happen.”  
   Counterwaves: sacral-family; also sex-based-rights vs trans-inclusive (both are **frames** with orgs — treat as contention, not a vibe).

Plot vs fact: marriage was operational. Current loyalty tests are the plot.

## 6. Feminism (one family, not the spine)

Carriers: suffrage associations, WCTU (overlap temperance), NOW, consciousness-raising groups, campuses, #MeToo as **connective action** (Bennett & Segerberg) more than a single SMO.

1. **First (~1848–1920)** — Seneca Falls; vote, property, own name — persons, not wards. **Denominator.**  
2. **Second (~1960s–80s)** — work, body, Pill, no-fault, credit (US ECOA 1974), Title IX. Survival-need of a husband **falls**.  
   Slogan on this crest (**not the cause**): Irina Dunn, Sydney ~1970, “A woman needs a man like a fish needs a bicycle” (Steinem popularized). Dunn = Patricia Irene “Irina” Dunn (b. 17 Mar 1948, Shanghai; Sydney Uni; Australian Senator NSW 1988–90). Senate bio: https://biography.senate.gov.au/dunn-patricia-irene/ · WP: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina_Dunn  
   BLS women in labor force: https://www.bls.gov/opub/reports/womens-databook/  
3. **Third (~1990s–2000s)** — choice, pop, intersectionality (Crenshaw), queer. **Expression / who counts after the win.** Thin new pyramids of law; thick critique of who 2nd meant.  
4. **Fourth (~2012–now)** — #MeToo: NDAs, courts, HR, settlements = **needed half** (file). Feed “men as villain class” = **misandry** when that is the frame = **ridiculous half**. Trans as inside split overlaps LGBTQ-4.

Counter: tradwife / sacral-family / anti-girlboss / manosphere — **not** a clean 5th wave. Content SKU ≠ electorate.

Fact: own wage. Plot: desire = betrayal.

## 7. Nation, empire, borders (cycle, not four feminist-style waves)

Repeats: **build the people → expand → aftershock in the metropole → restriction.**

- 19th c. nationalism / self-determination (Anderson; Gellner; Hobsbawm)
- Decolonization (~1945–1975): flags, FLN, Viet Minh, ANC, Bandung 1955
- Guest worker / post-imperial immigration
- Restriction / populist border (~2010s–): who is the people

Twin movies: **hospitality vs order** — same scarce goods (belonging, wages, safety).  
Live splitter in UK/DE more than US family-law (NatCen).

## 8. Religious-political movements (numbered because we owed them)

Not “God as weather.” These **carry bodies** like unions.

1. **Early modern / Reformation template** — exit the old church; new churches police (print as medium).  
2. **Great Awakenings / evangelical politics** — US Moral Majority, party capture. Carriers: churches, parachurch, GOP coalition.  
3. **Political Islam** — Brotherhood (1928), Iranian Revolution 1979, FIS, AKP, then jihadi *factions* (do not collapse Roy’s “failure of political Islam” into ISIS-as-the-whole). Kepel/Roy.  
4. **Hindu nationalism** — RSS (1925), BJP as electoral carrier (Jaffrelot).  
5. **Other national-religious** — Buddhist nationalism (Myanmar), Jewish religious Zionism as a **faction** inside a broader national movement, etc. Local names.  

Live: often **who is the people + who certifies law (sharia, halakha, dharma, biblical)**.  
Plot leak: “they” as one faith. Strict: **orgs + votes + courts**.

## 9. Anti-colonial and Global South (not a footnote)

Carriers: parties, armies, unions, churches, students; then **post-independence** states that become the new wall.

1. **High imperialism + early protonationalism (19th c.–WWI)**  
2. **Wilson / Lenin moment + mandates**  
3. **1945–1975 independence wave** — India 1947, China 1949 as revolution-state, Ghana 1957, Algeria 1962, Portuguese Africa 1970s, Vietnam, etc.  
4. **Post-colony fights** — who is the people *inside* the flag (ethnic, religious, land).  
5. **Structural adjustment / next contention** — IMF as constraint; then populist or religious-political carriers.

Read: Fanon; Prashad as survey; country monographs over “the Global South” as one actor.

## 14. Who carries (entities) — not who “engineers history”

Nobody is “responsible” for cycles the way Facebook is responsible for Like.

| Slot | Entities |
|---|---|
| Material | wages, law, guns, housing, child survival |
| Structures | churches, unions, parties, campuses, mosques, diasporas |
| Framers | intellectuals, preachers, Dunn-type slogans |
| State | target, represser, sponsor, absorber |
| Money | dues, churches, foundations (990s), parties — fuel, not Grey Pope |
| Medium | pulpit, paper, TV, ranker — **selects** this crest |

Attention **engineers** (Parker, Chamath, Sandberg, Pearlman/Rosenstein, Chaslot, Haugen, Harris) built **watch-time**. They did not schedule Reconstruction. **Pepe Orsini / Grey Pope** is a hidden-hand plot on a real medieval family name — not a carrier.
