# Demographic waves (cohort / age-structure)

**Not cycles of contention. Not a tweet. Not Strauss–Howe / Fourth Turning. Not Nisaba.**

**Subject:** *cohort and age-structure*. Formal demography: mortality, fertility, migration, and the **age pyramid** they leave. Youth bulge, boom/echo, below-replacement, aging. Coale, Notestein, Caldwell, Lee, Goldin, Hajnal — not a podcast calendar.

**Job of this file:** how many people, what age, whether they **pair, house, and care**. Movements named it; they did not invent the **denominator**. Border fights belong in the contention file.

**Plot leak:** “feminism ended the family” or “immigrants replaced the nation” as the **whole equation**.

**Strict:** TFR fell with child survival, women’s wages, contraception, housing, phones. Goldin-type operational (not a clip): women’s agency is real; male “dad vs dud” commitment signal is a **bottleneck for births among the educated**. Not a villain class.

A dissertation is **one named crest + one periodization fight + one historical file + one WPP/census table + one method**. Date live pulls `YYYY-MM-DD`. Don’t renumber the baby boom because TikTok discovered bread. Skip Fourth Turning as a calendar. Teen tradwife is a **content SKU**, not a census.

## 0. Theory and unit of analysis

**Mechanism stack (this machine):** mortality fall (esp. child) → more surviving 15–29s (youth bulge) → later, fertility fall (demographic transition) → boom/echo if a catch-up birth pulse → TFR < replacement → aging / dependency → migration as the **residual** that rich + old + low-TFR states pull. Pairing and housing sit **between** TFR and “values.” Phones track partnership collapse; AI companions are a **forming plot** on top of that fact (medium file).

**Unit of analysis for every numbered crest below** (not Lasswell acts. This lock is for **bodies and rates**):

1. **Count** — how many, by age and sex
2. **Rates** — TFR, NRR, e0, nuptiality, age at first birth, childlessness
3. **Pairing / housing / care** — household formation, price-to-income, unpaid care
4. **Who is in paid work** — BLS/ILO; female LFP (Goldin)
5. **Date** — as-of of the pyramid + cohort vs period TFR + provisional vs final
6. **Where** — Hajnal line, East Asia, SSA, US — **not** a global tweet
7. **Receipt** — **history** (vital registration, parish, census) **and** **table** (WPP, OWID, ACS). Pew without Wrigley is a dashboard. Wrigley without housing is an incomplete transition.
8. **Falsifier** — “values only” / “policy only” / “immigrants are the whole TFR”
9. **Grain** — a viral tradwife ≠ a cohort; period TFR ≠ completed cohort fertility
10. **Institutions** — statistical office, family law, housing finance, pension. Not an SMO.

If 1–5 will not fill, it is a story. If 1–5 fill and 7–8 will not, it is a **clean story** (a sermon about the family).

## 1. Mortality fall → youth bulge (development)

1. **Count:** lots of 15–29s **because children stopped dying**, not because “they breed.”
2. **Rates:** IMR/U5MR down; TFR still high for a lag.
3. **Pairing/housing/care:** rural→urban first; jobs may not exist yet.
4. **Paid work:** can be labor **or** street. Street = contention file (Urdal is the bridge paper).
5. **Date:** historically NW Europe 19th c.; 20th c. rest of world in waves. As-of is **country**.
6. **Where:** SSA and parts of WANA still live here in WPP; Europe does not.
7. **Receipt:** *History* — McKeown fight (medicine vs nutrition vs public health) · Preston curve · Wrigley. *Table* — WPP age 15–29 share · OWID child mortality https://ourworldindata.org/child-mortality
8. **Falsifier:** “youth bulge causes revolution” as destiny. It **can** feed contention; it is not a law.
9. **Grain:** a protest year ≠ a bulge. A bulge is a **pyramid**.
10. **Institutions:** public health, primary school, labor market.

**Method owed:** 15–29 share vs job creation. Comparison: East Asia absorbed a bulge into **Perez** manufacturing; others did not.

## 2. Baby boom → echo (West, post-1945)

1. **Count:** large 1946–64-ish cohorts (dates vary by country), then echo.
2. **Rates:** period TFR up, then down. Completed fertility of boom women is the **honest** cell.
3. **Pairing/housing/care:** schools, then housing, then pensions — the same cohort **ages through** institutions.
4. **Paid work:** male breadwinner then dual earn; Goldin’s quiet revolution is **this** aging, not TikTok.
5. **Date:** post-1945 West. Don’t globalize. Don’t renumber because of bread.
6. **Where:** US, Canada, ANZ, much of Europe — not China (China’s “boom” is a **policy** pyramid).
7. **Receipt:** *History* — Easterlin · Hajnal already broken · *Table* — Census/ONS cohort size · SSA (US) · pension dependency later.
8. **Falsifier:** Fourth Turning “hero generation” calendar. If the mechanism isn’t **count → school → house → pension**, you left demography.
9. **Grain:** 1946 is a year. The boom is a **cohort**.
10. **Institutions:** GI Bill-class housing, comprehensive school, PAYG pension.

**Method owed:** follow **one** boom cohort through three institutions. Comparison: Japan’s smaller boom, faster aging.

## 3. Fertility below replacement (most rich + E. Asia; spreading)

1. **Count:** fewer births. **Fewer couples**, not only fewer kids per couple (nuptiality).
2. **Rates:** TFR < 2.1; watch **cohort** fertility and childlessness. Tempo can fake a crash.
3. **Pairing/housing/care:** housing cost, dual-career mismatch, phones as **time/attention** (medium-adjacent). Goldin: educated women’s agency + men’s commitment signal.
4. **Paid work:** female LFP high where TFR can be **either** higher (Nordics, with care) or crushed (E. Asia overtime). Care policy is a **cell**.
5. **Date:** Western Europe from 1970s; E. Asia later and lower; spreading. As-of WPP.
6. **Where:** Italy/Spain/Korea/China are not interchangeable. Name the country.
7. **Receipt:** *History* — Lesthaeghe (SDT — fight it) · Goldin · Bongaarts. *Table* — OWID TFR · HFD · Pew housing (below).
8. **Falsifier:** “feminism ended the family” as the whole equation. If child survival, wages, contraception, housing, and pairing are empty, the row is a plot leak.
9. **Grain:** a tradwife clip ≠ TFR. Period TFR 1.3 can rebound in cohort (tempo).
10. **Institutions:** housing finance, childcare, family law, platforms (pairing markets).

**Live (date the pull).** Pew, 24 Jun 2026, ACS/CPS analysis + ATP 4–17 May 2026: US households headed **under 40**, median home price-to-income **~2.9 (2019) → ~3.5 (2024)**; **89%** of under-40s say buying is harder than for parents (87% of all adults). https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/06/24/buying-a-home-has-gotten-harder-for-young-adults-in-most-us-metro-areas/ — **housing constraint**, not a fertility census. Pair with NCHS births.

## 4. Aging / dependency

1. **Count:** more 65+; inverted pyramid.
2. **Rates:** old-age dependency; health-span vs life-span.
3. **Pairing/housing/care:** **care is the scarce good** — unpaid family, then migrant labor, then fiscal.
4. **Paid work:** retirement ages, disability, who is still in the labor force (BLS/ILO).
5. **Date:** Japan first; S. Europe; China “old before rich” as a **fight** (Arrighi-adjacent, still a pyramid).
6. **Where:** name the country. SSA is not here yet.
7. **Receipt:** *History* — pension politics (contention **rides** this) · *Table* — WPP dependency · OECD pensions · NCHS/ONS deaths.
8. **Falsifier:** “we’ll grow our way out” without a labor or migration residual. Also: robots as a **Perez** hope — not a census.
9. **Grain:** one fiscal year ≠ a pyramid.
10. **Institutions:** social security, NHS-class systems, long-term care, visa-for-care.

**Method owed:** workers per retiree **and** hours of care. Comparison: Japan vs US (migration residual).

## 5. Migration as the residual

1. **Count:** net migration vs natural increase. Rich + old + low TFR → inflows **unless** closed.
2. **Rates:** net migr. per 1,000; origin mix; age of migrants (usually younger — it **is** a pyramid patch).
3. **Pairing/housing/care:** housing politics; care workforce.
4. **Paid work:** occupation tables, not a people-noun.
5. **Date:** as-of of the statistical office. A boat week is an **event** (contention).
6. **Where:** Gulf (labor without citizenship) vs US/EU (partial political membership) — **different institutions**.
7. **Receipt:** *History* — Hatton & Williamson · MPI · UNHCR (refugees are a **legal** subset). *Table* — WPP net migration · Census/ACS · MPI https://www.migrationpolicy.org/ · UNHCR https://www.unhcr.org/refugee-statistics
8. **Falsifier:** “immigrants replaced the nation” as the whole equation. If TFR, age, and housing are empty, you are in plot leak. The **movie** of “who is the people” = **contention map**, not this one.
9. **Grain:** a border year ≠ a demographic regime.
10. **Institutions:** visa class, asylum law, citizenship. Street = contention.

**Method owed:** residual accounting (WPP). Comparison: Canada points vs Italy geography vs Gulf kafala.

## Pocket terms

- **Replacement** — ~2.1 in low-mortality populations
- **Dependency** — retirees (and children) per worker
- **Residual** — migration that patches a pyramid
- **Proximate determinants** — Bongaarts
- **Goldin cell** — agency + commitment bottleneck
