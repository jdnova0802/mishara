# Religion as Institution Map

Descriptive / institutional reference. Governance, legal status, money, land, and authority — **not** belief or practice. Primary sources first. Wikipedia is a finding aid. OPEN rather than invented. Pull date **2026-11-14**. Companion to the Peoples & Ethnicities Atlas (`docs/study/PEOPLES_ETHNICITIES_ATLAS.md`) but **not** the same 11-field grid.

This is not a theology paper and not a people-count. Where two bodies claim the same flock, land, or fatwa (a legal opinion, binding only if a named state so provides), **name both machines**. Do not mash the Holy See’s consolidated accounts with the Istituto per le Opere di Religione (IOR, “Vatican Bank”). Do not mash a temple trust’s board-approved budget with a state’s endowment department. Do not treat a 13F equity portfolio as a church’s total assets.

---

## How to read an entry

Every entry uses the same fixed 11-field template. Field labels stay in this form throughout. In any **Falsifier** line, “cell N” means field N on this list (so “cell 5” is Financial assets + revenue).

1. **Institution name** — self-designation vs. external/common name
2. **Governance structure** — who holds final authority, how they are selected, term/succession rules
3. **Legal status** — sovereign entity, registered nonprofit, state religion, tax treatment — and how this varies by country
4. **Geographic/jurisdictional reach** — where authority is actually recognized or enforced, vs. where it is nominal
5. **Financial assets + revenue** — audited or officially reported figures, dated and sourced; OPEN where undisclosed
6. **Defining institutional rupture** — schism, reformation, nationalization, state suppression — with a real date
7. **Internal jurisdictional/denominational subdivisions** — the real distinct bodies inside an apparent single institution
8. **Common misconception corrected** — the specific false assumption about how this institution works
9. **Receipt** — the actual primary source
10. **Falsifier** — the one fact that would prove the entry wrong
11. **OPEN** — anything undisclosed or unverifiable, named honestly

### Holy See / Vatican City State / IOR (three legal persons)

1. **Institution name:** Self: *Sancta Sedes* / Apostolic See (Holy See); *Stato della Città del Vaticano* (Vatican City State); *Istituto per le Opere di Religione* (IOR). External: “the Vatican,” “Vatican Bank.” Those three names are **not** one corporation.
2. **Governance:** The Roman Pontiff has “full, supreme and universal power over the Church” and may always exercise it (Second Vatican Council, *Lumen Gentium* 22, 21 November 1964, restating Vatican I). He is elected by the College of Cardinals under *Universi Dominici Gregis* (the 1996 papal-election constitution, John Paul II, 22 February 1996, as later amended). Curial dicasteries (departments of the papal government) are reorganized by *Praedicate Evangelium* (Francis, 19 March 2022). Vatican City State: the Pope is sovereign; day-to-day civil administration is the Governatorate. IOR: Board of Superintendence plus a Commission of Cardinals; supervised by ASIF (Autorità di Supervisione e Informazione Finanziaria, the Holy See/VCS financial supervisor).
3. **Legal status:** Holy See — subject of international law that concludes treaties and accredits diplomats (older than 1929). Vatican City State — territorial state created by the Lateran Treaty (Holy See–Italy, **11 February 1929**, ratifications exchanged **7 June 1929**). Art. 3: Italy recognizes the Holy See’s “exclusive and absolute power and jurisdiction” over the Vatican as constituted. Art. 26: Italy recognizes Vatican City State under the sovereignty of the Supreme Pontiff; the Holy See declares the “Roman Question” settled. IOR — legal person **no. 1** in the Governatorate register; ASIF authorization no. 1 of **10 July 2015** to carry out financial activities in VCS. Dioceses, religious institutes, and national bishops’ conferences are **other** legal persons (in the US, typically Internal Revenue Code §501(c)(3) tax-exempt churches). Do not export VCS sovereignty onto a parish corporation in Chicago.
4. **Reach:** Holy See: diplomatic relations and canonical claims worldwide; enforcement of canon law (the Church’s internal legal code) depends on civil reception. VCS: ~44 hectares, plus Lateran Treaty extraterritorial properties in Italy (Art. 13: Lateran, Santa Maria Maggiore, St Paul Outside the Walls). IOR: professional financial activity **in Vatican City State**, not a universal retail bank.
5. **Finance — three machines, not added:**
   - **Secretariat for the Economy, Holy See Consolidated Financial Statements 2024** (published 25 November 2025): **assets €6,326.7 million**; **net equity €4,277.0 million**; operating income **€1,230.7 million**; operating expenses **€1,275.2 million**; operating result **€(44.4) million**; surplus **€1.6 million** (2023 deficit €51.2 million). Ex-hospitals operating income **€475.4 million**. Governatorate contribution **€20.0 million**. External donations **€237.6 million**. https://www.spe.va/content/dam/spe/documenti/2024_Holy_See_Financial_Statements.pdf ; Holy See Press Office 25 Nov 2025 https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/info/2025/11/26/251126a.html
   - **IOR Annual Report 2024** (International Accounting Standards / International Financial Reporting Standards as adopted by ASIF; audited by Forvis Mazars S.p.A.): **total assets €2,437,423,056** (2023: €2,431,820,477); **due to customers €1,676,740,185**; capital **€300,000,000**; profit **€32,759,748**; proposed dividend to the Commission of Cardinals **€13.8 million**. https://www.ior.va/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/IOR_Annual_Report_2024.pdf
   - Do **not** add €6.327 billion + €2.437 billion. The SPE pack is Holy See entities; IOR is a separately licensed legal person. Global Catholic parish/diocesan real estate is **not** in either table.
6. **Rupture:** **20 September 1870** (Italian capture of Rome) and the prisoner-in-the-Vatican standoff; closed by **11 February 1929** Lateran Treaty. Financial: 2010s–2020s ASIF/IOR reform as a dated compliance rupture, not a new state.
7. **Subdivisions:** Holy See (curia + diplomatic apparatus) ≠ VCS (tiny civil state) ≠ IOR (bank) ≠ APSA (Administration of the Patrimony of the Apostolic See) ≠ each diocese. Eastern Catholic *sui iuris* churches (self-governing ritual churches in communion with Rome) are **not** Latin-rite copies.
8. **Misconception:** “The Vatican Bank’s balance sheet is the Catholic Church’s wealth.” It is a deposit-taking institute with €1.68 billion due to customers. “The Pope’s €6.3 billion is all cathedrals.” SPE assets are a Holy See consolidation, not St Peter’s plus every diocese.
9. **Receipt:** Lateran Treaty English, Vatican City State: https://www.vaticanstate.va/phocadownload/laws-decrees/LateranTreaty.pdf ; Italian authentic: https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/secretariat_state/archivio/documents/rc_seg-st_19290211_patti-lateranensi_it.html ; *Lumen Gentium* 22 https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html ; SPE 2024 and IOR 2024 (URLs above). vaticanstate.va on Holy See vs State: https://vaticanstate.va/en/state-and-government/general-informations.html
10. **Falsifier:** If SPE 2024 total assets ≠ €6,326.7 million, or IOR 2024 total assets ≠ €2,437,423,056, cell 5 dies. If the Lateran Treaty did not create a territorial state, cell 3 dies.
11. **OPEN:** APSA standalone 2024 integer if not in the SPE pack as a named line; Peter’s Pence (the annual papal collection) as a separate collection total this pass; diocesan real-estate universe.

### Eastern Orthodox patriarchates (not one corporation)

1. **Institution name:** Self: *Orthodox Catholic Church* as communion of autocephalous churches. External: “the Orthodox Church,” “Greek Orthodox,” “Russian Church” as if one HQ. There is **no** Orthodox equivalent of the Holy See.
2. **Governance:** Autocephaly (self-headed local church with its own primate and synod). Constantinople claims canonical privileges as Ecumenical Patriarchate, including a disputed competence over the “diaspora” and the right to grant autocephaly. Moscow treats primacy as *primus inter pares* (first among equals) and rejects “first without equal.” Bishops are elected by synods under each church’s statute. **Do not referee the ecclesiology as a census.**
3. **Legal status:** Varies by state: Turkey (Rum Patrikliği in Istanbul as a Turkish-recognized minority institution, **not** a Vatican-style state); Russian Federation (Moscow Patriarchate as a registered religious organization); Ukraine (two competing registered churches after 2018–19); Greece (Church of Greece, constitutional); United States (multiple jurisdictions as 501(c)(3)s). No Orthodox UN seat.
4. **Reach — contested, named:**
   - **Ecumenical Patriarchate tomos (a patriarchal decree granting autocephaly), 6 January 2019:** declares the Orthodox Church within the State of Ukraine “canonically autocephalous, independent and self-administered”; primate title “Metropolitan of Kyiv and all Ukraine”; diaspora remaining with Constantinople. https://ec-patr.org/en/06/01/15/32/patriarchal-and-synodal-tomos-for-the-bestowal-of-the-ecclesiastical-status-of-autocephaly-to-the-orthodox-church-in-ukraine/
   - **Moscow Patriarchate:** does **not** recognize that tomos; DECR (Department for External Church Relations) English statement of the Holy Synod that it is “impossible to continue the Eucharistic communion with the Patriarchate of Constantinople” until those decisions are abandoned. https://mospat.ru/en/news/47058/pdf/ (session commonly dated **15 October 2018**, Minsk; treat a later English “2019” header, if present, as reprint variance, not a second break).
   - Other autocephalous churches have **split on recognition** of the Orthodox Church of Ukraine (OCU, the body named in the 2019 tomos). That split is the jurisdiction map. Do not print a single “Orthodox membership” as if one payroll.
5. **Finance:** No pan-Orthodox audited consolidation. Moscow Patriarchate, Ecumenical Patriarchate, and OCU publish **uneven** reports. **OPEN** as a communion total. Do not use a Wikipedia diocese-count × guessed budget.
6. **Rupture:** **1054** as the conventional East–West dating (mutual excommunications; communion failure is older and messier). Modern: **2018–19** Ukraine autocephaly crisis as the live jurisdictional rupture.
7. **Subdivisions:** The autocephalous churches (Constantinople, Alexandria, Antioch, Jerusalem, Moscow, Serbia, Romania, Bulgaria, Georgia, Cyprus, Greece, Poland, Albania, Czech Lands and Slovakia, plus those whose autocephaly is not pan-Orthodox-received — OCU, the Orthodox Church in America (OCA), etc.)). Ukraine: **OCU** (tomos) vs **Ukrainian Orthodox Church** still claimed by/aligned with Moscow’s canonical narrative (UOC statute changes after 2022 are a **separate legal fight** — OPEN as a single registration integer this pass).
8. **Misconception:** “The Patriarch of Constantinople is the Orthodox pope.” The tomos itself has to *recommend* that other churches commemorate Ukraine. “Moscow runs all Slavs.” Serbia, Bulgaria, and the OCU disagree in different directions.
9. **Receipt:** Tomos (URL above); Moscow synod PDF (URL above); each church’s statute for internal election rules.
10. **Falsifier:** If a pan-Orthodox council with Moscow *and* Constantinople seated had recognized a single Ukrainian autocephalous church, the “two machines” clause in cell 4 would need a rewrite. If the 2019 tomos text did not restrict Ukraine from the diaspora, cell 4’s Constantinople claim dies.
11. **OPEN:** 2024/25 audited accounts for Moscow, Constantinople, and OCU; UOC legal status after Ukrainian statutory changes.

### Major Protestant structures (three governance models, not one “Protestant Church”)

1. **Institution name:** There is no Protestant Holy See. This entry is a **contrast set:** (A) Southern Baptist Convention (SBC); (B) Anglican Communion; (C) independent congregationalism as a model.
2. **Governance:**
   - **SBC:** “While independent and sovereign in its own sphere, the Convention does not claim and will never attempt to exercise any authority over any other Baptist body, whether church, auxiliary organizations, associations, or convention” (SBC Constitution Art. IV). Messengers from cooperating churches meet annually; entities (International Mission Board (IMB), North American Mission Board (NAMB), seminaries, GuideStone, Ethics and Religious Liberty Commission (ERLC), Executive Committee) are **separate corporations** with Convention-elected trustees. President: annual election, two-year limit (Art. V). https://www.sbc.net/about/what-we-do/legal-documentation/constitution/
   - **Anglican Communion:** Four **Instruments of Communion** (not a curia): Archbishop of Canterbury (focus of unity, not a patriarch with ordinary jurisdiction over other provinces); Lambeth Conference; Primates’ Meeting; Anglican Consultative Council (ACC). https://www.anglicancommunion.org/instruments-of-communion/ Each member church is autocephalous in civil and canonical fact (Church of England is established in law in England; The Episcopal Church is a US 501(c)(3) / diocesan corporations). GAFCON (Global Anglican Future Conference) and other extra-instrument alignments are **competing communion projects** — name them; do not pick a winner.
   - **Congregationalism:** Final earthly authority sits in the gathered local church (members/elders). Associations exist for fellowship and mission, not as courts of appeal unless a church *consents*. No instrument can un-pastor a congregation from London or Nashville.
3. **Legal status:** SBC entities: US nonprofit corporations, church-plan / §501(c)(3). Church of England: established church, ecclesiastical law as part of English law, Church Commissioners as a statutory corporation. Other Anglican provinces: national nonprofit/established hybrids. Independent congregations: whatever the local statute requires (unincorporated association, 501(c)(3), UK community interest company (CIC), etc.).
4. **Reach:** SBC: US + territories by constitution; IMB operates abroad as an SBC *entity*, not as a second convention with power over foreign Baptist unions. Anglican Communion: global provinces in communion *as recognized by the instruments* — GAFCON provinces may reject Canterbury’s primacy of honour. Congregationalism: as wide as each congregation’s property deed.
5. **Finance — do not invent a Protestant total:**
   - **SBC Cooperative Program (CP, the voluntary pooled funding stream) Allocation Budget 2024–2025**, adopted Indianapolis 11–12 June 2024: **$190,250,000**. Executive Committee / SBC operating budget **$10,235,000**. EC statements of activities year ended 30 Sept 2023: cash received from CP and designated gifts **$392,580,280**. 2024 SBC Annual https://www.sbc.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2024-SBC-Annual.pdf
   - **GuideStone Financial Resources** (SBC entity; **does not receive CP dollars** per SBC ministry-report framing): audited consolidated statements 31 Dec 2024 — fair-value **investments $17,789 million** (2023: $15,739 million in the comparative fair-value table). Unrestricted reserves **$168 million** at 31 Dec 2024. These are **participant/plan assets plus enterprise reserves**, not an SBC endowment. https://www.sbc.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/2024-GSFR-Consolidated-Financial-Statements-Final.pdf
   - Church Commissioners for England / other Anglican national accounts: **OPEN** as integers this pass (UK Charity Commission / Commissioners annual report exists; not copied here).
   - Independent congregationalism: **no** central treasury.
6. **Rupture:** SBC: **8 May 1845** Augusta organizing (slavery-era split from northern Baptists is the dated origin). Anglican: **1534** Act of Supremacy as the English state rupture; **1998–2020s** instruments-vs-GAFCON as the live communion rupture. Congregational: 17th-c. gathered-church settlements (specific statutes vary).
7. **Subdivisions:** SBC churches ≠ state conventions ≠ national entities. Anglican: 40-plus provinces, extra-provincial dioceses, GAFCON, Anglican Church in North America (ACNA, not an instrument member). Congregational: each church; some in the National Association of Congregational Christian Churches (NACCC) or similar — optional, not a pope.
8. **Misconception:** “Southern Baptists have a Vatican in Nashville.” Art. IV forbids it; the Executive Committee is a fiduciary for *Convention* affairs, not a bishop. “The Archbishop of Canterbury can fire an American bishop.” He cannot. “Protestantism has no money.” GuideStone’s $17.8 billion is plan assets with a named trustee, not a counterexample that creates a Protestant Holy See.
9. **Receipt:** SBC Constitution/Annual (URLs above); Anglican Instruments page; GuideStone 2024 audited PDF.
10. **Falsifier:** If SBC Constitution Art. IV were repealed and a convention court could depose local pastors, model A dies. If an Instrument issued binding ordinary jurisdiction over all provinces, model B dies. If GuideStone 2024 total investments ≠ $17,789 million as tabulated, the finance cell dies.
11. **OPEN:** Church Commissioners 2024 total return/assets; number of SBC cooperating churches as of the Annual Church Profile (ACP) 2024 if not copied from the Annual this pass.

### Sunni Islamic religious authority (no caliphate, several state machines)

1. **Institution name:** There is no single Sunni “church.” Named machines here: **Al-Azhar** (Egypt); **Dar al-Ifta al-Misriyyah** (Egypt’s official fatwa office); **Saudi Council of Senior Scholars** (*Hayʾat Kibār al-ʿUlamāʾ*) and the **Permanent Committee**; national ministries of *awqāf* (religious endowments)/Islamic affairs; **Majelis Ulama Indonesia** (MUI, Indonesia’s ulama council) as a large non-Egyptian/Saudi fatwa body. External: “the Islamic Pope,” “Al-Azhar runs Sunnis.”
2. **Governance:**
   - **Al-Azhar:** Egyptian Law **103 of 1961** (10 July 1961) reconstitutes Al-Azhar as the “great Islamic scientific body,” seat Cairo, **attached to the Presidency**; the Shaykh al-Azhar is Grand Imam (*al-imām al-akbar*) with presidency over Azhar’s Islamic studies. Bodies include Supreme Council of Al-Azhar, Islamic Research Academy, Azhar University, institutes. https://www.mksegypt.org/ar/laws/24426
   - **2014 Constitution Art. 7** (as amended 2019): Al-Azhar is an **independent** Islamic scientific institution, main reference for religious sciences and Islamic affairs; State provides sufficient funding; Grand Sheikh is **independent and may not be dismissed**; appointment method from the **Council of Senior Scholars** is by law. Unofficial English: https://www.elections.eg/images/pdfs/laws/Constitution_2014-En.pdf
   - **Saudi Council of Senior Scholars:** Royal Decree **A/137** of **8/7/1391 AH** to opine on matters referred by the ruler and recommend general religious rulings. Recomposed by royal order (e.g. A/83 of 29/2/1446 AH). Official: https://alifta.gov.sa/senior-scholars-council The Grand Mufti chairs; this is a **Saudi state organ**, not a vote of world Sunnis.
   - **Fatwa:** Four Egyptian issuers are often listed (Council of Senior Scholars, Islamic Research Academy, Dar al-Ifta, Ministry of Awqaf fatwa directorate) — **four machines**. A fatwa is binding only if a named state so legislates.
3. **Legal status:** Al-Azhar: Egyptian public institution with constitutional independence claims **and** a state budget. Saudi ʿulamāʾ (religious scholars): royal appointments. MUI: Indonesian civil-society/state-adjacent council, not a caliphate. 1924 abolition of the Ottoman caliphate is the **absence** that makes this entry necessary.
4. **Reach:** Al-Azhar’s graduates and *daʿwa* (religious outreach) are global; its **enforceable** writ is Egyptian. Saudi *fatāwā* bind in the Kingdom where the state so provides. A Malaysian *mufti* (state-appointed jurist who issues fatwas) or Nigerian *hisbah* board (a statutory religious-enforcement body in some states) is **another** statute. Do not treat a Cairo fatwa as Lagos law.
5. **Finance:** Al-Azhar’s state allocation is in the Egyptian budget — **OPEN** the named-year integer this pass (Art. 7 requires “sufficient” funding; that is not a figure). Saudi Ministry of Islamic Affairs / Council: **OPEN**. MUI: **OPEN**. Do not print “Al-Azhar’s $X billion endowment” from a magazine.
6. **Rupture:** **3 March 1924** (abolition of the caliphate). Egypt: **Law 103/1961** (Nasserite reorganization). Constitution 2014 Art. 7 as the modern independence clause. Saudi: **1391 AH** decree creating the Council.
7. **Subdivisions:** *Madhhab* (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafiʿi, Hanbali — the four Sunni legal schools) is a **jurisprudential** map, not a hierarchy. State muftiates vs independent *madrasas* (religious schools) vs Sufi *ṭarīqa*s (orders). Deobandi/Barelvi, Salafi networks, and Ikhwan-descended movements are **not** Al-Azhar departments.
8. **Misconception:** “Sunnis have a Vatican in Cairo or Riyadh.” Law 103 and the Saudi decree are **national**. “A fatwa is a global command.” Only if a named state so legislates.
9. **Receipt:** Law 103/1961; Egypt Constitution Art. 7; Saudi alifta.gov.sa Council page.
10. **Falsifier:** If a reconstituted caliphate with recognized Sunni jurisdiction existed, the frame dies. If Art. 7 did not make the Grand Sheikh non-dismissible, cell 2’s constitutional clause dies.
11. **OPEN:** Al-Azhar budget line in the latest Egyptian state budget; Dar al-Ifta 2007 independence statute integer; MUI legal basis text as fetched Indonesian statute.

### Shia Islamic religious authority (*marjaʿiyya* vs *wilāyat al-faqīh*)

1. **Institution name:** Self: *marjaʿ al-taqlīd* (source of emulation) as a scholarly office; *ḥawza* (seminary) of Najaf and Qom as campuses. External: “the Ayatollah,” “Iran’s pope,” collapsing **Sistani’s office** with **Iran’s Leader**. *Wilāyat al-faqīh* (guardianship of the jurist) is a **state doctrine**, not a synonym for *marjaʿiyya*.
2. **Governance:**
   - **Marjaʿiyya:** A *mujtahid* (a jurist qualified to issue independent legal rulings) whom lay Twelver Shia *choose* to emulate in *fiqh* (Islamic jurisprudence). Multiple living *marājiʿ* can coexist. Offices (*bayt*) collect *khums* (a religious levy on specified income paid to the marja’s office), answer *istiftāʾ* (written legal questions), pay stipends. No synod elects “the” marja. Sistani office (Najaf): official English describes Board of Istifta, stipends, preachers — https://www.sistani.org/english/data/8/ Sistani on *wilāyat al-faqīh*: *wilāya* in *umūr ḥisbiyya* (limited trusteeship over unattended public duties) for any fully qualified jurist; for public order affairs, further conditions including acceptance by the generality of believers (Persian Q&A https://www.sistani.org/persian/qa/01083/).
   - **Iran:** Constitution Art. 5: during the Occultation (the Twelfth Imam’s hidden absence), *wilāya* and leadership devolve on a just, pious *faqīh* (jurist) per Art. 107. Art. 107: Assembly of Experts designates the Leader after Khomeini. Guardian Council official English: https://www.shora-gc.ir/en/news/87/constitution-of-the-islamic-republic-of-iran-full-text
3. **Legal status:** Najaf *marjaʿ* offices operate under Iraqi civil conditions (not a Vatican). Iran’s Leader is a **constitutional organ** with command authority over the state. Qom *ḥawza* is inside the Islamic Republic’s legal space and is **not** Najaf.
4. **Reach:** *Taqlīd* (emulation of a living marja in law) follows the believer, not the passport: a Lebanese or Pakistani may emulate Sistani. Iranian state authority follows Iranian public law. Do not treat a Leader decree as a Najaf *fatwā*, or the reverse.
5. **Finance:** *Khums* and *ṣaḥm al-imām* (the Imam’s share of khums, administered by the marja) are the marja’s fiscal machine — **not audited as a public 10-K**. **OPEN** as Najaf/Qom totals. Iran’s Leader does not publish a personal audited *khums* statement comparable to SPE 2024. Do not use “Sistani’s $X billion” from secondary estimates.
6. **Rupture:** **1979** Islamic Republic / constitution (referendum 29–30 March 1979) as the state-*faqīh* rupture. Najaf’s *marjaʿiyya* predates it and **did not dissolve**.
7. **Subdivisions:** Najaf vs Qom vs Karbala vs Lebanese/Iraqi other *marājiʿ*. Usuli vs Akhbari (historical). Ismaili and Zaydi Shia are **not** this Twelver map (do not pad).
8. **Misconception:** “Khamenei is the Shia pope, Sistani his deputy” (or the reverse). Cell 2’s two constitutions — one scholarly-voluntary, one state-mandatory — are the correction.
9. **Receipt:** Iran Constitution Arts. 5, 107 (shora-gc.ir); Sistani office and Q&A URLs above.
10. **Falsifier:** If Sistani’s published *wilāya* answer were identical to Art. 5’s general *wilāya* of the Leader, the split dies. If Iran’s Assembly of Experts did not elect the Leader, Art. 107 dies.
11. **OPEN:** Any audited *khums* total; number of *muqallidūn* (emulators of a given marja) per marja (no census).

### Waqf / *awqāf* / *vakıf* (endowment law, not a people)

1. **Institution name:** Self: *waqf* (pl. *awqāf*; Turkish *vakıf*; “pious endowment”). External: “Islamic Church property,” “the Waqf Board owns India.” A waqf is a **dedicated asset** plus a **statute that administers it**. The mutawalli (trustee of the dedicated property) is not a bishop.
2. **Governance:** Classic fiqh: founder’s deed (*waqfiyya*, the founding instrument) binds; mutawalli administers. Modern states **overwrote** that with ministries and boards:
   - **India:** Waqf Act **1995** (Act 43, 22 November 1995) — Central Waqf Council; State Waqf Boards; alienation without Board sanction void. India Code: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15941/1/the_waqf_act%2C_1995.pdf **Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025** (Act 14 of 2025, President’s assent **5 April 2025**; in force **8 April 2025** by S.O. 1646(E)) — short title of the 1995 Act becomes “Unified Waqf Management, Empowerment, Efficiency and Development” Act. Gazette: https://centralwaqfcouncil.gov.in/system/files/Wakf-Gazette.pdf
   - **Turkey:** *Vakıflar Kanunu* **No. 5737**, 20 February 2008 (RG 27 February 2008, 26800), administered by **Vakıflar Genel Müdürlüğü** (VGM, the General Directorate of Foundations). Categories: *mazbut* (directly administered by VGM), *mülhak* (annexed, still with a named trustee under VGM oversight), community, trades, new vakıfs. https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5737.pdf
   - **Egypt:** *waqf ahlī* (family) abolished by Law **180 of 1952**; charitable *waqf khayrī* administered with Ministry of Awqaf as nāẓir (supervisor) and **Egyptian Awqaf Authority** as the investment body (Authority FAQ: https://awkafegypt.gov.eg/Home/FAQs ). Law 48 of 1946 remains the older waqf statute layer.
3. **Legal status:** India: statutory boards under central act, now as amended 2025 (constitutional challenges **OPEN** as litigation, not a census). Turkey: VGM as a public legal person; community vakıfs are non-Muslim as well as Muslim — **do not** call VGM “the Muslim church.” Egypt: ministry + authority after 1952 nationalization of family waqf.
4. **Reach:** Each statute is territorial. A Delhi Board order does not run Istanbul. UNESCO/Haram al-Sharif “Waqf” (Jordanian/Jerusalem Islamic Waqf) is a **fourth** machine — **do not** mash with India 1995.
5. **Finance:** WAMSI (Waqf Assets Management System of India) / Board property lists exist in India — **OPEN** a single national rupee total this pass (do not reprint a parliamentary talking-point acreage as if it were a Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) audit). VGM publishes inventories — **OPEN** a 2024 lira total this pass. Egypt Awqaf Authority investment stock — **OPEN**.
6. **Rupture:** **1952** Egypt abolition of *waqf ahlī*. **1926–** Turkish republican vakıf centralization (1926 Civil Code / 1936 declaration layer; 2008 law as the current code). India: **1995** Act; **5–8 April 2025** amendment as the live statutory rupture.
7. **Subdivisions:** *Khayrī* vs (historical) *ahlī*; mosque vs shrine vs school vs graveyard; mutawalli-run vs board-seized. Turkey’s *cemaat vakıfları* (non-Muslim community foundations) sit in the **same** 5737 statute.
8. **Misconception:** “Waqf is one global treasury.” Three countries, three codes. “Boards own the religion.” They administer dedicated property under statute.
9. **Receipt:** India 1995 PDF; 2025 Gazette PDF; Turkey 5737; Egypt Authority FAQ + Law 180/1952 as the dated abolition.
10. **Falsifier:** If the 2025 Act did not receive presidential assent on 5 April 2025, cell 6’s India date dies. If Egypt still licensed new family waqf as in 1946, cell 6’s 1952 rupture dies.
11. **OPEN:** National asset totals; 2025 Act litigation outcomes; Jerusalem Islamic Waqf accounts.

### Chief Rabbinate of Israel (state-backed personal-status machine)

1. **Institution name:** Self: *ha-Rabbanut ha-Rashit le-Yisrael* (Chief Rabbinate of Israel). External: “Israel’s Vatican,” “the rabbis.” It is a **Knesset creature**, not the global Jewish people (see Atlas Jewish-diaspora file if present; not reconstructed here).
2. **Governance:** **Chief Rabbinate of Israel Law, 5740–1980** (passed 19 March 1980, *Sefer Ha-Chukkim* 965, 28 March 1980). Council functions include halakhic responsa (formal legal answers), kashrut (kosher-food) certificates, eligibility for *dayanim* (rabbinical court judges) under the **Dayanim Law, 5715–1955**, town rabbis, and **eligibility and appointment of marriage registrars**. English: https://israelilegislation.com/laws/chief-rabbinate-of-israel-law-1980/ Two Chief Rabbis (Ashkenazi and Sephardi) as a dual presidency of the Council — term rules in the 1980 law as amended. **Conversion Authority** sits in the Prime Minister’s Office and “operates in accordance with the Halachic instructions of the Chief Rabbi” (gov.il): https://www.gov.il/en/pages/aboutconversion — that is a **PMO administrative machine guided by the Chief Rabbinate**, not the 1980 law’s only organ.
3. **Legal status:** Statutory religious establishment for Jews in Israel. **Rabbinical Courts Jurisdiction (Marriage and Divorce) Law, 5713–1953**, s.1: marriage and divorce of Jews in Israel who are citizens or residents are under the **exclusive jurisdiction** of Rabbinical Courts; s.2: solemnized **according to the law of the Torah**. https://israelilegislation.com/laws/rabbinical-courts-jurisdiction-marriage-and-divorce-law-1953/ This is **not** the Law of Return (who is a Jew for immigration) — two machines (Interior/Law of Return vs rabbinical marriage).
4. **Reach:** Inside Israel, for Jews as defined for personal status. Diaspora Orthodox rabbis may or may not have their conversions **recognized for Israeli marriage**; that recognition fight is the Bene Israel / Ethiopian / Reform-Conservative collision. The Rabbinate does **not** govern Manhattan synagogues.
5. **Finance:** State-budgeted religious services (local religious councils, rabbinical courts, Chief Rabbinate) — **OPEN** the named-year Ministry of Religious Services line this pass. Not a global Jewish endowment.
6. **Rupture:** **1953** marriage/divorce jurisdiction law (the monopoly). **1980** Chief Rabbinate Law (statutory council). Supreme Court judgments on overseas conversions for *registration* vs marriage are a **third** machine (civil registration ≠ ḥuppah, the marriage canopy/ceremony).
7. **Subdivisions:** Ashkenazi vs Sephardi Chief Rabbis; municipal rabbis; rabbinical courts vs Conversion Authority vs local religious councils. Haredi (strictly Orthodox, non-state) courts that do not sit as state *dayanim* are **not** the 1953 monopoly.
8. **Misconception:** “The Chief Rabbinate is Judaism.” It is Israel’s Orthodox personal-status agency. “Conversion to Judaism anywhere equals Israeli marriage eligibility.” Gov.il itself distinguishes state conversion as what is “fully recognized… for Jewish marriage, divorce and burial.”
9. **Receipt:** 1980 Law; 1953 Law; Conversion Authority page (URLs above).
10. **Falsifier:** If s.1 of the 1953 law did not give exclusive marriage/divorce jurisdiction to rabbinical courts for Jews in Israel, cell 3 dies. If the 1980 law did not include marriage-registrar eligibility, cell 2 dies.
11. **OPEN:** Current budget line; count of unrecognized diaspora conversions pending; Bene Israel historical circulars as archival PDFs this pass.

### Hindu temple trusts, *akharas*, and state endowment law

1. **Institution name:** There is no Hindu Vatican. Named machines: **Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams** (TTD); **Travancore Devaswom Board** (TDB) and **Cochin Devaswom Board**; state Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments (HR&CE) / endowments departments; **akhara**s (monastic-martial orders) federated (unevenly) in the Akhil Bharatiya Akhara Parishad. External: “the Hindu Church,” “temples are privately run like US megachurches.”
2. **Governance:**
   - **TTD:** Special chapter of the **Andhra Pradesh Charitable and Hindu Religious Institutions and Endowments Act, 1987** (Act 30/1987, in force 28 May 1987), ss. 95–131, replacing the dedicated TTD Act 20/1979. Board/Executive Officer (EO) appointed under that statute — **state law, not a pontiff**. TTD training excerpt of the Act: https://spwpc.tirumala.org/Documents/EndowmentAct.pdf
   - **Kerala Devaswom Boards:** **Travancore-Cochin Hindu Religious Institutions Act, 1950** (Act XV of 1950). s.3: administration of incorporated/unincorporated Devaswoms (except Sree Padmanabhaswamy) vests in the Travancore Devaswom Board; s.4: body corporate. https://490kdbtemples.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/the-travancore-cochin-hindu-religious-institution-act-xv-of-1950.pdf
   - **Akhara**s: monastic-martial orders with their own *mahants* (heads/superiors of the order or temple); the Parishad is an association, **not** a Companies Act parent of TTD.
3. **Legal status:** Public religious trusts under state Hindu-endowment statutes. **Shirur Mutt**: *Commissioner, Hindu Religious Endowments, Madras v. Sri Lakshmindra Thirtha Swamiar of Sri Shirur Mutt*, **16 April 1954**, 1954 AIR 282, 1954 SCR 1005 — struck parts of the Madras HR&CE Act 1951 as violating Arts. 19(1)(f), 25, 26; contribution under s.76(1) held a **tax**, not a fee. Supreme Court of India (SCI): https://api.sci.gov.in/jonew/judis/933.pdf That judgment **limits** state control; it does **not** abolish TTD or TDB.
4. **Reach:** TTD: Tirumala and scheduled temples under AP law. TDB: Travancore temples under Act XV (Sabarimala is in this universe, with High Court Devaswom Bench supervision — a **court machine**, not a mahant). A Tamil Nadu HR&CE temple is a **third** statute. Diaspora Hindu temples (US 501(c)(3)s) are **not** TTD branches.
5. **Finance:**
   - **TTD 2023–24 budget ₹4,411.68 crore**, released 22 March 2023 by TTD Chairman/EO (TTD News, official): https://news.tirumala.org/ — treat as **board-approved budget**, not a CAG residual statement. Latest full TTD “annual report” PDF on tirumala.org publications list is **stale (through 2016–17)** — **OPEN** a 2023–24 audited integer.
   - TDB: **OPEN** the latest Board budget integer this pass (Kerala legislature / Board site).
   - Akhara assets: **OPEN**.
6. **Rupture:** Colonial HR&CE acts (Madras 1927/1951); **1950** Devaswom Boards (princely temples → statutory boards); **1954** Shirur Mutt; **1987** AP Act 30 consolidating TTD.
7. **Subdivisions:** Vaishnava/Shaiva/Shakta *sampradāya*s (lineages); mutts (monastic institutions) vs public temples vs *akharas*; Padmanabhaswamy (royal-trust litigation) **carved out** of TDB in s.3. Do not file Jains or Sikhs here.
8. **Misconception:** “Hindus have no institutions, only festivals.” TTD’s ₹4,411.68 crore budget is an institution. “The Supreme Court banned state control of temples.” Shirur Mutt banned *particular* provisions and a tax-disguised-as-fee; boards still exist.
9. **Receipt:** AP Act 30/1987 excerpt; Act XV 1950; Shirur Mutt SCI PDF; TTD News budget item.
10. **Falsifier:** If Act 30/1987 ss.95–131 did not govern TTD, cell 2 dies. If Shirur Mutt 1954 AIR 282 did not void s.76(1) as a tax, cell 3 dies. If TTD News did not publish ₹4,411.68 crore for 2023–24, cell 5 dies.
11. **OPEN:** TTD audited 2023–24 accounts; TDB budget; Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra trust accounts; Parishad legal status.

### The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (corporate contrast)

1. **Institution name:** Self: *The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints*. External: “Mormon Church,” “LDS Church.” Legal affiliates include **Ensign Peak Advisors, Inc.** (investment manager) and national charities (e.g. Great Britain unlimited company, charity no. **242451**).
2. **Governance:** **General Handbook** ch. 5: the President of the Church is the senior Apostle, the only person authorized to exercise all priesthood keys; counselors form the **First Presidency** (Doctrine and Covenants (D&C) 107:22). On the President’s death the First Presidency dissolves; the Quorum of the Twelve leads; the senior Apostle becomes President. The Council of the First Presidency and Quorum of the Twelve, by unanimous voice, declares doctrine and policy. https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/5-general-and-area-leadership Apostles serve for life. **Current named officers change by death/succession — OPEN the occupants as of this pull** (do not freeze a newsroom HTML that may lag).
3. **Legal status:** US: unincorporated church / corporations sole (a legal form in which a single office-holder, not a board, holds the organization’s property and authority) and affiliates; **churches need not file IRS Form 990**. Ensign Peak: nonprofit investment adviser that **must** file Form 13F (a quarterly report of 13(f) securities holdings) when over the 13(f) threshold. UK: registered charity 242451, Companies Act accounts. Australia: Australian Charities and Not-for-profits Commission (ACNC) filings as a **different** machine.
4. **Reach:** Hierarchical worldwide (stakes, wards, missions, temples) under Salt Lake general authority — the contrast with SBC Art. IV. Local leaders do not own the meetinghouse in the congregational sense.
5. **Finance — disclosed slices, not a global 10-K:**

Civil penalties: Ensign Peak **$4,000,000**; Church **$1,000,000**. https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/admin/2023/34-96951.pdf **13F equities ≠ total Church assets** (real estate, non-13F securities, operating companies).
   - **UK Charity 242451**, year ended 31 December 2023: incoming resources **£65,458,000**; member donations **£40,634,000**; related-party (Corporation of the Presiding Bishop / CHC) funding **£23,667,000**; total charity funds **£232,112,000**. This is **Great Britain**, not Salt Lake. Charity Commission accounts.

### Sikh institutional governance (SGPC / Akal Takht / other Boards)

1. **Institution name:** Self: **Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee** (SGPC); **Sri Akal Takht Sahib**. External: “Sikh Vatican,” “Golden Temple trust.” SGPC is the statutory **Board** under the **Sikh Gurdwaras Act, 1925** (Punjab Act VIII of 1925; Governor-General’s assent **28 July 1925**; in force **1 November 1925**). It is **not** the Akal Takht, and it is **not** every gurdwara (Sikh house of worship) on earth.
2. **Governance:** Act 1925 creates a Board (SGPC) elected by registered Sikh electors; s.125: duty to ensure committees deal with gurdwara property per the Act; **control, direction and general superintendence** over committees vest in the Board (as amended). Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) text: https://mha.gov.in/files/acts_rules_documets/Sikh_Gurrdwara_Act1925.pdf **Akal Takht** *jathedar* (the appointed head who speaks for a Takht, a Sikh seat of temporal authority) issues religious *hukamnama*s (formal edicts from a Takht); SGPC’s own “About” page says it “works under the Directives of Sri Akal Takht Sahib” while being the elected manager of notified gurdwaras — **two hats, often in tension**. https://sgpc.net/about-sgpc/ **Sikh Gurdwaras (Amendment) Act, 2016** (Act 21 of 2016, 5 May 2016): elector disqualifications (trimming beard/*keshas* (uncut hair); smoking; alcohol).
3. **Legal status:** Statutory body under a 1925 colonial/Punjab act (now in force as adapted). **Delhi Sikh Gurdwaras Act, 1971** creates the **Delhi Sikh Gurdwara Management Committee (DSGMC)** — a **different** Board. **Haryana Sikh Gurdwara Management Act, 2014** is a **competing state claim** over Haryana gurdwaras; SGPC still budgets for them while denying the takeover (SGPC president, 2023 budget briefing). Do **not** resolve that lawsuit in this cell.
4. **Reach:** Notified gurdwaras under the 1925 Act (historically Punjab & associated lists), not Toronto or Southall except by voluntary affiliation. Takhts beyond Akal Takht (Kesgarh, Damdama, Patna, Hazur Sahib) have **other** managing committees.
5. **Finance:** SGPC **general house budgets** (offerings-based estimates, not a CAG audit):
   - FY **2023–24: ₹1,138.14 crore** (passed 28 March 2023). Gurdwara Sahibs **₹855 crore**. Official: https://sgpc.net/sgpc-passes-budget-of-rs-1138-14-crore-for-2023-24-amid-resonance-of-sikh-slogan-of-high-spirit/
   - FY **2024–25: ₹1,260.97 crore** (passed 29 March 2024). Gurdwaras **₹994.51 crore**; educational institutions **₹251 crore**. Official: https://sgpc.net/sgpc-passes-budget-of-rs-1260-97-crore-for-2024-25/
   - Label these **passed budgets**, not audited residual equity.
6. **Rupture:** Gurdwara Reform movement / **1925 Act** (wresting mahant control). **1984** (army action / Delhi massacres) as political trauma, **not** a rewrite of s.125. **2014** Haryana Act as the live territorial rupture.
7. **Subdivisions:** SGPC vs DSGMC vs Haryana committee vs Hazur Sahib board vs overseas gurdwara trusts. Jatha/misal (historical Sikh fighting-band / confederacy) history is not the 1925 Board.
8. **Misconception:** “SGPC is the Sikh pope.” It is an elected property Board under statute; Akal Takht claims religious supremacy; they collide. “All gurdwaras report to Amritsar.” Delhi and Nanded have other acts/committees.
9. **Receipt:** 1925 Act (MHA PDF); 2016 Amendment; SGPC budget pages (URLs above).
10. **Falsifier:** If s.125 did not vest general superintendence in the Board, cell 2 dies. If the 2024–25 passed budget ≠ ₹1,260.97 crore on sgpc.net, cell 5 dies.
11. **OPEN:** Audited 2023–24 accounts; Supreme Court/final status of Haryana gurdwaras; DSGMC latest budget.
