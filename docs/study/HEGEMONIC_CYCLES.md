# Hegemonic / systemic cycles

**Not cycles of contention. Not a tweet. Not “globalists” as a people. Not Nisaba.**

**Subject:** *hegemonic / systemic cycles*. Arrighi: successive **systemic cycles of accumulation** (Genoa → Dutch → British → US). Modelski: **long cycles of world leadership** (sea power + order-provision, often Portugal → Netherlands → Britain → US). Gilpin / Kennedy: rise, overstretch, relative decline. Kindleberger: who provides the **public goods** of the world economy (reserve, navy, last-resort lending).

**Job of this file:** who holds **reserve money, navy (or equivalent force projection), standards, and the after-empire aftershock in the old core**. Same *shape* as a borders cycle in the movement file — different question (**table vs street**). Not four feminist-style numbered waves.

**Repeats:** rise → extend → overstretch → challenger + aftershock in the old core.

**Plot leak:** hidden-hand people, blood libel, “they.” **Twin mistake:** treating midterm `#globalist` volume as this cycle. That is hidden-hand hitchhiking on clips (medium file).

**Strict file:** currency share, bases, industry, alliances, migration after empire. Pair the churches. One SIPRI year is not a world-system.

A dissertation is **one named cycle + one periodization fight + one historical file + one table (COFER or SIPRI or industry) + one method**. Date live pulls `YYYY-MM-DD`. Change Live lines only. Skip Elliott. Skip Q-style “they.”

## 0. Theory and unit of analysis

**Mechanism stack (this machine):** a core polity concentrates **production + finance + coercion + rule-setting** → others use its money and its peace → costs of order rise (overstretch) → challenger(s) in production/finance/force/standards → war or cold succession → aftershock in the old core (industry loss, then inflows, then “who is the people” — that last movie is **contention**, measured here only as residual).

**Unit of analysis for every numbered stack below** (not Lasswell acts — that lock is for claim-making. This lock is for **the table**):

1. **Reserve / key money** — who others hold and invoice in
2. **Force projection** — navy, then air/nuclear/forward bases; sea lanes
3. **Production** — share of manufactures, energy, later chips/shipping
4. **Standards** — weights, law of the sea, accounting, tech stacks, SWIFT-class rails
5. **Date** — rise / extend / overstretch / succession as-of + provisional vs final
6. **Who pays for order** — taxpayers, colonies, allies, seigniorage
7. **Receipt** — **history** (monograph, treaty, base, charter) **and** **table** (COFER, SIPRI, IISS, WB). Pew without Arrighi is a dashboard. Arrighi without COFER is a sermon.
8. **Falsifier** — “unipolar forever” / “they” / GDP-only ranking without money+force+standards
9. **Grain** — a war ≠ a cycle; a poll drop ≠ succession; a factory move ≠ hegemony
10. **Institutions** — central bank, admiralty, alliance, standard-setting body. Not an SMO. Not a people-noun.

If 1–5 will not fill, it is a story. If 1–5 fill and 7–8 will not, it is a **clean story** (a map with villains).

## 1. Dutch commercial / financial (~17th c.)

(Arrighi: Dutch cycle after Genoa. Modelski: Dutch after Portuguese. Do not start history in 1945.)

1. **Money:** Amsterdam bank, bill of exchange, VOC paper.
2. **Force:** Dutch sea, convoy, VOC/WIC as **armed commerce**.
3. **Production:** carry trade, Baltic grain, herring, then finance over workshop.
4. **Standards:** commercial law, joint-stock, information in the bourse.
5. **Date:** rise after Spanish overstretch; 17th-c. peak; 18th-c. British succession. Provisional on whether Genoa/Portugal is numbered **0**.
6. **Who pays:** stadholders, VOC investors, taxed provinces — not “the Jews” as a people-noun (plot leak).
7. **Receipt:** *History* — Israel, *Dutch Primacy in World Trade* · de Vries & van der Woude, *The First Modern Economy* · Arrighi chs. on Dutch cycle. *Table* — ship tons, VOC capital (as **history of a firm**, not a meme).
8. **Falsifier:** if you cannot name the **bank and the fleet**, you have a painting of tulips.
9. **Grain:** tulip mania is a **finance episode**. Hegemony is the carry trade + sea.
10. **Institutions:** VOC, Wisselbank, States General.

**Method owed:** freight and bills, not a Golden Age coffee-table book.

## 2. British industrial / naval (~18th–early 20th)

1. **Money:** sterling as invoice and reserve; City of London.
2. **Force:** Royal Navy, coaling stations, Pax Britannica.
3. **Production:** first industrial core (pair the **Perez** file — cheap coal/iron/steam — **here** it is who **held** it).
4. **Standards:** Greenwich, English commercial law, gold standard (late).
5. **Date:** 18th-c. rise vs France; 19th-c. extend; overstretch (Boer, 1914–18, 1931 sterling leave-gold); US succession **after** 1914–45, not in a tweet.
6. **Who pays:** British taxpayer, India, dominion, then US loans (Tooze).
7. **Receipt:** *History* — Kennedy · Cain & Hopkins, *British Imperialism* · Darwin, *The Empire Project* · Tooze, *The Deluge*. Treaties: Treaty of Utrecht / Vienna / Versailles on Avalon. *Table* — naval tonnage, sterling share, industrial output vs US/Germany (Mitchell; Maddison).
8. **Falsifier:** “the sun never set therefore hegemony was culture.” If Germany outproduces steel and there is no navy gap, the **force** cell is already in play (1890s–1914).
9. **Grain:** Waterloo is an event. Sterling + fleet + workshop is the cycle.
10. **Institutions:** Bank of England, Admiralty, Colonial Office, gold-standard clubs.

**Aftershock (old core):** deindustrialization is **later** (post-1945/1970s) — don’t dump 1970s Britain into 1815. Migration after empire → **count** (ONS, MPI), then the street is contention.

**Method owed:** naval estimates + industrial shares. Comparison: France as the failed peer, not a villain race.

## 3. US dollar / security (~1945–)

1. **Money:** dollar invoice + reserve + Treasuries. Bretton Woods then **Nixon shock** then still-dollar (exorbitant privilege).
2. **Force:** two-ocean navy, nuclear, forward bases, alliance system (NATO, bilateral Asia).
3. **Production:** 1945 peak share; then relative decline in manufactures; still tech/finance/energy high. **Measure**, don’t mythologize.
4. **Standards:** IMF/World Bank, ISO-class, later internet/chip/export-control stacks.
5. **Date:** 1945 extend; 1971 money form changes, security form does not automatically; unipolar 1991– as **provisional**; live overstretch **open**.
6. **Who pays:** US taxpayer + seigniorage + allied host; NATO/Ukraine = **whose war, whose tax** (contention overlaps; this file only asks who holds the **guarantee**).
7. **Receipt:** *History* — Ikenberry · Gaddis, *Strategies of Containment* · Tooze, *Crashed* · Steil, *The Battle of Bretton Woods*. *Table* — COFER USD share https://data.imf.org/ · SIPRI https://www.sipri.org/ · IISS · DoD posture.
8. **Falsifier:** a poll drop is **not** succession. If COFER + bases + invoice currency still concentrate, “the empire fell on Tuesday” dies first.
9. **Grain:** a president is an event. The dollar-base-alliance bundle is the cycle.
10. **Institutions:** Fed, Treasury, NATO, combatant commands, IMF.

Pair Pew global “reliable partner” (drops in 2026 in several European states; Hungary not the same shape) https://www.pewresearch.org/global/ — **re-pull before you quote**.

## 4. Challenger / contested (open)

Not a numbered “Chinese century” until the **bundle** moves. Manufacturing ≠ reserve ≠ navy ≠ standards. Four meters, not a myth.

1. **Money:** USD still dominates COFER; CNY share is a **cell**, not a coronation. Invoice currency in Asia is the fight to measure.
2. **Force:** PLA(N) growth, dual-use ship count, nuclear, bases (Djibouti…) vs US alliance geography. IISS + SIPRI, not a parade video.
3. **Production:** manufacturing value-added, shipbuilding, rare earths, later foundry/GPU. WB/UNIDO/SEMIA-class stats.
4. **Standards:** 5G/telecom, payment rails (CIPS vs SWIFT), export-control lists, AI compute — **who others must plug into**.
5. **Date:** **open**. “Contested” is the honest as-of. A war scare is an event.
6. **Who pays:** PRC investment/state banks vs US alliance tax vs EU autonomy talk. Three bills.
7. **Receipt:** *History* — Arrighi, *Adam Smith in Beijing* (as **hypothesis**) · Allison, *Destined for War* (as **Thucydides marketing** — take or kill with data) · IISS *Military Balance*. *Table* — COFER · SIPRI · WB industry · UN Comtrade.
8. **Falsifier:** GDP PPP “already #1” without money+force. Also: “they” as a people. If the aftershock in the old core is **only** a clip about migrants, you left this file.
9. **Grain:** a factory in Shenzhen is production. Hegemony is whether others **must** use your money and your rules.
10. **Institutions:** PBOC, PLA, BRI contracts, AIIB, export-control regimes — name them.

**Aftershock in the old core:** industry loss, then inflows, then “who is the people.” First two are **this file + the demographic file**. The third is **contention**. Midterm `#globalist` volume is the **medium** file hitchhiking.

**Method owed:** four-meter dashboard updated annually. Comparison: late-19th-c. US vs Britain (production first, money/force later) — the usual historical rhyme, not a prophecy.
