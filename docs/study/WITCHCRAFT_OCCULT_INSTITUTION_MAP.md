# Witchcraft & Occult as Institution Map

Descriptive / institutional reference. Governance, legal status, money, land, and authority — **not** belief, ritual, or a how-to. Primary sources first. Wikipedia is a finding aid. OPEN rather than invented. Pull date **2026-11-20**. Companion to Religion as Institution (`docs/study/RELIGION_AS_INSTITUTION_MAP.md`) but **not** the same flock and **not** a people-count.

This is not a theology paper, not a spellbook, and not a census of “witches.” **Witchcraft-as-crime**, **Wicca-as-church**, **census write-in**, **consumer-law marketplace**, and **African-diaspora houses** are different machines. Do not mash the 1735 English statute with Gardnerian covens. Do not mash the Church of Satan with The Satanic Temple. Do not mash Office for National Statistics (**ONS**) write-ins for “Pagan” with those for “Wicca.” Do not treat a Form 990 (the Internal Revenue Service (**IRS**) annual nonprofit return) as a global occult treasury.

## How to read an entry

Every entry uses the same fixed 11-field template. Field labels stay in this form throughout. In any **Falsifier** line, “cell N” means field N on this list (so “cell 5” is Financial assets + revenue).

1. **Institution name** — self-designation vs. external/common name
2. **Governance structure** — who holds final authority, how they are selected, term/succession rules
3. **Legal status** — crime, church, registered nonprofit, state-recognized association, tax treatment — and how this varies by country
4. **Geographic/jurisdictional reach** — where authority is actually recognized or enforced, vs. where it is nominal
5. **Financial assets + revenue** — audited or officially reported figures, dated and sourced; OPEN where undisclosed
6. **Defining institutional rupture** — statute, court, incorporation, recognition, or suppression — with a real date
7. **Internal jurisdictional/denominational subdivisions** — the real distinct bodies inside an apparent single institution
8. **Common misconception corrected** — the specific false assumption about how this institution works
9. **Receipt** — the actual primary source
10. **Falsifier** — the one fact that would prove the entry wrong
11. **OPEN** — anything undisclosed or unverifiable, named honestly

### Frame: several machines, not one church

1. **Institution name:** External: “the witchcraft world,” “the occult,” “Wicca,” used as if one HQ. Self: none. There is no occult equivalent of the Holy See. This row is the map’s frame, not a corporation.
2. **Governance:** No primate, no synod with worldwide ordinary jurisdiction, no single credential that makes a person a “witch” in public law. Named later machines (coven confederations, 501(c)(3) churches under the Internal Revenue Code (**IRC**), Icelandic *trúfélag* rolls, English criminal statutes, **ONS** write-ins) each have their own governors. A coven that never joined Covenant of the Goddess (**CoG**) is still a coven; CoG’s charter says so.
3. **Legal status:** In England and Wales after **26 May 2008**, “being a witch” is not a named crime; selling a spell is consumer law if the commercial practice misleads. In the United States, *Dettmer v. Landon*, **799 F.2d 929** (United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit, **4 September 1986**) treats the Church of Wicca as a religion for the First Amendment; that holding is not a federal incorporation of all witches. In Iceland, Ásatrúarfélagið is a ministry-recognized religious association on Statistics Iceland (**Hagstofa Íslands**) table **MAN10001** — a *roll*, not a census write-in.
4. **Reach:** Global folklore and book markets are not jurisdiction. Enforcement follows the named statute or the named tax-exempt person. A Cairo fatwa, a Gardnerian lineage, and a Salem tourism board do not share a court.
5. **Finance:** There is **no** consolidated occult balance sheet. Later rows copy named **IRS** Employer Identification Numbers (**EIN**s) and **Hagstofa** rolls. Magazine “witchcraft industry worth $X billion” figures are **not** used here.
6. **Rupture:** For English public law, the **Witchcraft Act 1735** (9 Geo. 2 c. 5) ends the crime of *being* a witch and substitutes the crime of *claiming* magical powers. For public Wicca, **22 June 1951** (Fraudulent Mediums Act royal assent) is the dated repeal of 1735. For United States veterans’ markers, **23 April 2007** (Department of Veterans Affairs (**VA**) pentacle settlement).
7. **Subdivisions:** Crime ≠ church ≠ write-in ≠ marketplace ≠ African-diaspora *casa*. Reconstructionist heathenry (Ásatrúarfélagið) is **not** Wicca. Thelema / Ordo Templi Orientis (**OTO**) is **not** Wicca. Lucumí / Santería is **not** Wicca. Church of Satan is **not** The Satanic Temple.
8. **Misconception:** “Witchcraft is one underground church with a secret pope.” Public law treats it as crime, then fraud, then church, then consumer practice — in that order, in England — and as several 501(c)(3)s and one First Amendment holding in the United States. Mesopotamian *āšipūtu* (the named exorcist-scholar of a palace/temple) is a **different** historical machine; it is not Gardnerian Wicca and is not mapped as a living corporation here.
9. **Receipt:** Standing rules in [`docs/study/STUDY_SERIES_RULES.md`](STUDY_SERIES_RULES.md); later cells cite statutes and dockets, not this paragraph.
10. **Falsifier:** If a single legal person held ordinary worldwide jurisdiction over “witchcraft” the way *Lumen Gentium* 22 describes papal power, this frame dies.
11. **OPEN:** Any global headcount of “witches”; any industry-size dollar figure not copied from a named filing.

### English / British criminal statute sequence (crime → fraud → consumer law)

1. **Institution name:** Self: successive Acts of Parliament. External: “the Witchcraft Act,” as if one law from 1542 to now.
2. **Governance:** Parliament makes and repeals. Prosecutions under the 1951 Act in England and Wales required the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions (**DPP**) (Fraudulent Mediums Act 1951 s. 1, as enacted). No church sits on the statute.
3. **Legal status — dated stack, not mashed:**
   - **1542** — Witchcraft Act, 33 Hen. 8 c. 8: first English statute making witchcraft a felony in the royal courts (repealed 1547; restored in substance **1563**, 5 Eliz. 1 c. 16).
   - **1604** — 1 Jac. 1 c. 12 (James I): capital felony for named conjuration/witchcraft offences; remains until the 1735 repeal of the older capital scheme.
   - **1735** — Witchcraft Act, 9 Geo. 2 c. 5: repeals the older witchcraft felonies and creates an offence of **pretending** to exercise witchcraft, sorcery, enchantment, or conjuration (fine/imprisonment, not hanging). United Kingdom Parliament heritage note: the 1736 Act “imposing fines or imprisonment on people who claimed to be able to use magical powers.” https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/private-lives/religion/overview/witchcraft/
   - **22 June 1951** — Fraudulent Mediums Act 1951 (14 & 15 Geo. 6 c. 33): long title “An Act to repeal the Witchcraft Act, 1735, and to make, in substitution for certain provisions of section four of the Vagrancy Act, 1824, express provision for the punishment of persons who fraudulently purport to act as, spiritualistic mediums or to exercise powers of telepathy, clairvoyance or other similar powers.” https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo6/14-15/33
   - **26 May 2008** — Fraudulent Mediums Act repealed by The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008 (S.I. 2008/1277), Sch. 2 para. 3 and Sch. 4 Pt. 1 (with reg. 28(2)(3)). https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/Geo6/14-15/33 ; https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2008/1277
4. **Reach:** England and Wales for the 1951 Act as the legislation.gov.uk text marks it; Scotland and Ireland had their own witchcraft statutes historically and are **not** this stack. Do not export 9 Geo. 2 c. 5 onto a Massachusetts 1692 indictment.
5. **Finance:** A criminal statute has no endowment. **OPEN** any Treasury line for 1951-Act fines this pass.
6. **Rupture:** **1735** (crime of being a witch ends; crime of claiming powers begins). **1951** (1735 repealed; *intent to deceive* + *for reward* become the commercial elements). **26 May 2008** (named mediums offence dissolved into general unfair-trading law).
7. **Subdivisions:** Capital-era Acts (1542/1563/1604) ≠ 1735 pretence offence ≠ 1951 fraudulent-medium offence ≠ 2008 consumer regulations. Vagrancy Act 1824 s. 4 (fortune-telling) is a **parallel** street-offence machine, not a rewrite of 1735.
8. **Misconception:** “Witchcraft is still a hanging crime in England.” It has not been, as a named felony, since 1735. “The 1951 Act legalized Wicca.” It repealed 1735 and punished *fraudulent* paid mediumship; it did not incorporate a church. Gerald Gardner’s *Witchcraft Today* (1954) is a book after the repeal, not a charter.
9. **Receipt:** Parliament heritage page (URL above); Fraudulent Mediums Act 1951 on legislation.gov.uk; S.I. 2008/1277 repeal note on the same 1951 page.
10. **Falsifier:** If 9 Geo. 2 c. 5 did not repeal the older capital witchcraft felonies, cell 3’s 1735 row dies. If S.I. 2008/1277 did not repeal the 1951 Act on 26 May 2008, cell 3’s last row dies.
11. **OPEN:** Full session-statute text of 9 Geo. 2 c. 5 as fetched from legislation.gov.uk this pass (the Geo2/9/5 path 404’d here); count of 1951-Act prosecutions as an official Ministry of Justice table (not a magazine).

### Massachusetts 1692 — Court of Oyer and Terminer (a court, not a coven)

1. **Institution name:** Self: Court of Oyer and Terminer (special commission, Province of Massachusetts Bay, 1692) and, after its dissolution, the Superior Court of Judicature. External: “the Salem witch trials,” “Salem,” as if the 2026 tourism city were the court.
2. **Governance:** Governor Sir William Phips commissioned the Court of Oyer and Terminer in **May 1692**. Chief Magistrate: William Stoughton. The court was dissolved in **October 1692**; remaining cases moved to the Superior Court of Judicature in 1693. This is a **royal-provincial court**, not a church court and not a town meeting.
3. **Legal status:** Indictments for witchcraft under Massachusetts law (the colony had treated witchcraft as a capital offence; compare Body of Liberties 1641, capital laws). Spectral evidence (testimony that a person’s specter afflicted the witness) was used in the Oyer and Terminer phase and curtailed later. Convictions and executions are **judicial acts**, not census counts of a religion.
4. **Reach:** Essex County towns (Salem Village / Danvers, Salem Town, Andover, others). Not England. Not 2026 Wicca. Not the **VA** pentacle.
5. **Finance:** Court costs, confiscations, and later reverse-attainder payments are a **different** money chain from any modern 501(c)(3). **OPEN** a named 1692–1710 restitution total copied from the legislative resolve this pass.
6. **Rupture:** Commission of the Court of Oyer and Terminer (**27 May 1692**, Old Style, as commonly dated in the trial records); last executions **22 September 1692**; dissolution **October 1692**; 1693 Superior Court acquittals/reprieves. Nineteen hanged; Giles Corey pressed to death — **twenty** dead by the court process, not “thousands in Salem.”
7. **Subdivisions:** Oyer and Terminer 1692 ≠ Superior Court of Judicature 1693 ≠ 1711 reversal/restitution statute ≠ Resolves of 1957 Chapter 145 (Ann Pudeator “and certain other persons”) ≠ **Acts of 2001 Chapter 122** (approved **31 October 2001**), which names Bridget Bishop, Susannah Martin, Alice Parker, Margaret Scott, and Wilmot Redd onto that 1957 resolve. 2026 Salem tourism and the Peabody Essex Museum are **other** legal persons.
8. **Misconception:** “Salem proves Wicca is an old American church.” 1692 is a capital docket. Modern Wicca’s public legal life in the United States is *Dettmer* (1986) and later 501(c)(3)s, not a 1692 charter. “Nine million witches died in Europe, therefore Salem.” Do not import an unsourced European total into this cell.
9. **Receipt:** *Records of the Salem Witch-Hunt*, ed. Bernard Rosenthal (Cambridge University Press, 2009) as the standard documentary edition of the indictments, depositions, and warrants — use the edition, not a tourist plaque. Massachusetts legislative naming of remaining victims: **31 October 2001** resolve (Chapter 122 of the Acts of 2001) as the late official naming machine — confirm text at https://malegislature.gov/Laws/SessionLaws/Acts/2001/Chapter122
10. **Falsifier:** If the 1692 court were a standing ecclesiastical tribunal of a Wiccan church, cell 2 dies. If the executed total in the documentary edition is not nineteen hanged plus Corey, cell 6 dies.
11. **OPEN:** Exact Phips commission text as copied from the Massachusetts Archives this pass; restitution dollar total.

### Wicca as First Amendment religion — *Dettmer v. Landon* (no Vatican)

1. **Institution name:** Self in the case: “Church of Wicca” / witchcraft as Dettmer’s religion. External: “the government recognized Wicca,” as if Congress chartered a denomination.
2. **Governance:** Herbert Daniel Dettmer, inmate at Powhatan Correctional Center (Virginia), studied a correspondence course of the Church of Wicca and sought ritual items. Prison officials denied the items under Division Guideline 141. No Wiccan synod is a party. The United States Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit (**4th Cir.**) is the court, not a church board.
3. **Legal status:** District Court, Eastern District of Virginia, *Dettmer v. Landon*, **617 F. Supp. 592** (E.D. Va. **28 August 1985**): Church of Wicca is a religion for First Amendment purposes; injunction on items. **4th Cir.**, **799 F.2d 929** (argued 7 April 1986, decided **4 September 1986**): **affirms** that “the doctrine taught by the Church of Wicca is a religion,” and **vacates** the injunction. Prisoners retain religious freedom but restrictions are reviewed with “wide-ranging deference” to prison security (*Bell v. Wolfish*); no inmate, of any faith, could possess candles. https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/799/929/117777/
4. **Reach:** Binding in the **4th Cir.** (Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, West Virginia) as circuit precedent; persuasive elsewhere. It is **not** a nationwide incorporation of every coven and **not** an **IRS** determination letter.
5. **Finance:** None. A prisoner docket is not a church 990.
6. **Rupture:** **4 September 1986** — Wicca named a religion in a published federal appellate opinion, **and** the access injunction dies the same day. Do not quote only the district court.
7. **Subdivisions:** Church of Wicca (the correspondence-course body in the record) ≠ Gardnerian/Alexandrian/Dianic covens ≠ CoG ≠ Circle Sanctuary. Prison Free Exercise after *Employment Division v. Smith*, **494 U.S. 872** (1990), and the Religious Land Use and Institutionalized Persons Act (**RLUIPA**, 42 U.S.C. §§ 2000cc et seq., 2000) is a **later** statute; *Dettmer* is pre-*Smith* on the religion-definition holding.
8. **Misconception:** “*Dettmer* means prisons must give witches candles.” The **4th Cir.** reversed that injunction. “Wicca is not a religion in US law.” Cell 3’s appellate holding is the opposite, inside that circuit, on that record.
9. **Receipt:** 799 F.2d 929 (URL above); district 617 F. Supp. 592.
10. **Falsifier:** If 799 F.2d 929 did not hold the Church of Wicca to be a religion, cell 3 dies. If the injunction was affirmed, cell 6 dies.
11. **OPEN:** Whether later **4th Cir.** panels treated *Dettmer*’s religion holding as limited to that plaintiff’s sincerity record; **RLUIPA** item-access outcomes in named Wiccan prison cases this pass.
