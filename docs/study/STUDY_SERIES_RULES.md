# Study-series standing rules

Applies to **every** document in `docs/study/` — Peoples & Ethnicities Atlas, Religion as Institution Map, Standards & Certification Map, War & Alliance Systems Map, Class & Social Mobility Map, International Pricing & Trade Map, Socionomics & Social Mood Theory Map, Witchcraft & Occult as Institution Map, Practice / Study / Maps order (Dual Throne replacement), and anything built after. Each document is self-contained. These rules are not optional per-doc flavor.

**Counting machines never mashed.** A census table, a registration roll, a treaty article, a budget line, and a vendor summary are different machines. Never merge casualty, troop, spending, membership, or headcount figures across sources with different methodologies. Name the machine; if two machines disagree, report both.

**OPEN rather than invented.** If a trigger, count, decision chain, or legal status is classified, disputed, or not copied from a primary table this pass, write **OPEN**. Do not resolve it to sound complete.

**Wikipedia is a finding aid**, never a final citation. Primary sources: treaty text, statute, court ruling, official statistical table, government legal opinion, Congressional Research Service report, depositary note.

**Primary sources over mashups.** Quote the short clause. Cite the article, section, or table. Do not use a think-tank paraphrase as the legal basis cell.

**Acronym discipline, every doc, every entry:** every acronym, initialism, or technical term gets expanded in full parenthetically at its *first occurrence in that document* — even if it was already expanded in an earlier companion doc, even if it seems self-evident, even if it's a well-known body (UN agencies, international courts, government offices included). Each document is self-contained; a reader opening only this one file should never hit an unexplained acronym. Before marking any document complete, do one full pass checking every capitalized 2+ letter string against this rule.

(File extensions, URL path segments, and statute/report *numbers* such as `50 U.S.C. § 1541` or `LSB11232` are not acronyms. The issuing body still is: expand Congressional Research Service (CRS) at first use, then `CRS LSB11232` is fine.)
