Families first (Niger-Congo/Bantu, Nilotic, Cushitic, Khoisan), then named groups. Many states **do not census ethnicity** (Nigeria 2006). Pull **2026-11-14**. ## Frame: four families (not four peoples)

1. **Niger-Congo (incl. Bantu):** Atlantic-Congo; Bantu as a late expansion (archaeology + linguistics: from the Cameroon/Nigeria borderlands, last 3,000 years — exact chronology contested). Named below: Yoruba, Igbo, Zulu, Xhosa.
2. **Nilotic:** Eastern Sudanic (Nilo-Saharan, itself a contested phylum). Named: Maasai; Luo not in the user’s list — do not pad.
3. **Cushitic:** Afroasiatic. Named: Oromo, Somali. Amhara is **Ethiosemitic**, not Cushitic — do not file Amhara under Cushitic.
4. **Khoisan:** Used here as a **cover term** for Khoe, Tuu, and Kx’a families — **not** one family. Named: San. “Khoisan” as a racial type is a 20th-c. flattening.

Falsifier for the frame: if Bantu were Cushitic, or Amhara were Cushitic, the filing dies.

## Yoruba

1. **Name:** Self: *Yorùbá*. Older exonyms: *Nago* (Brazil/Dahomey), *Lucumí* (Cuba).
2. **Language:** Yoruba (Volta-Niger, Niger-Congo). Literary 19th c. (Crowther); spoken far older.
3. **Homeland:** Southwestern Nigeria / Republic of Benin / Togo (Yoruba-speaking belt). Origin traditions: Ilé-Ifẹ̀.
4. **Subdivisions:** Ọ̀yọ́, Ẹ̀gbá, Ìjẹ̀bú, Ìjẹ̀ṣà, Ọ̀wọ̀, Ìkálẹ̀, Ketu, etc. “Yoruba” as a 19th-c. umbrella (Peel).
5. **Population:** Nigeria **2006 census did not publish ethnicity**. NPC (Nigeria’s National Population Commission) 2006 total population is not a Yoruba count. **OPEN.** Afrobarometer / DHS language is a survey proxy, not NPC. Benin INSAE: **OPEN** this pass.
6. **Rupture:** 19th-c. Yoruba wars and the fall of Oyo; Atlantic slave trade (Yoruba-speakers as a large documented share of Brazilian/Cuban arrivals — numbers **OPEN** as a people-count).
7. **Religion:** Mix: Protestant/Catholic/Pentecostal, Islam (especially Ilorin), and Ifá/Òrìṣà. Not “animist default.”
8. **Flattening:** “Yoruba = Nigeria southwest = one tribe.” City-state history contradicts tribe-as-unit.
9. **Receipt:** History — Peel, *Religious Encounter and the Making of the Yoruba*; Johnson, *History of the Yorubas*. Census — National Population Commission Nigeria 2006 (no ethnicity table).
10. **Falsifier:** If NPC 2006 (or 2023 if published) listed Yoruba ethnicity with a number, cell 5 OPEN dies.
11. **OPEN:** The headcount; Benin/Togo; 2023 Nigerian census ethnicity (if any — **not seen this pass**).

## Igbo

1. **Name:** Self: *Ndị Igbo*. External: “Ibo” (colonial orthography).
2. **Language:** Igbo (Volta-Niger). Standard Igbo is a 20th-c. compromise among dialects (Onitsha, Owerri, etc.).
3. **Homeland:** East of the Niger (Anambra, Imo, Abia, Ebonyi, Enugu) plus Delta/Rivers minorities.
4. **Subdivisions:** Clan/town identities were historically primary; “Igbo” as a political identity thickened in the 20th c. and during Biafra.
5. **Population:** No NPC ethnicity table. **OPEN.** Do not print “40 million” as census.
6. **Rupture:** 1967–70 Nigerian Civil War / Republic of Biafra (dated).
7. **Religion:** Christian majority (Catholic, Anglican, Pentecostal); traditional *ọdịnala* persists. Islam is minority (not the regional default).
8. **Flattening:** “Igbo are a tribe that suddenly appeared in 1967”; or Ahiara/Biafra as the whole people.
9. **Receipt:** History — Afigbo; Harneit-Sievers. Census — NPC 2006, no ethnicity. War — Gowon / Ojukwu period sources; mortality **contested range** (do not pick a death toll).
10. **Falsifier:** NPC ethnicity count; or a demonstration that Igbo was not a self-name before 1967 (it was).
11. **OPEN:** Headcount; war dead.

## Hausa–Fulani (umbrella — flag it)

1. **Name:** “Hausa–Fulani” is a **Nigerian political umbrella**, not one ethnolinguistic people (ethnolinguistic: pertaining to a group defined by shared language as a marker of ethnic identity). Hausa: *Hausawa*. Fulani: *Fulɓe* (Pulaar/Fulfulde).
2. **Language:** Hausa (Chadic, **Afroasiatic** — not Niger-Congo). Fulfulde (Atlantic, Niger-Congo). Different families.
3. **Homeland:** Hausa: northern Nigeria / southern Niger (Kano, Katsina, Zaria, etc.). Fulɓe: Sahel-wide (Senegal to Cameroon), pastoral and urban (Torodbe clerical).
4. **Subdivisions:** Hausa city-states; Fulɓe: cattle Fulani vs town Fulani; Sokoto Caliphate overlay (1804) produced the political mash.
5. **Population:** No NPC ethnicity. Niger/Cameroon/Senegal have language tables — **not pulled as a single total this pass. OPEN.** Do not add “Hausa–Fulani 30% of Nigeria” as if it were 2006.
6. **Rupture:** 1804–08 Sokoto jihad (Usman dan Fodio) — dated political fusion.
7. **Religion:** Sunni Islam (Maliki, one of the four Sunni legal schools; Tijani and Qadiri are Sufi *tariqa*s — organized mystical orders) as the regional majority among both; not identical to ethnicity.
8. **Flattening:** Treating Hausa–Fulani as one census tribe; filing Hausa under Niger-Congo.
9. **Receipt:** History — Last, *The Sokoto Caliphate*; Hogben/Kirk-Greene. Linguistics — Hausa = Chadic. Census — NPC 2006 none; Niger INS **OPEN**.
10. **Falsifier:** If Hausa were Niger-Congo, cell 2 dies. If NPC published a combined “Hausa-Fulani” code as one language, the umbrella warning still holds linguistically.
11. **OPEN:** Separate Hausa vs Fulɓe counts for Nigeria.

## Zulu

1. **Name:** Self: *amaZulu*. Language: *isiZulu*.
2. **Language:** Nguni, Bantu, Niger-Congo.
3. **Homeland:** KwaZulu-Natal. 19th-c. kingdom (Shaka) as political crystallization, not the origin of Nguni speech.
4. **Subdivisions:** Regional *izifunda*; not the same as all Nguni (Xhosa, Swati, Ndebele).
5. **Population:** Stats SA Census **2022**: **isiZulu** home language **14,613,202** (**24.4%** of 59,893,885 who stated a language). This is **language, not ethnicity** — closest official handle. https://census.statssa.gov.za/assets/documents/2022/2022_Census_Main_Release.pdf
6. **Rupture:** 1879 Anglo-Zulu War; 1994 as civic inclusion in RSA, not an ethnogenesis.
7. **Religion:** Christian (Zionist/AIC, mainline) majority mix; ancestral practice not a census religion.
8. **Flattening:** “Zulu = all Black South Africans”; or 2022 language = ethnicity without remainder.
9. **Receipt:** Stats SA Census 2022 Main Release (table language, p. 28 in the PDF as published). History — Laband; Hamilton on Shaka.
10. **Falsifier:** If Census 2022 isiZulu ≠ 14,613,202, cell 5 dies. If Stats SA published ethnicity “Zulu” separate from language, method upgrades.
11. **OPEN:** Ethnicity vs language gap; diaspora (UK, etc.).

## Xhosa

1. **Name:** Self: *amaXhosa*. Language: *isiXhosa*.
2. **Language:** Nguni, Bantu. Click consonants via Khoe/San contact — not “Khoisan people.”
3. **Homeland:** Eastern Cape. Kingdoms: Gcaleka, Rharhabe, etc.
4. **Subdivisions:** Gcaleka, Rharhabe, Thembu, Mpondo, Mfengu — **not identical**; “Xhosa” can flatten Thembu/Mpondo.
5. **Population:** Census **2022**: **isiXhosa 9,786,928 (16.3%)**. Language ≠ ethnicity.
6. **Rupture:** 19th-c. Cape Frontier Wars; 1856–57 cattle-killing (Nongqawuse) as a dated demographic shock (**death/displacement figures contested — range, do not pick**).
7. **Religion:** Christian majority mix; ancestral.
8. **Flattening:** Clicks ⇒ San ethnicity; all Eastern Cape = Xhosa.
9. **Receipt:** Stats SA 2022 (same PDF). History — Peires, *The Dead Will Arise*.
10. **Falsifier:** 2022 isiXhosa count wrong; or Thembu not distinguished in any historical source (they are).
11. **OPEN:** Ethnicity vs language; cattle-killing mortality.

## Amhara

1. **Name:** Self: *Amaräñña* speakers / *Amhara*.
2. **Language:** Amharic (Ethiosemitic, Afroasiatic). Geʿez as liturgical/classical (attested Aksumite inscriptions, 1st millennium CE).
3. **Homeland:** Northern Ethiopian highlands (Gondar, Gojjam, Wollo, Shewa).
4. **Subdivisions:** Regional; Orthodox vs Muslim Amhara (Wollo). Not “all highland Ethiopians” (Tigray, Agaw).
5. **Population:** Ethiopia CSA Census **2007** (last published full census): Amhara **19,867,817 (26.9%)**. **2012/2020s estimates exist; 2024 census not used here. OPEN for post-2007.** Do not use 2020s media “30 million.”
6. **Rupture:** 1974 fall of Haile Selassie; 1991 EPRDF ethnic federalism (Amhara as one *kilil* — an Ethiopian ethnolinguistic regional state; not a Soviet ASSR, though both are territorial nationality units. Soviet *korenizatsiya* / ASSR machinery is the Caucasus–Central Asia analogue, not used here).
7. **Religion:** Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo majority; Muslim Amhara are real.
8. **Flattening:** “Amhara = Abyssinia = Ethiopia”; filing Amhara as Cushitic.
9. **Receipt:** CSA 2007 National Census. History — Tadesse Tamrat; Bahru Zewde.
10. **Falsifier:** If 2007 Amhara ≠ 19.87M; if Amharic were Cushitic.
11. **OPEN:** Post-2007; 2020s conflict displacement.

## Oromo

1. **Name:** Self: *Oromoo*. Exonym: *Galla* (pejorative, historical texts).
2. **Language:** Afaan Oromoo (Cushitic).
3. **Homeland:** Southern and eastern Ethiopian highlands / lowlands; 16th-c. expansions (Oromo migrations) are the documented spread, not “always everywhere in Ethiopia.”
4. **Subdivisions:** Borana, Barentu; Christian, Muslim, Waaqeffannaa.
5. **Population:** CSA **2007**: Oromo **25,488,344 (34.5%)**. Post-2007 **OPEN**.
6. **Rupture:** 16th-c. expansions; 19th-c. Shewan incorporation; 1991 Oromia *kilil*.
7. **Religion:** Mix: Islam (east), Orthodox/Protestant, Waaqeffannaa — **not** a Muslim default.
8. **Flattening:** “Oromo are a recent invasion with no highland history”; or one religion.
9. **Receipt:** CSA 2007. History — Mohammed Hassen, *The Oromo of Ethiopia*.
10. **Falsifier:** 2007 count; Cushitic misfiled as Semitic.
11. **OPEN:** Post-2007; religion shares by zone as census (2007 has some religion tables — not re-pulled this pass).

## Somali

1. **Name:** Self: *Soomaali*.
2. **Language:** Somali (Cushitic). Osmanya/Latin scripts 20th c.; language older.
3. **Homeland:** Horn: Somalia, eastern Ethiopia (Somali Region), Djibouti, northern Kenya.
4. **Subdivisions:** Clan (Darod, Hawiye, Dir, Rahanweyn/Digil-Mirifle, Isaaq) — **primary political units**; “Somali” is the ethnolinguistic umbrella. Do not pretend clan is not load-bearing.
5. **Population:** Somalia has **no complete conventional census** in the 2020s comparable to CSA 2007. UNFPA (United Nations Population Fund) 2014 Population Estimation Survey is a survey, not a full census. Ethiopia 2007 Somali **4,581,794 (6.2%)**. Kenya 2019 census Somali **OPEN** this pass (KNBS published ethnicity). **Do not print 15 million as if counted.**
6. **Rupture:** 1991 state collapse; 1977–78 Ogaden War; colonial partition (British/Italian/French/Ethiopian).
7. **Religion:** Sunni Islam (Shafiʿi, one of the four Sunni legal schools) as near-default — here the default **matches**.
8. **Flattening:** “Somalis are Arabs”; or Somalia’s borders = the people (they don’t).
9. **Receipt:** CSA 2007 (Ethiopia Somali). UNFPA Somalia 2014 PESS (estimate). History — Lewis; Samatar.
10. **Falsifier:** If Somalia published a 2020s full census ethnicity table, cell 5 changes. If Somali were Semitic, cell 2 dies.
11. **OPEN:** Somalia total; Kenya 2019 Somali figure not fetched this pass; Djibouti.

## Maasai

1. **Name:** Self: *il-Maasai* / *Maa*.
2. **Language:** Maa (Eastern Nilotic).
3. **Homeland:** Southern Kenya / northern Tanzania rift-savanna. Nilotic southward movement (dating contested).
4. **Subdivisions:** Purko, Kisongo, etc.; Samburu and Ariaal are related Maa-speakers, not automatically “Maasai” in census.
5. **Population:** Kenya 2019 census ethnicity exists (KNBS); Tanzania 2022 census ethnicity **may** exist — **neither number fetched as a primary table this pass. OPEN.** Do not use tourist “1 million.”
6. **Rupture:** 1880s–90s rinderpest / colonial reserves; 20th-c. park evictions (dated locally, e.g. Serengeti/Ngorongoro — specific decrees **OPEN** to pull).
7. **Religion:** Christian missions + *Enkai* practice. Not “uncontacted.”
8. **Flattening:** Maasai as the image of “all East African pastoralists” (Turkana, Borana, Somali are not Maasai).
9. **Receipt:** History — Spear & Waller, *Being Maasai*. Census — KNBS 2019 (must be opened for the number — **OPEN**).
10. **Falsifier:** KNBS 2019 Maasai count when pulled; Maa as Bantu.
11. **OPEN:** The count; Tanzania.

## San (and why “Khoisan” is a problem)

1. **Name:** Self: many — *ǃKung / Juǀʼhoan*, *Khwe*, *Naro*, *ǂKhomani*, etc. External: “Bushmen” (exonym); “San” (contested).
2. **Language:** **Not one family:** Kx’a (Ju), Tuu (!Xóõ, Nǀuu), Khoe. Clicks are areal, not a genetic proof of one people.
3. **Homeland:** Southern Africa drylands (Botswana, Namibia, South Africa, Angola). Deep time: among the region’s oldest forager populations — genetics is a **separate machine** from census ethnicity; do not substitute.
4. **Subdivisions:** Named language groups above. “Khoisan” mashed Khoe pastoralists (*Khoekhoe*) with San foragers.
5. **Population:** Stats SA 2022 language table does **not** give a San ethnicity. Namibia/Botswana census San/Khoe categories **OPEN** this pass. Advocacy totals **OPEN**.
6. **Rupture:** Colonial genocide in German South West Africa (1904–08 is Herero/Nama-centered — **do not silently fold San into that death toll**); 20th-c. farm labor incorporation; Botswana CKGR evictions (1990s–2000s, court-dated).
7. **Religion:** Not a single church; Christian missions + local practice.
8. **Flattening:** One “Khoisan race”; “extinct”; clicks = one language.
9. **Receipt:** History — Barnard, *Hunters and Herders of Southern Africa*; Suzman. Census — Stats SA 2022 (no San ethnicity line in the language table used above). Namibia NSA / Botswana Stats Botswana: **OPEN**.
10. **Falsifier:** If Ju, Tuu, and Khoe were one language family, cell 2 dies. If Census 2022 listed San ethnicity with a number, cell 5 OPEN dies.
11. **OPEN:** Country headcounts; CKGR remaining residents as of a named year.
