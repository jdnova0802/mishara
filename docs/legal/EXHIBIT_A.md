## 1. What this is called

It is **not** “AI governance,” “cybersecurity,” “media law,” or “having a scary trial team.”

It is the practice of making a **computational decision** (bind, ALLOW / BLOCK / HOLD, control passed, GO / NO-GO) into something that:

1. a **stranger** can check without an account,  
2. a **court** can admit without flying in a witness,  
3. a **carrier / regulator** can treat as the file, not as marketing.

That stack already has names in law. Use them. Do not invent a brand-word for the retainer.

| Layer | Legal name | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| Get it in | **Authentication** — FRE 901; **self-authentication** — FRE 902(11), **902(13), 902(14)** (since 2017) | Hash + qualified-person cert → no live witness |
| Keep it in | **Business records** — FRE 803(6); **best evidence** — FRE 1001–1004 | The log is the writing |
| Don’t lose it | **Spoliation / preservation** | The log dying is the case dying |
| Broker-dealer shape | **SEC Rule 17a-4(f)** audit-trail alternative (2022) | Complete time-stamped trail; recreate original + every rewrite |
| Commercial paper | **UCC Article 12** — control of a controllable electronic record | The *moment* control passed; qualifying purchaser |
| Insurance mouth | **Binder / coverage evidence / surplus lines** | MAY (deck) vs CAN (stranger-checkable instrument) |
| Media ingredient | **C2PA** (content credentials) | Soft-bind only — never promoted to hash proof |

**Internal names we already use, mapped:**

- Stranger-verifiable receipt → the **instrument**  
- Absence proof (no ALLOW / LIVE / GO in `[t0, t1]`) → **negative evidence**  
- Policy transcript → **replayable rule application**  
- Key directory → **who could have signed**  
- Exclusive-door session → **clearance-only; write not executed**  
- Items 112–116 in the Cursor handoff → **FRE 902 packet, responsible identity, ingredient chain, non-redactable verdict, hard vs soft bind**

If a lawyer says “you need a governance program,” they have the wrong subject. If they say “you need a 902(13) qualified person and a preservation hold that actually holds,” they have the right one.

## 3. What “best legal team” means here

Wrong hire: Charles Harder (media/privacy), Wachtell (deals), a First Amendment boutique, an “AI ethics” panel, a cybersecurity firm that writes policies.

Right hire: people who have **gotten a system log into evidence and kept it there**, plus people who know **whether a bind existed**.

Godly for *this* subject is not one letterhead. It is **five seats**, hired in order, mostly project-based until a weld pays.

### Seat 1 — Insurance coverage / surplus lines (first)

**Job:** the commercial mouth. MAY vs CAN. What a dec page, binder, surplus-lines filing, or authority schedule actually is. How an opposing counsel attacks “you never had coverage.”

**Not:** a personal-injury mill. Not an E&O appointment mill unless that is the weld.

**Shape:** insurance regulatory + coverage, not “tech transactions.” Firms of this type: Wiley Rein, Locke Lord, Clyde & Co, a coverage partner at a national firm who has tried *was there a binder* — not a startup generalist.

**When:** now, as a **short paid consult** on how Gate’s public bind/absence receipt should be worded so a coverage lawyer would attach it, not laugh.

### Seat 2 — ESI / FRE 902 (the actual Exhibit A lawyer)

**Job:** turn a receipt into a **902(13)/(14) certification packet**: system description, hash match, qualified-person cert, opposing-party notice flag (902(11) timing). Close the gap already named as item 112. If this seat is empty, “stranger-verifiable” dies the day someone files a motion in limine.

**Not:** an e-discovery vendor that only collects Slack. Not a computer-forensics shop that cannot write a cert a judge will take.

**Shape:** ESI authentication counsel + a **qualified person** (often a forensic examiner, not the trial lawyer) who will sign. The Peck / Facciola school of ESI opinions is the doctrine. Craig Ball–type examiners are the certifiers. The lawyer’s job is the packet and the notice.

**When:** discrete project, after one live receipt exists that is worth authenticating.

### Seat 3 — Preservation / spoliation

**Job:** the log cannot be the thing we lose. Litigation hold that applies to **our** Merkle window, key directory, and transcript store. Redaction rules that match item 115 (verdict and timestamp never redacted; redaction itself is a receipt).

**When:** before the first hostile letter, not after.

### Seat 4 — UCC Article 12 (only if we sell control)

**Job:** opinion-letter ready **control-event** receipts. Gate attests mechanical facts (keys, hashes, time). Counsel/operator attest **good faith** and **notice** — Gate does not mint those. See `docs/handoff/UCC_ARTICLE_12_CONTROL.md`. No reported QP take-free case on CERs as of 1 Nov 2026; this seat is **statute-first**.

**When:** not before a customer will pay for a control event. Do not retain this as a standing army.

### Seat 5 — Trial (rented, late)

**Job:** one lawyer who has survived a fight over **whether the computer’s record is the event**. Motion in limine, 803(6), 902. Plaintiff or defense does not matter; **log-as-fact** does.

**Not:** Quinn-for-the-logo. Not a celebrity defamation lawyer. When the fight is real, *then* rent a trial shop (Quinn, Susman, a coverage-trial name). Until then, seats 1–3.

### Later, only if the customer is the state

Covington / Wilmer for **CFIUS, FedRAMP, government contracts**. That is Bezos/Lockheed’s stack. Do not buy it to feel adjacent to Palantir.

### Never let this seat drive product

A **trade-secret** lawyer whose instinct is to hide the log. Stranger-verifiability and OPSEC are not enemies (band thresholds, prove consistency, don’t publish the exact cut). If counsel wants the receipt private “for protection,” they are arguing Thiel’s side.
