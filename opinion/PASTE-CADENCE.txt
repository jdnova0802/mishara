1. CADENCE — LISTING TO FIRST RETAIN

There is no public median time-to-first-retain for a new, unproven expert.

SEAK 2024 fee survey (n=1,633) does not report time-to-first-case, conversion, or new-vs-experienced split.

SEAK’s own marketing book: “Nobody can tell you with certainty how effective any technique will be.”

What exists, with bias:

SEAK directory ads: “two weeks after listing”; “second person within a week”; “2–3 referrals per month.” Seller of the listing. Survivorship.

SEAK marketing book (2015): refund window was 8 months because lag is “very common.” Anecdote of a refund ask at ~7 months. Anecdote of an email on day 3 asking where the cases are.

Current SEAK first-listing refund: cancel through 1 April 2028. They lengthened the window. That is not a “weeks” product.

r/expertwitness (Nov 2024): one person, 8 months, zero leads, requesting refund. Others: multiple leads, or a case after a SEAK course not the listing alone. Tiny n.

Practice blogs: test a directory 6–12 months before judging ROI.

SEAK 2025 Marketing Survey (n>450) ranks channels. Full tables are paywalled. We did not buy it. We do not know the surveyed hit-rate for “listing only, no prior testimony.”

WORKING ESTIMATE (labeled): plan on months, not weeks.
Non-crazy range: 6–12 months to first paid retain from a cold directory listing.
Fat left tail: never. Thin right tail: weeks (the testimonials).
Attorneys hire when they have a case, not when you list.

Cold letters to Patel / Bredehoft / Eisenhower: not in any table. Warmer than a stranger listing, colder than a referral. Low-probability, high-ticket. Not this month’s payroll.

2. RATE — $650–$900 ASSUMES A TRACK RECORD WE DON’T HAVE

$650 / $800 / $900 is not the market median. It is a top-quartile public card for a payments-operations expert with a C-suite CV.

SEAK 2024 (n=1,633, all specialties, mixed experience):
File review / prep: median $450/hr (avg $466, range $55–$2,500)

Median retainer (of those who take one): $3,000

The $15k–$75k written-opinion SKU sits above the median entire-case billing ($7k).

3. CREDENTIALS

Directories: SEAK and JurisPro require no bar, no CPA, no license. Pay, upload a CV, go live. That is advertising, not qualification.

US federal court (testifying): FRE 702 — knowledge, skill, experience, training, OR education. No bar required as evidence law. Judge is gatekeeper, case by case.

The actual gap is what the opinion is.

The one-pagers are doctrine: what Ocean Frost / Houda / Taikoo / 4A-207 mean.

US federal court: experts generally may not testify on domestic questions of law. The judge instructs on UCC. A testifying expert on “what 4A-207 requires” is the thing courts strike. What gets in: bank-operations facts (did an employee actually read the alert). That is Carrubba’s lane.

Foreign law (Houda, Ocean Frost, Taikoo) in a US court: FRCP 44.1 — court may consider expert testimony. Buyers of that testimony are almost always English solicitors/barristers. We have no data that a US technical founder gets retained for English commercial-court doctrine at $15k–$75k against HFW / 7KBW comps.

England & Wales: CPR 35 expert evidence is not reserved to solicitors, but opining on issues of English law the judge must decide is not the expert’s function. Calling yourself a solicitor/barrister without qualification is a criminal offence.

Written “legal opinions” sold to the public (not through instructing solicitors): UPL risk in the US; “acting as a solicitor” risk in England.

Consulting (non-testifying) to a law firm: no bar required. The lawyer remains counsel. Scope as “here is what the machine did / here are the primary sources,” not “here is how the court should rule.” That is the only SKU that does not need a license.

DEMOND — public check only (LinkedIn + web, 20 Sep 2026). Not a complete private CV.
Public identity: founder / technical (Velaru), Toledo, Ohio. No bar number, law firm, or CPA listing turned up.
If a private CV has an active US bar, English qualification, or long payments-ops employment, that changes Q2–Q3.
On the public record as searched: the gap is open.

FRE 702-shaped piece without a license: technical testimony on rel/ receipts (what the JWT proves). That is Opinion 02’s exhibit half, not “I am an Ocean Frost authority.”

Gap to close for testifying legal expert: bar admission (US) and/or English qualification, OR stop selling legal conclusions.

4. TIME-TO-CASH IF RETAINED THIS MONTH

Do not mix the clocks.

A. Retainer before work starts
SEAK median retainer $3,000 among those who take one. Practice blogs $2,500–$10,000 for review+report.
Cash in days after the attorney signs and wires.
This is the only path that is actually “this month.”
64% of SEAK respondents require a signed retention contract. 65% accept ACH/wire.

B. Invoice after the opinion, net-30
Clock starts at delivery, not at retain. Net-30 is the named standard. Law-firm AP is often net-45/60. We have no survey table that splits expert invoices by net-30 vs net-60 vs “when the client pays the firm.”

C. Tied to case milestones
Months to years. SEAK: 29% of experts reported at least one ultimate non-payment in the last five years (higher on the plaintiff side).

Honest this-month cash: retainer-upfront, ACH, work only after the wire clears.
Even then, getting to “attorney signs this month” is the bottleneck, not AP terms. See Q1.

A $15k–$75k opinion paid after trial is not a Q3 lever.

5. BANDWIDTH

The five-item send list plus a Greenbrier paper is not executable on top of Tuesday and Gate.

Greenbrier CLE paper (14–18 Oct 2026): a NY-CLE-worthy paper is days to a week. 24 calendar days from 20 Sep. Real cannibal of Gate.

Nacha CFP (opens 28 Sep): 3–6 hours. Event is April 2027. Not this-quarter cash even if accepted.

First live retain, if it happens: tens of hours, then you are on the other side’s calendar.

Deprioritize:
1. Do not treat the five-item send list as this week’s job unless Tuesday and Gate are paused.
2. Do the $1k directory listing only if you want 6–12 month option value.
3. Do not write a Greenbrier paper on spec this month.
4. If a named buyer replies and wires a retainer, that file jumps the queue. Until then, this is not H1-weighted.
