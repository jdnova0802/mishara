# Cadence check — expert/consulting lane vs this quarter

Honest answers to the five unknowns. Sources named. “We don’t know” where the data does not exist.

**Bottom line:** treat this as a **6–18 month practice-build**, not a this-quarter cash lever comparable to a live Gate/Tuesday file. Directory listing is a cheap experiment (~$1,100). The five-letter send list plus a Greenbrier CLE paper is a real hour-commitment and **does cannibalize** the other lanes if done properly.

---

## 1. Cadence: listing → first retain

**There is no public median time-to-first-retain for a new, unproven expert.** SEAK’s own marketing book says it outright: “Nobody can tell you with certainty how effective any technique will be” (*How to Market Your Expert Witness Practice*, ch. 2).

What *does* exist:

| Source | What it actually says | Bias |
|---|---|---|
| SEAK directory marketing | Testimonials of “two weeks after listing, first case”; “second person within a week”; “2–3 referrals per month” | Seller of the listing. Survivorship. |
| SEAK marketing book (2015) | Refund window was **8 months** because lag is “very common”; anecdote of an expert requesting a refund at **~7 months** with zero attributed leads; another emailing on **day 3** asking where the cases are | SEAK still selling patience. Useful as *their* implied expected lag. |
| Current SEAK first-listing refund | Cancel anytime through **1 April 2028** | They lengthened the window. That is not a “weeks” product. |
| r/expertwitness (Nov 2024 thread) | One: **8 months, zero leads**, requesting refund. One: multiple leads. One: case almost immediately after a SEAK *course* (not listing alone). One: 1 case in a renewal year | Tiny n, self-selecting. |
| Quora / practice blogs | Test directories **6–12 months** before judging ROI | Practice advice, not a survey table. |
| SEAK 2024 fee survey (n=1,633) | **Does not report** time-to-first-case, conversion rate, or new-vs-experienced split | The large dataset we have is silent on cadence. |

SEAK’s 2025 *Marketing Survey* (n>450) ranks directories vs other channels; the **full tables are paywalled** ($). We did not buy it. We therefore **do not know** the surveyed hit-rate for “listing only, no prior testimony.”

**Working estimate, labeled as such:** for a new expert with no case history, **plan on months, not weeks**. A non-crazy planning range is **6–12 months to first paid retain from a cold directory listing**, with a fat left tail (never) and a thin right tail (weeks — the testimonials). Attorneys hire when *they* have a case, not when you list (SEAK’s own point).

Cold letters to Patel/Bredehoft/Eisenhower are not in the directory data. We **don’t know** conversion on those. They are warmer than a stranger listing (they already live the doctrine) and colder than a referral (they have never seen this face). Treat as **low-probability, high-ticket**, not as this month’s payroll.

## 2. Credibility gap and the $650–$900 rate

**$650 / $800 / $900 is not the market median. It is a top-quartile public card for a *payments operations* expert with a C-suite CV.**

SEAK 2024 fee survey (n=1,633, Jan–Feb 2024) — all specialties, mixed experience:

- File review / prep: **median $450/hr**, average $466, range $55–$2,500
- Deposition: **median $500/hr** (summary page also says $475 in the blog writeup; PDF table says $500 — use the PDF table)
- Trial: **median $500/hr**, average $633
- **Typical total billings on a single case: median $7,000** (average $14,376)
- Most money billed on one case: median $25,000
- Median retainer (of those who take one): **$3,000**

The $15k–$75k *written opinion* SKU sits above the **median entire-case** billing ($7k) and in the neighborhood of the **median “most money ever billed on one case”** ($25k). That is a high-end legal-expert price, not a first-file price.

Comps we cited:

- **David Rich** ($650/$800/$900): former Mastercard EVP / Citi — 25 years in payments *operations*. Not a UCC scholar. The rate is for network/ops/fraud practice knowledge plus testimony history on the JurisPro page.
- **Paul Carrubba**: 45+ years bank operations **and** a banking attorney; “hundreds of cases”; NACHA Payments Institute faculty.
- **Dick Fraher**: former counsel to the Fed’s Retail Payments Office; drafted regs/operating circulars.

ExpertPages’ 2024 survey (cited in their “new expert rate” article): average ~$451/hr across fields; ~80% of experts between $200–$600. Same article, explicitly: *“The fact that Ernie Expert charges $400 an hour doesn’t mean that an expert witness who has never testified before can charge the same rate.”*

SEAK/ExpertPractice blogs tell new experts **not to underprice** because it signals inexperience — that is marketing advice, not evidence that first-timers collect $650.

**Realistic entry (labeled):** if retained at all as a *consulting* expert with no testimony history, **$250–$450/hr** is the band that matches the SEAK median and ExpertPages “new expert” warning. Deposition/trial premiums come after you have survived one. A $15k written opinion is not the median first product; a **$3k–$7k** fixed memo (in the SEAK typical-case and median-retainer neighborhood) is the honest first SKU. $650–$900 is a **target after track record**, not the door.

We **don’t know** the unpublished SEAK specialty medians for Banking (n=19), Financial (n=45), Legal (n=20), Fraud (n=30), Maritime (n=16). Those tables are in the paid 206-page book.

## 3. Licensing / credential — directories vs courts vs this face

**Directories:** neither SEAK nor JurisPro requires a bar card, CPA, or any license. Pay, upload a CV, go live. That is advertising, not qualification.

**US federal court (testifying):** FRE 702 — qualified by knowledge, skill, experience, training, **or** education. No bar required as a matter of evidence law. The judge is the gatekeeper, case by case.

**The actual gap for *this* SKU is not the directory. It is what the opinion is.**

The one-pagers are **doctrine**: what Ocean Frost / Houda / Taikoo / 4A-207 *mean*. In US federal court, **experts generally may not testify on domestic questions of law** (the judge instructs on UCC). See e.g. the standard treatment in “The Impropriety of Expert Witness Testimony on the Law.” *Studco* is Virginia UCC. A testifying expert on “what 4A-207 requires” is the thing courts strike. What *does* get in: bank-operations facts (did an employee actually read the alert; how AML queues work). That is Carrubba’s lane, not a case-law memo.

**Foreign law (Houda, Ocean Frost, Taikoo) in a US court:** FRCP 44.1 — the court *may* consider expert testimony on foreign law. Buyers of that testimony are almost always **English solicitors/barristers** with rights of audience or long commercial-court practices. A US technical founder can be *heard*; we have no data that they get *retained* for English commercial-court doctrine at $15k–$75k against HFW/7KBW comps.

**England & Wales:** CPR 35 expert evidence is not reserved to solicitors, but **opining on issues of English law the judge must decide is not the expert’s function**. Calling yourself a solicitor/barrister without qualification is a criminal offence. Written “legal opinions” sold to the public (not through instructing solicitors) is the UPL / reserved-activity risk in the US and the “acting as a solicitor” risk in England.

**Consulting (non-testifying) to a law firm:** no bar required. The lawyer remains counsel; you are a technical/research consultant. This is the only SKU that does not need a license **if** it is scoped as “here is what the machine did / here are the primary sources we pulled,” not “here is how the court should rule.”

**Does Demond currently qualify?** Public check only (LinkedIn + web; not a complete private CV):

- Public identity: founder/technical (Velaru), Toledo, Ohio. No bar number, law firm, or CPA listing turned up in this search.
- **We do not have a private CV.** If there is an active US bar, English qualification, or a long payments-ops employment history, that changes Q2–Q3. On the public record as searched **20 Sep 2026, there is no license that closes the gap.**
- FRE 702 could still qualify a **technical** expert on `rel/` receipts (what the JWT proves, Shephard/Lizarraga analogue). That is Opinion 02’s *exhibit* half, not “I am an Ocean Frost authority.”

**Gap to close if the lane is testifying legal expert:** bar admission (US) and/or English qualification, **or** stop selling legal conclusions and sell only (a) technical receipt/hash testimony and (b) consulting memos to instructing counsel.

## 4. Time-to-cash if retained this month

Two different clocks. Do not mix them.

**A. If the engagement letter requires a retainer before work starts** (SEAK: median retainer $3,000 among those who take one; practice blogs: $2,500–$10,000 for review+report): **cash in days** after the attorney signs and wires. This is the only path that is actually “this month.” 64% of SEAK respondents require a signed retention contract. 65% accept ACH/wire.

**B. If you invoice after the opinion, net-30:** clock starts at *delivery*, not at retain. Net-30 is the standard the practice blogs name. Law-firm AP is often **net-45/60** in real life. We **don’t have a survey table** that splits expert invoices by net-30 vs net-60 vs “when the client pays the firm.”

**C. If payment is informal / “when the case allows”:** you are in the 29% of SEAK experts who reported **at least one ultimate non-payment in the last five years** (higher on the plaintiff side). Litigation milestones (report → deposition → trial) can be **months to years**. Do not accept this structure for a first file.

**Honest this-month cash path:** retainer-upfront, ACH, work only after the wire clears. Even then, **getting to “attorney signs this month” is the bottleneck**, not AP terms. See Q1.

A $15k–$75k opinion paid after trial is not a Q3 lever.

## 5. Bandwidth — is the five-item send list executable on top of Tuesday and Gate?

**No, not at full quality.** Something has to drop.

| Item | Actual hours if done properly | Conflicts with |
|---|---|---|
| SEAK + JurisPro listing | 2–4 hours (CV, keywords, fee sheet, photo). ~$1,104. Refundable at SEAK through Apr 2028 | Low conflict. Cheap experiment. |
| Two 4A letters + one maritime CLE ask + one IG/eBL letter | 8–15 hours to write *non-embarrassing* letters that survive a later cross on “you marketed yourself.” Plus follow-up. | Tuesday outreach copy, tone, and calendar. Same mouth, different buyer — still the same hours. |
| Greenbrier CLE paper (14–18 Oct 2026) | A NY-CLE-worthy paper is **days to a week**, not an email. 24 calendar days from 20 Sep. | Gate build cycle. This is the real cannibal. |
| Nacha CFP (opens 28 Sep) | 3–6 hours for an abstract. Event is **April 2027**. Not this-quarter cash even if accepted. | Low conflict, long horizon. |
| First live retain, if it happens | A written opinion is **tens of hours**, then you are on the other side’s calendar. | Everything else. |

SEAK’s own rule 6: the biggest expert-practice failure is not carving time for business development. The inverse is also true: **carving that time out of a product cycle with a Tuesday clock is how both lanes get mediocre.**

**Deprioritize to keep this honest:**

1. **Do not** treat the five-item send list as this week’s job unless Tuesday and Gate are explicitly paused.
2. **Do** the $1k directory listing if you want a 6–12 month option value. That is the only piece that does not eat the other lanes.
3. **Do not** write a Greenbrier paper on spec this month. Pitch Eisenhower a *topic* (one paragraph) or wait.
4. If a named buyer replies and wires a retainer, **that** file jumps the queue. Until then, this is not H1-weighted.

## Weighting vs H1

| Claim from the last pack | After this check |
|---|---|
| Fastest real lever this quarter | **Not supported.** Fastest *optional* spend is ~$1k and a CV. Fastest *cash* still requires a stranger to have a live file *and* pick an unproven expert *and* pay a retainer. |
| $650–$900 / $15–75k | **Aspirational**, copied from a Mastercard-EVP public card and from high-end legal opinions. Median first-file economics: ~$450/hr, ~$3k retainer, ~$7k typical case. |
| Directories produce retains in weeks | **Anecdotes exist; the seller’s refund window and the 7-month refund anecdote say months.** No median in the public survey. |
| Demond can be the testifying 4A / Houda expert tomorrow | **Public record: no bar.** Directories will take the listing. Courts and instructing solicitors will ask who you are. Technical receipt testimony is the only FRE 702-shaped piece without a license. |
| Time-to-cash if retained | **Days, if retainer-upfront.** Otherwise 30–90+ days, with a documented non-payment tail. |

**Call:** longer-horizon bet. Keep the one-pagers as inventory. Spend hours on the lane only after (a) a bar/ops CV exists that survives a Patel/Bredehoft Google, or (b) a live file is already asking. Don’t weight it against H1 as if the send list were a closeable pipeline.
